# 🧭 [Lesson 30: Israel in the Promised Land Under Judges and Kings](../README.md)

## 🧩 Introduction

The Israelites had wandered in the wilderness for forty years.

All the generation who refused to believe that the Lord could give them the promised land had died.

This was God's punishment on them because they refused to believe His words.

## 🧩 Joshua Led Israel into Canaan

Just as God said they would, Moses and Aaron also died before Israel entered the promised land.

So God appointed Joshua to be the new leader of Israel.

## 📖 READ – Joshua 1:1,2 ESV

> After the death of Moses the servant of the Lord, the Lord said to Joshua the son of Nun, Moses' assistant, “Moses my servant is dead. Now therefore arise, go over this Jordan, you and all this people, into the land that I am giving to them, to the people of Israel.

The children of those who didn't believe God were now adults.

It was now the Lord's time to take them into Canaan.

🧵Theme - God is faithful; He always does what He says; He never changes.

Joshua led the Israelites into Canaan, and God gave them the land that He had promised to Abraham and his descendants.

- 🔎 Recall:

  _Satan and the king of Egypt had tried to stop the Israelites from leaving Egypt, and the Israelites had doubted God many times, but the Lord still did what He had promised! He made a way for them through the Red Sea. He led them for forty years in the wilderness. The Lord did not fail to do any of the things He had promised them._

## 📖 READ – Joshua 11:23 ESV

> So Joshua took the whole land, according to all that the Lord had spoken to Moses. And Joshua gave it for an inheritance to Israel according to their tribal allotments. And the land had rest from war.
