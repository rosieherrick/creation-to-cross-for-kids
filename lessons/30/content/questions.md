# 🧭 [Lesson 30: Israel in the Promised Land Under Judges and Kings](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. Who was the leader of Israel after Moses died?

   _Joshua (Joshua 1:1-2)_

1. Did God give Abraham's descendants the land which He had promised them?

   _Yes, God kept His promise and gave them the land. (Joshua 11:23)_

1. What did the Israelites do after Joshua died?

   _They turned away from the Lord and worshiped idols, like the people near them who didn't know the true living God and did not have His Word. (Judges 2:11–13)_

1. Who deceives people so they worship idols and other created things?

   _Satan. (1 Peter 5:8)_

1. Why does Satan lead people to worship idols and other things?

   - _Because Satan hates God and doesn't want anyone to worship God. (John 8:44)_

   - _Because Satan hates all people and doesn't want them to trust in God and be delivered from death. (Isaiah 14:13–14)_

1. How did God punish the Israelites for worshiping idols?

   _God allowed their enemies to defeat them. (Judges 2:14, 15)_

1. What did God do when the Israelites admitted they were wrong and asked God to deliver them?

   _God used the Israelite men and women judges to help them fight and overcome their enemies. (Judges 2:16-18)_

1. How was David different from many of the kings who ruled over Israel?

   - _David believed and obeyed God. (1 Samuel 13:14)_

   - _David agreed with God that he was a sinner, and he brought the blood of animals, trusting in God to forgive his sins and accept him. (Acts 13:22)_

1. What important promise did God make to David?

   _God promised David that one of his descendants would be the Savior. (2 Samuel 7:12, 13, 16)_

1. After David had finished his own house, what did he decide to build?

   _David decided to build a temple for the Lord in Jerusalem. (2 Samuel 7:1–3)_

1. Did David build this temple for the Lord?

   _No, his son Solomon built it. (2 Chronicles 5:1)_

1. In what way was the new stone temple like the tabernacle God had the Israelites build in the wilderness?

   - _The temple still had two inner rooms and furniture similar to the tabernacle. (2 Chronicles 5:1; 7:1-3)_

   - _The heavy, large curtain still hung across the entrance to the most holy place as a "DO NOT ENTER" sign for everyone except the high priest. (2 Chronicles 3:14)_

1. When was the high priest allowed to enter this inner room?

   _Only once a year. (Leviticus 16:2, 34)_

1. What did the high priest do in the inner room?

   _The high priest sprinkled the blood of the animals on the mercy seat, the golden lid of the box under the cherubim. (Leviticus 16:14–15)_

1. Did the blood of animals pay for the sin of the Israelites?

   - _No, sin must be paid for by the death of the sinner. (Romans 3:23)_

   - _The blood of animals was only a temporary covering, holding off the punishment until a complete payment for sin would be made. (Hebrews 10:4)_

1. What did God do because the high priest sprinkled the animals' blood on the mercy seat?

   _God forgave the sins of Israel committed during the past year. (Hebrews 9:7)_

   _God held off His punishment until a complete payment for sin would be made. (Romans 3:25)_

1. What happened after King Solomon died?

   _The ten northern tribes split away from the two southern tribes. (1 Kings 11:11–13; 12:19–20)_
