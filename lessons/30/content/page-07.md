# 🧭 [Lesson 30: Israel in the Promised Land Under Judges and Kings](../README.md)

## 🧩 Many Kings Ruled Over Israel after Solomon Died

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

After Solomon's death, the nation of Israel argued over who should be king, and they split into two kingdoms.

The ten northern tribes were called **Israel**, and the two southern tribes were called **Judah**.

## 🧩 Conclusion

We have just covered many years of Israel's history:

- From Joshua to David was about 400 years.

- From David through all the kings was about 400 more years.
