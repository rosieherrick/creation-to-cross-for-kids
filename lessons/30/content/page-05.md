# 🧭 [Lesson 30: Israel in the Promised Land Under Judges and Kings](../README.md)

## 🧩 David Planned to Build the Temple

- 🔎 Background:

  _As king of Israel, David gained a great deal of wealth. He built himself a beautiful palace of wood, stone, gold, and silver. One day, David was thinking about his beautiful palace and realized that the Lord's house was still the same one that had been made in the wilderness from animal skins and curtains of cloth. It was the tabernacle that the Israelites had made for the Lord when they were at Mount Sinai._

  _Therefore, David planned to build for the Lord a permanent and beautiful dwelling place also made of stone, wood, silver, and gold._

David wanted the Lord to have a permanent place where the people could come to worship Him and offer their sacrifices.

🧵Theme - God communicates with man.

The Lord was very pleased with David for wanting to do this.

God sent His prophet Nathan to speak to David.

## 📖 READ – 2 Samuel 7:1-3 ESV

> Now when the king lived in his house and the Lord had given him rest from all his surrounding enemies, the king said to Nathan the prophet, “See now, I dwell in a house of cedar, but the ark of God dwells in a tent.” And Nathan said to the king, “Go, do all that is in your heart, for the Lord is with you.”

🧵Theme - God is faithful; He always does what He says; He never changes.

God gave David the same promise He had given to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

🛟 **Savior** - God said one of David's future family members would be the promised Savior of mankind and conqueror of Satan.

- This great descendant of David would rule as king forever.

- God never forgot His promise to send a Savior.
