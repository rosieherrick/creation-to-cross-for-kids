# 🧭 [Lesson 30: Israel in the Promised Land Under Judges and Kings](../README.md)

## 🧩 Solomon Built the Temple

David prepared the materials for the house of the Lord that was to be built in Jerusalem.

- 🔎 Note:

  _Jerusalem is still a city in the news today. Like most ancient cities, it was surrounded by great stone walls to protect the people from their enemies. Archaeologists have unearthed many ancient sites in the city and many levels of rebuilding of the wall. Visitors to Jerusalem today can see many archaeological sites, some dating back to the times of the kings of Israel._

Before David died, he gave his son Solomon the job of building the house of the Lord.

## 📖 READ – 1 Chronicles 22:5,6 ESV

> For David said, “Solomon my son is young and inexperienced, and the house that is to be built for the Lord must be exceedingly magnificent, of fame and glory throughout all lands. I will therefore make preparation for it.” So David provided materials in great quantity before his death. Then he called for Solomon his son and charged him to build a house for the Lord, the God of Israel.

Solomon became king over all Israel after his father, David, died.

## 📖 READ – 1 Chronicles 29:26-28 ESV

> Thus David the son of Jesse reigned over all Israel. The time that he reigned over Israel was forty years. He reigned seven years in Hebron and thirty-three years in Jerusalem. Then he died at a good age, full of days, riches, and honor. And Solomon his son reigned in his place.

Solomon built the house of the Lord in Jerusalem.

## 📖 READ – 2 Chronicles 2:1; 5:1 ESV

> Now Solomon purposed to build a temple for the name of the Lord, and a royal palace for himself. Thus all the work that Solomon did for the house of the Lord was finished. And Solomon brought in the things that David his father had dedicated, and stored the silver, the gold, and all the vessels in the treasuries of the house of God.

Now, there was no further need for the tabernacle that the Israelites had constructed in the wilderness using animal skins and cloth.

This new house of God was called the temple.

The temple of the Lord had two inner rooms and furniture similar to the tabernacle:

- The thick curtain was hung in front of the most holy place, the inner room where God was to dwell.

- This curtain was there as a "DO NOT ENTER" sign for sinful man.

When the temple was dedicated and sacrifices were offered to the Lord, the Lord came and entered the most holy place of the temple, just as He had entered the tabernacle.

As the Lord came down and entered the temple, the people saw the brilliant light, the Shekinah glory, and worshiped God.

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

🧵Theme - Man can come to God only according to God's will and plan.

🧵Theme - God is holy and righteous. He demands death as the payment for sin.

- 🔎 Recall:

  _The high priest was only allowed to enter this inner room once a year on behalf of the people. The Israelites could not come to God themselves. The only way they could be accepted was to depend on the high priest and the animal blood he offered for them._

  _Every year, the high priest took the blood of animals into the most holy place in the temple and sprinkled the blood on the mercy seat, the pure gold cover of the ark of the covenant._

  _The Israelites were never to forget that they were sinners, that God is perfect, and that the punishment for sin is death. Because the blood of animals could not pay for their sin, the blood had to be placed before God every year._

  _Every year, God forgave their sins and held off His judgment, waiting for the time when a perfect and complete payment for sin would be made._
