# 🧭 [Lesson 30: Israel in the Promised Land Under Judges and Kings](../README.md)

## 🧩 The Times of the Judges

🧵Theme - God is loving, merciful, and gracious.

When Israel repented, that is, agreed with God about their sin and called on the Lord for help, He chose a man or woman to lead and deliver them from their enemies.

These people who were chosen by God to lead Israel were called **judges**.

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

But again and again, the Israelites forgot the Lord.

Therefore, the Lord allowed them to be conquered over and over by their enemies.

## 📖 READ – Judges 2:16-19

> Then the Lord raised up judges, who saved them out of the hand of those who plundered them. Yet they did not listen to their judges, for they whored after other gods and bowed down to them. They soon turned aside from the way in which their fathers had walked, who had obeyed the commandments of the Lord, and they did not do so. Whenever the Lord raised up judges for them, the Lord was with the judge, and he saved them from the hand of their enemies all the days of the judge. For the Lord was moved to pity by their groaning because of those who afflicted and oppressed them. But whenever the judge died, they turned back and were more corrupt than their fathers, going after other gods, serving them and bowing down to them. They did not drop any of their practices or their stubborn ways.

🧵Theme - God is faithful; He always does what He says; He never changes.

Even though the Israelites were disobedient to God and He had to punish them, He still loved them.

🛟 **Savior** is someone who rescues or saves.

- 🔎 Recall:

  _In the garden of Eden, the Lord had promised that He would send a Savior who would destroy Satan and save the world from Satan's power. God also gave this promise of a Savior to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. The Lord promised them that the Savior would be one of their descendants. God protected Israel, the descendants of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, for it was through Israel that God planned to fulfill all His promises of the Savior, a Savior for the whole world._

God wanted the world to know about Him.

We can only come to know God through the Bible.

It was through Israel that God gave much of the Bible.

- God used Jewish men to record His message in the Bible.

- 🔎 Consider:

  _God preserved Israel so that His Word and the story of the Savior could be taught to you and me._
