## 🧭 Lesson 30: Israel in the Promised Land Under Judges and Kings

### 📖 Scripture – Joshua 1:1,2; 11:23; Judges 2:7-19; 2 Samuel 5:4; 7:1-3,12-17; 1 Chronicles 22:5,6; 29:26-28; 2 Chronicles 2:1; 5:1

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** Leviticus 16:2, 3, 34; Numbers 33:51–53, 55, 56; Deuteronomy 4:25, 26; 8:19; 30:15–20; Joshua 23:9–13, 16; 24:19, 20; 1 Samuel 8:4–7; 1 Kings 11:9–13, 31–37; 2 Chronicles 7:1–3; Psalm 106:34–45; Isaiah 41:8–10; 44:1–8; Romans 3:1, 2; Hebrews 10:4

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show the terrible sin of the Israelites and their godless neighbors.
- To show God's faithfulness to keep His promises to undeserving people.
- To introduce David as part of the line of the Savior.
- To show God's desire for all to be saved.

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To see the historical progression of the Old Testament.
- To see God's desire for them to be saved.

### 🧭 Overview

This lesson covers the period from Joshua through the judges and the kings of Israel, offering only a brief overview that highlights David, his plan for the temple, and the temple's completion under Solomon.

Throughout, the emphasis remains on God's judgment, mercy, grace, and faithfulness to His people Israel, as well as His promise to send a Savior for the whole world.

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – Psalm 95:3 NIV

> For the Lord is the great God, the great King above all gods.

---

### 💭 Perspective for the Teacher

Many stories in the Old Testament are not mentioned in these lessons. Instead, we present the basic structure upon which the gospel can be built. Some have said there is a “scarlet thread” running through the Bible. Along this thread is the story of the eternal Lord and Savior, who promised to make a way for fallen humanity to be saved from sin, Satan, and eternal punishment.

The goal of these lessons is to lead the student to a saving knowledge of Jesus Christ by building a foundation that leads them to an understanding of their need for salvation based on a true knowledge of God. We are not doing an exhaustive study of the Bible. When that new birth takes place, the Holy Spirit places a hunger in that new heart to know more of God's Word. We hope the student will eventually want to explore for themselves the riches of the whole Bible.

We need to pray that we are clearly communicating the Old Testament foundations, and that each child will have a heart prepared to receive the gospel when it is presented to them from God's Word.

### 🗣️ On Teaching This Lesson

You are carefully laying a scriptural foundation on which the Gospel will later be presented. Each lesson builds on previous lessons. So be sure to cover each point carefully. DON'T COMPLICATE THE MESSAGE! Follow the outline carefully and emphasize the doctrinal 🧵Theme given.
