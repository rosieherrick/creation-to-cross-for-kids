# 🧭 [Lesson 20: God Kept His Promise to Abraham Through Isaac and Jacob](../README.md)

## 🧩 God Passed to Jacob the Promises Given to Abraham and Isaac

🧵Theme - God is faithful; He always does what He says; He never changes.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 28:13-15 NIV

> There above it stood the Lord, and he said: “I am the Lord, the God of your father Abraham and the God of Isaac. I will give you and your descendants the land on which you are lying. Your descendants will be like the dust of the earth, and you will spread out to the west and to the east, to the north and to the south. All peoples on earth will be blessed through you and your offspring. I am with you and will watch over you wherever you go, and I will bring you back to this land. I will not leave you until I have done what I have promised you.”

God was continually to work out His plan to send the Savior.

- Many years had passed since God gave the first promise of the Savior in the garden of Eden.
- God had called Abraham and promised that the Savior would come in his family line.
- Now Abraham had gotten old and had died.
- But God had not forgotten His promise.

God promised Jacob, Abraham's grandson, that the coming Savior would be in his family line.

Jacob knew for certain that the promises given to his grandfather, Abraham, and his father, Isaac, now belonged to him.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 29:1 NIV

> Then Jacob continued on his journey and came to the land of the eastern peoples.

Many years later, Jacob returned to Canaan.

In all, Jacob had twelve sons.

Look at what God had already done to multiply Abraham' family
!

Now each of these treat-grandsons of Abraham was to become a creat tribe of people.

We still refer to the names of thes men as the "twelve tribes of Israel".

Israel is the name that God gave to their father, Jacob.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 32:28 EASY

> Then the man said, ‘Your name will not be Jacob any longer. Your name will now be Israel.That is because you have fought with God and you have fought with men. And you have won!'

Jacob, the grandson of Abraham was now Israel.

Even today, we call the nation of Israel by this man's name.
God was already fulfilling His promises to Abrahham to make him a creat nation!
