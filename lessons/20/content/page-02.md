# 🧭 [Lesson 20: God Kept His Promise to Abraham Through Isaac and Jacob](../README.md)

## 🧩 Esau Didn't Believe or Value God's Promises

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner; he needs God and is helpless to save himself.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 25:27a NIV

> The boys grew up, and Esau became a skillful hunter, a man of the open country.

Esau was a skillful hunter; he spent his time tracking and killing wild animals out in the fields.

The promises concerning the Savior would have ordinarily been passed on to Esau because he was Isaac's firstborn son.

The Savior from God would then have been one of Esau's family.

But Esau was not interested in the promises of God.

- He did not trust in God like Abraham and Isaac did.
- Esau was like Cain.

  He did not see that he was a sinner or that he needed to be accepted by God.

- Esau went his own way and lived only for the things of this world.
- These were more important to him than the things which God wanted to give and teach him.
