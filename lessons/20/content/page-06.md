# 🧭 [Lesson 20: God Kept His Promise to Abraham Through Isaac and Jacob](../README.md)

## 🧩 Conclusion

God didn't forget His promises to send a Savior, the promise made to Adam and Eve, Abraham, and Isaac. God had a plan and was working to keep His promise, even though many years had passed since He first made the promise.

Jacob was a sinner, and he needed God's mercy just as we do. In his dream, God showed Jacob that the Savior would one day come and make a way for people to be brought back to God.

We can see that God is keeping and will continue to keep His promises. He is faithful and will always do what He says. We can count always count on Him!
