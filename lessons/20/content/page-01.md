# 🧭 [Lesson 20: God Kept His Promise to Abraham Through Isaac and Jacob](../README.md)

## 🧩 Introduction

Today we are going to look at some of the things that happened in the lives of Abraham's family, as recorded in the first book of the Bible, Genesis.

God kept careful record of these events that took place nearly 4,000 years ago!

Our story begins in Canaan, but it will close with Abraham's family living in Egypt.

## 🧩 Isaac Married Rebekah

## 📖 READ – Genesis 25:19-20 EASY

> This is the story of Isaac. Abraham had a son named Isaac. 20 When Isaac was 40 years old, he married Rebekah...

Rebekah, Isaac's wife, was born in the land where Abraham had lived before he came to Canaan.

Remember, God had spared Isaac's life by providing a ram to die in Isaac's place!

God had promised Abraham that through Isaac would come **many** family members, including the Savior.

## 🧩 Esau and Jacob Were Born

## 📖 READ – Genesis 25:21 EASY

> Isaac's wife could not have children. So Isaac prayed to the Lord for her. The Lord heard Isaac's prayer, and he allowed Rebekah to become pregnant. When the right time came, Rebekah gave birth to twins.

God made it possible for Isaac and Rebekah to have children, just as He had for Abraham and Sarah.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 25:24-26 EASY

> The first baby was red. His skin was like a hairy robe. So he was named Esau. When the second baby was born, he was holding tightly to Esau's heel. So that baby was named Jacob. Isaac was 60 years old when Jacob and Esau were born.

Now Abraham's son Isaac had twins; and God said that from each of them would come a great nation of people.
