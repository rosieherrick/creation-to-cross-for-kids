# 🧭 [Lesson 20: God Kept His Promise to Abraham Through Isaac and Jacob](../README.md)

## 🧩 Jacob Cherished and Believed God's Promises

🧵Theme - Man must have faith in order to please God and be saved.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 25:27b NIV

> Jacob was content to stay at home among the tents.

Jacob lived quietly in his tent and kept sheep and cattle.

In contrast to Esau, Jacob was a believing man like Abraham and Isaac.

- Jacob admitted that he was a sinner and needed God to send the Savior.
- He was very interested in God's promises.

## 🧩 Jacob Returned to the Land of Abraham and Rebekah

Because of the great differences between Esau and Jacob, problems between the two brothers increased to the point that Esau threatened to kill Jacob.

Because of this, Jacob left his father and mother's home and began the long journey back to Mesopotamia, the land from which his grandfather, Abraham, had come.
