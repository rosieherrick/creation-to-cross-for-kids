# 🧭 [Lesson 20: God Kept His Promise to Abraham Through Isaac and Jacob](../README.md)

## 🧩 Jacob's Dream

🧵Theme - God communicates with man.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 28:10-11 NIV

> When he reached a certain place, he stopped for the night because the sun had set. Taking one of the stones there, he put it under his head and lay down to sleep.

It was a long way to travel from Canaan to Mesopotamia so, on his way, Jacob had to sleep out in the mountains.

One night as Jacob slept, God gave him a dream.

- Occasionally during those times, God would speak to people through dreams.
- But now that His Word is completed, He speaks to us through the Bible.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 28:12 NIV

> He had a dream in which he saw a stairway resting on the earth, with its top reaching to heaven, and the angels of God were ascending and descending on it.

Through this dream, God was showing Jacob something about the promised Savior.

🛟 The Savior would bridge the gap between man and God.

- God is the only One who can make a way for us to come to Him.
- Even if a person were to do many, many good deeds to try to please God, all his work would still not even come close to bringing us any closer to God.

🔎 Compare:

_This reminds us that, in the beginning, Adam and Eve were in oneness with God. God walked with Adam and Eve. They were friends with God. But when they disobeyed God, they and all their family, including you and me, were separated from God. The way to God had been removed. There was no way that any person could come back to God and be in fellowship with Him unless God Himself made a way._

_But God promised the Savior who would destroy Satan and restore the friendship between God and us. The Savior would be like the stairway which Jacob saw in his dream, reaching from earth to Heaven. Through the Savior, people would once again be able to be with God._

🧵Theme - God is loving, merciful, and gracious.

Jacob was a sinner, just like us.

- God graciously showed Jacob that there is only one way to God.
- God was also showing Jacob that he needed to trust God.
- God wants Jacob, and us, to see that there is nothing we can do to make us acceptable to God.
