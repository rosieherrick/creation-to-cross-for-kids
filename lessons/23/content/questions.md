# 🧭 [Lesson 23: God Sent Plagues on Egypt; God Passed Over Israel](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. How did God plan to use the wicked Pharaoh?

   _God planned to use this proud king to show everyone that the God of Israel is the only true and living God and that He is almighty. (Romans 9:17)_

1. How did the Lord show His power?

   _The Lord sent great and terrible plagues on the Egyptians. (Exodus 7:5; 9:14-16; 14:4)_

1. Why didn't these plagues also come on the Israelites?

   - _🎁 Because of the Lord's love and grace. (Deuteronomy 7:7-8)_
   - _🛟 Because the Lord had promised Abraham that He would make Abraham's descendants into a great nation and that, through them, the Savior would come into the world. (Deuteronomy 7:7-8)_
   - _👀 So that the Egyptians would realize that the God of Israel was the only true and living God in the whole earth. (Exodus 10:1-2)_

1. Did the Israelites deserve to be protected like this?

   _No, they, too, were sinners and deserved God's judgement. (Deuteronomy 9:4-6)_

1. What was the last plague which God sent on the Egyptians?

   _God sent a plague which killed the firstborn in every Egyptian family and the firstborn of all their livestock. (Exodus 11:4-5; 12:12-13)_

1. What did God tell the Israelites to do so their firstborn children would not die?

   - _🐑 They must choose a lamb without blemish. (Exodus 12:5)_
   - _🥣 They must kill the lamb and catch its blood in a basin. (Exodus 12:22)_
   - _🚪 They must put the blood on the doorposts and above the door. (Exodus 12:7)_
   - _🩸 They must stay inside the house where the blood was placed. (Exodus 12:22-23)_
   - _🦴 They must not break any of the lambs bones. (Exodus 12:46)_

1. What would have happened if the Israelites had not done everything exactly as God instructed Moses?

   _Their firstborn children would also have died. (Exodus 12:13, 22-23)_

1. Who is the only One who can tell us how we can come to God and be accepted by Him?

   _God. (Proverbs 3:5-6)_

1. What happened in the Egyptians homes on the night when God said that He would kill the firstborn of every family?

   _God killed them, just as He said He would. (Exodus 12:29-30)_

1. What was God's promise to the Israelites?

   _🩸 God promised them that when He saw the blood on the doorposts and over their doors, He would pass over them. (Exodus 12:13, 23)_

1. What happened to the Israelites?

   _All of their children were safe. None of them died. (Exodus 12:27-28)_

1. What did Pharaoh, the king, tell Moses after the Egyptians' firstborn children died?

   _Pharaoh told Moses to take all the Israelites out of Egypt. (Exodus 12:31-32)_
