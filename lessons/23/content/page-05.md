# 🧭 [Lesson 23: God Sent Plagues on Egypt; God Passed Over Israel](../README.md)

## 🧩 The Lord's Instructions to Moses

🧵Theme - God is communicates with man.

God told Moses how the Israelites must prepare for the final and most terrible plague of all.

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

The Israelites were also sinners and deserved to die for their sins.

🧵Theme - God is loving, merciful, and gracious.

They also would have suffered through this last terrible judgement if it had not been for Lord's mercy and grace.

🧵Theme - God is faithful; He always does what He says: He never changes.

God rememered His promises to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

- God had promised to take Abraham's descentants back to the land which God had given to him.
- It was now God's chosen time to do this.

This is what the Lord told Moses that the Israelites must do in order to save their firstborn children from death.
