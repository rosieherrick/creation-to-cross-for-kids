# 🧭 [Lesson 3: God Created the Angels](../README.md)

## 🧩 Angels Created Perfect by God

🧵Theme - God is holy.

God created all the angels perfect; not one of them was evil or unkind.

- Everything God does is perfect, because God is holy.

|        Learning About God         |
| :-------------------------------: |
|    📖 God wants us to know Him    |
| 🪐 God is everywhere all the time |
|      💡 God knows everything      |
|    👑 God is greater than all     |
| 💯 **God is holy, 100% perfect!** |

- God is holy – everything He thinks, says and does is **perfect**.
- He never has and never will sin.

## 🧩 Angels Created with Great Wisdom and Strength

God created the spirits with great wisdom and strength.

## 📖 READ – Psalm 103:20 ESV

_Bless the LORD, O you his angels, you mighty ones who do his word, obeying the voice of his word!_

🧵Theme - God is greater than all and more important than all. He is the highest in authority.

- Angels are stronger than we are, and the Bible tells us that God has given them the ability to do amazing things; but they are not all-powerful like God.
- Angels are very smart, too, but they are not all-knowing like God is.

God is wiser and stronger than all the angels.

- This truth is **very** important to remember.
- The Bible tells us the truth that **God is greater than all!**
