# 🧭 [Lesson 3: God Created the Angels](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. Who is the only One who lived before the beginning?

   _God. (Genesis 1:1)_

1. Where did angels come from?

   _They were created by God. (John 1:3)_

1. Did God create the angels with bodies of flesh and bones like us?

   _No._

1. Did God create good and bad angels?

   _No, He made them all good. (1 Timothy 4:4)_

1. Why did God create angels?

   _To be His servants. (Psalm 103:20-21)_

1. When they were first created, where did all the angels live?

   _With God in Heaven. (Job 38:4–7)_

1. Who was the smartest and most beautiful angel God made?

   _Lucifer. (Ezekiel 28:12)_

1. What special job did Lucifer get to have?

   _He was made leader of all the other angels. (Ezekiel 28:14)_

1. What did Lucifer plan to do?

   _He planned to be like God and take God's position. (Isaiah 14:13,14)_

1. Who else followed Lucifer in his rebellion against God?

   _Many of God's angels. (Revelation 12:9)_

1. Did God know what Lucifer and the rebellious angels were planning?

   _Yes. (Isaiah 14:13)_

1. What did God do?

   _He removed Lucifer from his position and kicked Lucifer and the rebellious angels out of Heaven. (Isaiah 14:12-15, Revelation 12:7-9)_

1. Is there anything that God doesn't see, hear, and know?

   _👀 No! God sees, hears, and knows everything. (Proverbs 15:3)_

1. What place did God prepare as a punishment for Lucifer and the rebellious angels?

   _The Lake of Fire. (Matthew 25:41)_

1. What new name did Lucifer get that means "enemy"?

   _Satan. (1 Peter 5:8)_

1. Who is Satan's enemy? Who is he against?

   _God. (John 8:44)_
