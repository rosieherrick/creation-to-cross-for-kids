# 🧭 [Lesson 3: God Created the Angels](../README.md)

## 🧩 Lucifer's rebellion

Because Lucifer was created by God and given the highest position over all the other angels, he should have loved, obeyed, and served God.

But after a time, Lucifer became very proud of his beauty, intelligence, and position.

## 📖 READ – Isaiah 14:13, 14 NIV

> You said in your heart, 'I will ascend to the heavens; I will raise my throne above the stars of God; I will sit enthroned on the mount of assembly, on the utmost heights; I will ascend above the tops of the clouds; I will make myself like the Most High.'

Lucifer wanted to be like _"the most High."_

- He decided that he wanted to take over God's position as the ruler of all things – Lucifer rebelled against God's authority over him.
- Do you know what "rebel" means?

  **TO REBEL means to think or act against the one in authority – TO DISOBEY**

- To rebel or disobey is very serious.
- Lucifer rebelled against God.
- Lucifer was the first one to do evil. (Evil is anything that is different from what God wants or agrees with.)

You may ask, "If Lucifer was created perfect, how could he disobey God?"

- In the Bible we see many examples of the fact that God allows **choices** to be made.

- Looking again at Isaiah 14:13,14 we see Lucifer choosing what he wanted... _"I **will** ascend”, “I **will** exalt”, “I **will** sit”, “I **will** ascend”, “I **will** make myself like the most High”,_

- Lucifer **made a choice** in his mind to rebel against God.

- God could have made the angels like robots, programmed to only do what God told them to do.

- He didn't. He gave the angels a choice.

🔎 Consider:

_What if someone said they wanted to be your friend – they were willing to do all the things you like to do and they were very nice to you. It would be great to have a friend like that, wouldn't it? It's wonderful to have someone **choose** to be your friend._

_But what if you found out that the person really had no choice about being nice to you – they were **forced** to act that way. That wouldn't be a friendship at all, would it!_

Choice is important to us, and it is important to God too. He doesn't program His created beings to be like robots. He lets them make choices – to obey or to disobey.
