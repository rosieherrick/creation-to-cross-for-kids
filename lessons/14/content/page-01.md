# 🧭 [Lesson 14: God Judged the World and Saved Noah](../README.md)

## 🧩 Introduction

In our last lesson we saw that God had made a way for man to come to Him by faith.

We saw the terrible results of sin passed down from Adam and Eve to their children, all the way to us.

Now we will see what happened after that, from Adam to Noah.

## 🧩 From Adam to Noah

From Adam to Noah there were ten generations of believers, in the family line of the future Savior God was going to send. That's ten generations of men who believed God and continued to look for the coming Savior promised to Adam and Eve back in the garden.

- The next events happened about 1,500 years after God created Adam.
- By the time of Noah and his three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth, there was a lot more people living on the earth.

The population had grown but most people were only interested in having a good time, and didn't care about God.

God saw and hated the sin of the people, but He also loved them and wanted them to change their minds and believe in Him.

## 🧩 Increased Wickedness

## 📖 READ – Genesis 6:5, 11 ESV

> The Lord saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every intention of the thoughts of his heart was **only evil continually**. Now the earth was corrupt in God's sight, and the earth was filled with violence.

These People:

- Were **born sinners** because they were in Adam's family line.
- **Loved** their **sin** and deliberately refused to agree with God.
- **Refused to acknowledge God** even though they knew He existed.

  🔎 Consider:

  _The people of Noah's day were a lot like the people in our day. We too were born sinners and in Adam's family line. Sometimes I know I have loved my sin, choosing to do what I want, instead of what I know God wants. There are more and more people today who refuse to acknowledge God, saying they don't believe that God exists._

The people were **only thinking about themselves**…

- they were proud, self-centered, and boastful,
- wanting what other people had,
- arguing and fighting all the time,
- being cruel and even killing each other,
- constantly tricking, lying, and deceiving one another,
- gossiping and saying evil things about other behind their backs
- and ignoring God.
