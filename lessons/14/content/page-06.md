# 🧭 [Lesson 14: God Judged the World and Saved Noah](../README.md)

## 🧩 Conclusion

God is patient – He waited for 120 years for the people to repent and turn away from their evil ways. They had no excuse for their sin.

They simply refused to listen to God, though He warned them again and again through Noah.

Then, in a moment, in the midst of their sin, they were swept away.

Every man, woman, and child outside the ark died in the flood.

But Noah and his family believed God, and He kept them safe inside the ark.
