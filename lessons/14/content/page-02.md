# 🧭 [Lesson 14: God Judged the World and Saved Noah](../README.md)

## 🧩 God Saw the Unbelieving World and Its Sin

## 📖 READ – Genesis 6:5, 12 ESV

> **The Lord saw that the wickedness of man** was great in the earth, and that every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually. And **God saw the earth**, and behold, it was corrupt, for all flesh had corrupted their way on the earth.

God saw their sin.

- They may have hidden their lies and stealing from others but nothing is hidden from God, He saw it all.
- God sees our sin too. No one can hide from God, He even sees our **thoughts**, the things we think and don't tell anyone.

## 🧩 God Decided to Destroy the World

🧵Theme - God is holy and righteous. He demands death as the payment for sin.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 6:6-7 ESV

> And the Lord **regretted that he had made man on the earth**, and it **grieved him to his heart**. So the Lord said, ‘I will blot out man whom I have created from the face of the land... for I am sorry that I have made them.'

The people were so wicked that the Lord said He would destroy them and everything on the earth that He had made.

## 🧩 God Was Gracious to Noah

🧵Theme - God is loving, merciful, and gracious.

🧵Theme - Man can come to God only according to God's way.

Because of God's love and mercy, one man and his family were **not** going to be destroyed.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 6:8-10 ESV

> But Noah found favor in the eyes of the Lord. These are the generations of Noah. Noah was a righteous man, blameless in his generation. Noah walked with God.

Noah was born a sinner under Satan's control just like all of Adam's family line.

- Noah didn't deserve to be saved by God.
- He, too, deserved to be punished.

Noah came to God like Abel, Seth, and Enoch, bringing the blood of animals.

- Noah listened to God's words to him.
- Noah trusted God to save him through the coming Saviour.

Because of God's grace, God forgave and accepted Noah.

🎁 **Grace** is getting something we don't deserve.
