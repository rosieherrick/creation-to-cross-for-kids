# 🧭 [Lesson 14: God Judged the World and Saved Noah](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. What was the attitude of the people in Noah's day?

   _They were selfish, always fighting, and lying, and hurting others. They were not interested in knowing or pleasing God. (Genesis 6:5,11)_

1. What did God say He was going to do because the people wouldn't repent?

   _God said He would destroy all living things on the earth. (Genesis 6:6,7)_

1. Did Noah deserve to be saved from God's punishment?

   _No, He was a sinner and deserved to be punished with the other people. (Romans 3:23)_

1. Why did God save Noah?

   _🙏 Because Noah agreed with God that he was a sinner and trusted God to save him through the coming Savior God promised to send. (Genesis 6:7,8)_

1. What did God tell Noah to do?

   _God told him to build an ark. (Genesis 6:14)_

1. Did God allow Noah to build the ark however he wanted to?

   _No, it had to be exactly as God commanded. (Genesis 6:14-16)_

1. What one important thing should be remember about the ark?

   _🚪 There was only one door. There was only one way to enter the ark and be saved from judgment. (Genesis 6:16)_

1. What else did Noah do while he was building the ark?

   _He was telling the people God's message and warning them that God was going to make it rain and the whole earth would be destroyed by a flood. (1 Peter 3:20)_

1. Did the people accept what Noah said and change their minds?

   _No, they refused to believe. (2 Peter 2:5)_

1. How long did God wait for the people in Noah's time to agree with Him?

   _120 years. (Genesis 6:3)_

1. How did those saved from judgment get into the ark?

   _🚪 They all entered by the one door God told Noah to put on the ark. (Genesis 7:16)_

1. Why did God shut the door?

   _To keep all those inside the ark safe from the flood. (Genesis 7:16,23)_

1. Did anyone outside the ark escape death?

   _No, they all died. (Genesis 7:21-23)_
