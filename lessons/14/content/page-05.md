# 🧭 [Lesson 14: God Judged the World and Saved Noah](../README.md)

## 🧩 God Shut Them In

🧵Theme - God is greater than all and more powerful than all, He is the highest authority.

After they were all inside. God shut the door.

- 🔎 Consider:

  _“...the LORD shut him in.”_ Sometimes we read right past verses in the Bible that tell us wonderful things without even realizing it. Look what God has said! God Himself shut Noah, his family, and all the animals, inside the place of safety.

God wasn't going to allow more time for the other people to change their minds and believe.

- When God shut the door, it was too late.

  Even if they cried or pleaded outside the door, they could not enter the ark.

  Noah could not let them in because God had shut them out. They had no way to be saved.

- Those inside the boat were now safe because God had shut them in.
- Those outside of the boat now had no way to escape God's anger because God had shut them out.

## 🧩 God Destroyed All Outside the Ark

🧵Theme - God is holy and righteous. He demands death as the payment for sin.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 7:17, 23 ESV

> The flood continued forty days on the earth. The waters increased and bore up the ark, and it rose high above the earth. He blotted out every living thing...**Only Noah was left, and those who were with him in the ark.**

After God shut the door of the ark, He sent the rain.

🧵Theme - God is all-powerful.

🧵Theme - God is greater than all and more important than all, He is the highest authority.

- God has control over all the earth, the rain, the wind, the sun, the moon, stars, and everything else.
- He made all these things, and He controls them all.

Not only did the rain come pouring down from the sky but the water came up from under the ground too.

- The Bible says that _“...all the fountains of the great deep burst forth...”_ (v.11)

God can do anything, nothing is impossible to Him.

God made it rain for forty days and nights until the whole earth was covered with water.

There was so much water that it covered the very highest mountains in water.
