# 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 [Creation to the Cross - for Kids](../../README.md)

## 🧭 Lesson 14: God Judged the World and Saved Noah

### 📖 Scripture – Genesis 6:3, 5-22; 7:1-5, 15-17, 23

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** Psalms 104:6–9; Ezekiel 18:32; Matthew 24:37–39; Romans 1:18–32; Hebrews 4:13; 11:7; 1 Peter 3:20; 2 Peter 2:5; 3:9; Jude 14–16

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show what God does what He says and will punish sin.
- To show that God saves those who believe in Him and come to Him in His way.
- To show that there is only one way to come to God.
- To show that God keeps His promises, He is faithful.

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To fear God.
- To see the deceitfulness of sin and the danger of going away from God.
- To see the necessity of obedience to God.

### 🧭 Overview

This lesson covers the period from Cain and Abel to Noah and the flood. It highlights God's omniscience and wrath against sin, alongside His grace and patience in calling people to repentance. God's sovereignty and omnipotence are displayed in His control over all creation.

In God's instructions to Noah about building the ark, we see His precise communication and the truth that there is only one way of salvation. The ark serves as a picture of Christ, a theme you will develop further when teaching the New Testament.

The moral decay of humanity in Noah's day is compared with the decadence of our own society, with a warning against hard-heartedness. God's deliverance of Noah and his family is presented as an act of grace, God's kindness to undeserving sinners. The flood is treated as historical fact, an exercise of God's sovereign power in judging unrepentant sinners.

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – Acts 16:31 ESV

> Believe on the Lord Jesus, and you will be saved.

### 💭 Perspective for the Teacher

The story of Noah is familiar to most people, but it is often viewed as a fairy tale.

These passages from Genesis are for us today so that we may know God's character: His hatred of sin, His patience with men and His desire for all to be saved, His swift and sure judgment, and His gracious care and salvation for all who come to Him by faith. How important it is for us to teach the Bible as God's truth!

Note: Virtually every ancient culture has a flood story. This is because there really was a world-wide flood. Only the Bible has the true story, given to us by God. [Answers In Genesis](https://www.answersingenesis.org) provides more information for further study with an article about [The Flood](https://answersingenesis.org/the-flood/) and [Noah's Ark](https://answersingenesis.org/noahs-ark/).

### 🗣️ On Teaching This Lesson

You are carefully laying a scriptural foundation on which the Gospel will later be presented. Each lesson builds on previous lessons. So be sure to cover each point carefully. DON'T COMPLICATE THE MESSAGE! Carefully follow the outline, emphasizing the doctrinal 🧵Theme given.
