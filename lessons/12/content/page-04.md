# 🧭 [Lesson 12: The Consequences of Sin and the Birth of Cain and Abel](../README.md)

## 🧩 Cherubim and Flaming Sword Blocking the way to the Tree of Life

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 3:24

> "He drove out the man, and at the east of the garden of Eden he placed the cherubim and a flaming sword that turned every way to guard the way to the tree of life."

God put some of His good angels, called cherubim, and a special _“flaming sword”_ that would turn every way at the edge of the garden to keep Adam and Eve away.

- There was absolutely no way they could get back to the tree of life and now they would grow old and die.

## 🧩 God Is the Giver of Life

🧵Theme - God is greater than all and more important than all; He is the highest authority.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 4:1 ESV

> "Now Adam knew Eve his wife, and she conceived and bore Cain, saying, 'I have gotten a man with the help of the LORD.'"

Eve knew that God is the giver of all life.

God had been the one to give them a son.
