# 🧭 [Lesson 12: The Consequences of Sin and the Birth of Cain and Abel](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. Why did God refuse the clothes which Adam and Eve made?

   _Because God wanted to show them that they can't do anything themselves to fix things and make themselves acceptable to Him. (Ephesians 2:8-9)_

1. Is there anything that anyone can do themselves to be acceptable to God?

   _No. (Romans 9:16)_

1. What are some of the things people might do to try to make themselves acceptable to God? Will God accept any of these?

   _No. (Isaiah 64:6)_

1. Who is the only one that can make Adam and Eve acceptable to God?

   _God. (Romans 8:3)_

1. Why did God kill animals to make clothing for Adam and Eve?

   _🩸 Because God was reminding Adam and Eve that the punishment for sin is death. (Romans 6:23)_

1. Why did God put them outside the garden of Eden?

   _So that they didn't eat from the tree of life and then live forever. (Genesis 2:22-23)_

1. What did God put at the edge of the garden to keep Adam and Eve out?

   _He put angels called cherubim and a sword of fire that flashed every direction. (Genesis 3:24)_

1. Where were Cain and Abel born?

   _Outside the garden. (Genesis 3:34-4:2)_

1. Why were Cain and Abel born separated from God?

   _Because Adam is their father. (Romans 5:12)_

1. Why are we born separated from God?

   _Because Adam is our father. (Psalm 51:5)_
