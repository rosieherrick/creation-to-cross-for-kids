# 🧭 [Lesson 12: The Consequences of Sin and the Birth of Cain and Abel](../README.md)

## 🧩 God Put Adam and Eve Out of the Garden, Away from the Tree of Life

🧵Theme - God is holy and righteous. He demands death as the payment for sin.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 3:22-23 ESV

> Then the LORD God said, 'Behold, the man has become like one of us in **knowing good and evil**. Now, lest he reach out his hand and take also of the tree of life and eat, and live forever—' **therefore the LORD God sent him out from the garden of Eden** to work the ground from which he was taken.

God the Father, God the Son, and God the Holy Spirit said this.

They were talking about Adam and Eve.

- When God first made Adam and Eve they were perfect and knew nothing about evil or bad things. Remember, everything God made was **good**.
- When they disobeyed God their Creator, they found out that not everything was good.

- Now they were ashamed and afraid.

- Adam and Eve should have trusted God instead of trying to find out for themselves.

God had given them every good thing to eat – He had even offered to them the opportunity to eat from the tree of life.

But they had **chosen to disobey God** and to eat, instead, from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil – the one and only tree from which God had forbidden them to eat.

Now, because they had disobeyed God, He would **not allow them to eat from the tree of life**.

Therefore, God put Adam and Eve, the father and mother of us all, out of the garden, away from the tree of life.
