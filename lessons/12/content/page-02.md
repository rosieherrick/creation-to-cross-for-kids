# 🧭 [Lesson 12: The Consequences of Sin and the Birth of Cain and Abel](../README.md)

## 🧩 God Killed Animals

🧵Theme - God is holy and righteous. He demands death as the payment for sin.

The first death in the world was brought about by sin.

- God killed animals.

  The animals' blood was shed.

  God then took the skins off the animals.

- God was reminding Adam and Eve that disobedience to Him brought death into the world.

## 🧩 God Provided Clothing for Adam and Eve

🧵Theme - God is loving, merciful, and gracious.

Why did God kill the animals?

To provide clothing for guilty Adam and Eve.

- God provided clothing, even though they did not deserve it.
