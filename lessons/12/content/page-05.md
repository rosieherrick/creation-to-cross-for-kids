# 🧭 [Lesson 12: The Consequences of Sin and the Birth of Cain and Abel](../README.md)

## 🧩 Cain and Abel Born Outside of the Garden

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 4:2 ESV

> "And again, she bore his brother Abel. Now Abel was a keeper of sheep, and Cain a worker of the ground."

Because Adam and Eve had sinned against God, they were put outside the garden.

- Cain and Abel were born outside the garden, away from the tree of life, because Adam, their father, was outside of the garden.

Because Adam disobeyed God and was separated from Him, all people in the world are born sinners, cut off from God.

## 🧩 Conclusion

Adam and Eve were real people, and we are their descendants.

Adam and Eve's sin affected all of us.

God has written down these events in the Bible so we can understand where we came from and who He is. He **wants** us to know Him!
