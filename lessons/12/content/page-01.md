## 🧩 Introduction

If you want to understand why things are the way they are in this world, read your Bible!

So far we have studied just the first three chapters of Genesis and have found that the events recorded affected **all** mankind – every person who has ever lived – including you and me.

## 🧩 God Refused the Clothing Adam and Eve Made

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner, He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

🧵Theme - Man can only come to God His way.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 3:21 ESV

> And the LORD God made for Adam and for his wife garments of skins and clothed them.

Let's read again what Adam and Eve tried to do to cover their nakedness.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 3:7 NIV

> Then the eyes of both of them were opened, and they realized they were naked; so they sewed fig leaves together and made coverings for themselves.

- They made themselves coverings of leaves.
- Because they had disobeyed God, they were embarrassed to have God see them naked.

But God refused to accept the clothes which Adam and Eve had made. Why?

He wanted to teach them that they couldn't do **anything** to make themselves acceptable to God.

God will not accept anything that is done man's way.
