# 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 [Creation to the Cross - for Kids](../../README.md)

## 🧭 Lesson 12: The Consequences of Sin and the Birth of Cain and Abel

### 📖 Scripture – Genesis 3:21-24; 4:1-2

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** 1 Chronicles 29:11, Job 41:11, Psalms 24:1; 100:3, Isaiah 64:6, Acts 17:25, Romans 5:12, Ephesians 2:8-9.

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show that God knows everything and will punish all sin.
- To show that man cannot save himself from sin.
- To show that only God can make man acceptable in His sight.
- To show that the sin of Adam has been passed on to all men.

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To realize that they are sinners.
- To realize that no one can “get away with” sin.
- To realize that there is nothing they can do to make themselves acceptable to God.

### 🧭 Overview

This lesson helps children see God's holiness and grace in the story of Adam and Eve after the fall. It teaches that the coverings Adam and Eve made were not acceptable to God, that God provided clothing of skins for them, and that He sent them out of the garden and guarded its entrance so they could not return. It also shows that God is the giver of life and that Cain and Abel, like all of Adam's descendants, were born outside the garden as sinners, separated from God.

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – Hebrews 4:13 ESV

> And no creature is hidden from his sight, but all are naked and exposed to the eyes of him to whom we must give account.

---

### 💭 Perspective for the Teacher

This lesson introduces some basic principles. First of all, the Bible is very clear in stating that God killed animals in order to make acceptable coverings for Adam and Eve. Although the blood of animals could never pay for sin, from this time until the death of Christ, God accepted the blood of animals as a picture of the punishment that all sin deserves. Romans 6:23 says, _“For the wages of sin is death...”_ Hebrews 9:22 says, _“...without the shedding of
blood is no forgiveness.”_

Secondly, the Bible shows us that man cannot come to God on man's terms; man must come to God in the acceptable way that God has provided. Adam and Eve's clothing of leaves was not acceptable to God; neither is anything we might do to try to make ourselves acceptable to God.

Refusal or distortion of these two principles has given rise to many false religions. How tempted man is to follow a religion that overlooks the heavy penalty for sin! How tempted man is to try to find his own way to take care of his sin problem! How deceitful man's heart is! This is the story of the first blood sacrifice for sin, and it shows us that God will accept only those who come to Him in the way He prescribes. May we be faithful to help the children see and choose to come to God in His way!

### 🗣️ On Teaching This Lesson

You are carefully laying a scriptural foundation on which the Gospel will later be presented. Each lesson builds on previous lessons. So be sure to cover each point carefully. DON'T COMPLICATE THE MESSAGE! Carefully follow the outline, emphasizing the doctrinal 🧵Theme given.
