## 🧭 Lesson 21: God Cares for Joseph and Brings Israel to Egypt

### 📖 Scripture – Genesis 37:1-14, 18-20, 24, 28; 39:1, 20; 41:1-8, 14-16, 25-32, 38-41; 42:1-3, 6-8; 43:1, 2; 45:3-9, 25-28; 46:5-7

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** Genesis 15:13; 45:1–2; 50:20. 1 Chronicles 29:1, 12. Psalm 22:4–5; 24:1–2; 105:16–23. Lamentations 3:22–23. Isaiah 46:10. Daniel 2:27–28; 4:17. Acts 7:9–15; 17:24–25. Hebrews 11:1–2, 6

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show that God is sovereign.
- To show that God fulfills His promises.
- To present Joseph as a man of faith.

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To see that God carries out His plans, even through difficult circumstances.
- To realize that God is in control.
- To see that God is faithful.

### 🧭 Overview

This lesson presents God's sovereign working and through the life of Joseph to bring Israel into Egypt. God's faithfulness is highlighted through Joseph's trust in Him. God communicated with Joseph through dreams and brought to fulfillment all He had promised.

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – Jeremiah 29:11 NIV

> For I know the plans I have for you,” declares the LORD, “plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future.

---

### 💭 Perspective for the Teacher

We are barraged daily by the news, but never has it been reported that God directed the heart of a king. The story of Joseph is God's accurate presentation of the news of Egypt and Israel. God not only **knows all** that happens, but He also **understands all** that happens, because **He is in supreme control over all** that happens.

Our students need to see that God still moves in the affairs of men and nations. He has not changed; and though some people consider this story ancient, it is really fresh for our needs today. God still works through men of faith and still carries out His purposes – even through the lives of pagan rulers.

As Joseph held fast in his faith, God was redirecting the future of the entire nation of Israel. Joseph was unshakable because his faith was deeply rooted in the invisible, immovable, unchanging God!

In a day of terrifying headlines and massive political struggles, our students need to see that God is still in control; He is our only hope for stability and salvation!

Joseph's story is a beautiful "type" of Christ – His trust, betrayal, suffering, and exaltation. Later on, after the Gospel has been presented, you will be able to refer back to this lesson and draw parallels between Joseph's life and life of the Lord Jesus Christ.

### 🗣️ On Teaching This Lesson

You are carefully laying a scriptural foundation on which the Gospel will later be presented. Each lesson builds on previous lessons. So be sure to cover each point carefully. DON'T COMPLICATE THE MESSAGE! Carefully follow the outline. Emphasize the doctrinal 🧵Theme given.
