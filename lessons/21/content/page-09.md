# 🧭 [Lesson 21: God Cares for Joseph and Brings Israel to Egypt](../README.md)

## 🧩 Israel Went Down to Egypt

🧵Theme - God is everywhere all the time; He knows everything.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 45:25-28; 46:5-7 ESV

> So they went up out of Egypt and came to the land of Canaan to their father Jacob. And they told him, “Joseph is still alive, and he is ruler over all the land of Egypt.” And his heart became numb, for he did not believe them. But when they told him all the words of Joseph, which he had said to them, and when he saw the wagons that Joseph had sent to carry him, the spirit of their father Jacob revived. And Israel said, “It is enough; Joseph my son is still alive. I will go and see him before I die.” Then Jacob set out from Beersheba. The sons of Israel carried Jacob their father, their little ones, and their wives, in the wagons that Pharaoh had sent to carry him. They also took their livestock and their goods, which they had gained in the land of Canaan, and came into Egypt, Jacob and all his offspring with him, his sons, and his sons' sons with him, his daughters, and his sons' daughters. All his offspring he brought with him into Egypt.

God knows what is going to happen; God always keeps His promises, every single one. God had long ago promised Abraham many things...

|                                                                                     🤝 God's Promises to Abraham                                                                                     |
| :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------: |
|                                                               ⛰️ **Land** - God told Abram that He would give him the land of Canaan.                                                                |
|                                                       👥 **Huge Family** - God promised that Abram's family would number more than the stars.                                                        |
|                                          ☂️ **Protection** - God told Abram that He would help those who helped his family, and hurt anyone who hurt them.                                           |
|                                                  🛟 **Future Savior** - God said one of Abram's future family members would be the promised Savior.                                                  |
| 💍 **Future Belongings** - God said that Abram's family would go live in another country and that they would be treated badly for 400 years, but after that they would leave with great possessions. |
|                  📍 **Returning Destination** - God said that although they would leave, God would bring them back to Canaan, to that very spot and then the land would be theirs!                   |

- Many years before, God had told Abraham that his descendants would go to another country.

- Even though many hundreds of years had passed, God did what He had promised.

- All that God says in His Word will happen just as God has said.

The very hard things that happened to Joseph turned out to be an important part of God's plan.

Joseph and his brothers and their father, Jacob, were now living in Egypt, just as God had told Abraham.

All of the descendants of Jacob, or Israel, were now called the children of Israel, the Israelites, or simply Israel.

## 🧩 Conclusion

We are still hearing about Israel in the news today.

And today, God is still keeping His promises.

God is carrying out His plans for good, even in the midst of very difficult circumstances.
