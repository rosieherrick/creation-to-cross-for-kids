# 🧭 [Lesson 21: God Cares for Joseph and Brings Israel to Egypt](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. Who was Jacob's favorite son?

   _Joseph (Genesis 37:3)_

1. How did Joseph's brothers feel about their father's love for Joseph?

   _They were jealous, and they hated Joseph. (Genesis 37:4)_

1. What did Joseph dream about?

   _Joseph dreamed about his family bowing down to him, first represented by bundles of crops (his brothers' sheaves bowing to his sheaf) and then by the sun, the moon, and 11 stars bending down in respect. (Genesis 37:7-9)_

1. What did these dreams mean?

   _🌟 They meant that Joseph would one day be given a position of authority over his family. (Genesis 37:8, 10; 42:6; 43:26)_

1. Who knew Joseph's future and showed it to him through his dreams?

   _God did. (Psalm 139:1-4)_

1. How much of our future does God know?

   _God knows all things, including our future. (Psalm 139:15-16)_

1. What did Joseph's older brothers do to him?

   _They sold Joseph to become a slave in Egypt. (Genesis 37:18-28)_

1. What happened to Joseph in Egypt?

   _The wife of Joseph's owner lied about him, and he was put into prison. (Genesis 39:11-20)_

1. Who took care of Joseph in prison?

   _God did. (Genesis 39:21)_

1. Why did God take care of Joseph?

   - _🙏 Because, although Joseph was a sinner, he trusted God and His mercy. (Psalm 105:17-19)_
   - _🛟 Because Joseph believed God's promises regarding the Savior. (Hebrews 11:22)_
   - _🌟 Because God had a wonderful plan for Joseph's life. (Jeremiah 29:11)_

1. Who gave Joseph the understanding of Pharaoh's dreams?

   _God did. (Genesis 41:25)_

1. What did Pharaoh's dreams mean?

   _God was making known to the king that there would be seven years of very good harvest which would be followed by seven years of drought. (Genesis 41:26-31)_

1. Is God able to do whatever He wants to do, even in places where the people do not know or worship Him?

   _Yes, God is able to do whatever He wants to do, for He is almighty. (Daniel 4:35)_

1. How did God fulfill the dreams which He had given to Joseph when he was a young man?

   _The Lord gave Joseph wisdom to interpret the king's dreams so that Joseph would be given the position next to the king. (Genesis 41:38-41)_

1. Why did Jacob take all of his family down to live in Egypt?

   - _Because Jacob had heard that Joseph was in Egypt. (Genesis 45:28)_
   - _Because there was plenty of food in Egypt. (Genesis 42:2)_
   - _Because the king invited Jacob to live in Egypt. (Genesis 45:17-20)_
   - _Because God had planned for this to happen. (Genesis 46:3-4)_

1. What were Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob's descendants called?

   _The children of Israel, Israelites, or Israel. (Genesis 32:28; 46:8)_
