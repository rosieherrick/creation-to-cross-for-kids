# 🧭 [Lesson 21: God Cares for Joseph and Brings Israel to Egypt](../README.md)

## 🧩 Joseph's Older Brothers Hated Him

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner; he needs God and is helpless to save himself.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 37:4 ESV

> But when his brothers saw that their father loved him more than all his brothers, they hated him and could not speak peacefully to him.

Because Joseph was his father's favorite son, all of his older brothers hated him.

- 🔎 Consider:

  _The reason why people get angry and hate one another is that everyone's heart is evil. Do you have anger, hatred, and other evil things in your heart and mind sometimes? All of us were born separated from God, and there is no way we can change ourselves. We do these sinful, evil things because we were born sinners just like our father, Adam._
