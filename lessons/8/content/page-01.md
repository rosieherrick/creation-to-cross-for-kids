# 🧭 [Lesson 8: Adam in the Garden](../README.md)

## 🧩 Introduction

Have you ever started a project that you didn't finish? Maybe you started reading a book and stopped part way through. Or maybe a craft project or a game of Monopoly that ended before it was done.

All of us have done it. You lose interest, change your mind, or run out of time.

God is not like us.

- He never gives up on what He plans to do.
- When He starts to do something, He **always** finishes it.
