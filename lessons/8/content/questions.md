# 🧭 [Lesson 8: Adam in the Garden](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. Does God ever begin a thing and then not finish it?

   _No. (Isaiah 46:10-11)_

1. Why doesn't God begin things and leave them unfinished?

   _Because God never changes, He always does what He says and nothing can keep Him from doing what He plans. (Isaiah 55:8-11)_

1. Why did God rest on the seventh day?

   _Because His work of creating was done. (Genesis 2:2)_

1. Who did God plant the garden of Eden for?

   _Adam. (Genesis 2:8)_

1. Why was it right for God to put Adam in the garden even though He didn't ask Adam if he wanted to live there?

   _God created Adam, so he rightfully belongs to God. (Psalm 100:3)_

1. To whom do all things in the universe belong?

   _To God, the Creator of everything. (Psalm 24:1)_

1. How many trees in the garden was Adam allowed to eat from?

   _From all of them except one, the tree of knowledge of good and evil. (Genesis 2:9)_

1. What would happen to Adam if he ate from the tree of knowledge of good and evil?

   _He would die or be separated. (Genesis 2:16-17)_

1. What would Adam be separated from?

   _He would be separated from God, from his body, and forever in the Lake of Fire. (Romans 6:23)_
