# 🧭 [Lesson 8: Adam in the Garden](../README.md)

## 🧩 Death – The Punishment for Disobedience to God

🧵Theme - God is holy and righteous. He demands death as the payment for sin.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 2:17 ESV

> But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil **you shall not eat**, for in the day that you eat of it **you shall surely die**.

What did God mean when He told Adam that he would die?

**The payment for sin is death (separation).**

### ⚰️ 1. Death – Separation From God

- It would be at the end of the relationship Adam has with God.

  _Do you remember what happened to Satan and the angels when they rebelled against God?_
  - _They were separated from God._
  - _They had their special jobs taken from them and God prepared a place of terrible punishment where he is going to send them forever._

- God warned Adam that, if he disobeyed His command, He would no longer be God's friend.

Adam would become God's enemy as Satan had.

Adam would be separated from God's love and friendship.

**The payment for sin is death (separation).**

### ⚰️ 2. Death – Separation From The Body

- God didn't mean that Adam would die physically on the same day that he ate the fruit.

- God meant that on that day, Adam would be cut off from God, who was his source of life. From that moment on his body would start the process of breaking down until one day he would finally die.

- When Adam finally dies, he will be separated from his body.

- 🔎 Consider:

  _It's just like a tree that loses a branch in a storm. That branch is not dead yet; the leaves are still green. But the branch has been separated from the tree, cut off from its source of life. Very soon it will dry up and die._

**The payment for sin is death (separation).**

### ⚰️ 3. Death – Separation Forever in the Lake of Fire

- Finally, if Adam disobeyed God, then not only would his body die, but he also would go to the Lake of Fire.

- This is the same place of terrible punishment which God prepared for Satan and the angels who followed him.

- Adam's punishment would be forever and ever.
