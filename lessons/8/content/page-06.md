# 🧭 [Lesson 8: Adam in the Garden](../README.md)

## 🧩 God's Command to Adam

## 📖 READ – Genesis 2:16-17 ESV

> And the Lord God commanded the man, saying, "You may surely eat of every tree of the garden, but of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil you shall not eat, for in the day that you eat of it you shall surely die."

🧵Theme - God communicates with man

God told Adam what was good to do and what was not good to do:

- When God put Adam in the garden, God did not leave it up to Adam to decide what was right and what was wrong.
- God spoke to Adam and told him what he must not do and what would happen if he disobeyed.

God gave Adam an endless variety of trees—trees which supplied food and were beautiful too.

- Only **one tree** was off limits.

Adam could choose to obey or to disobey God.

- Adam could choose to obey God and eat only what God had said was good.
- If Adam disobeyed God and ate the fruit from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, it would be because Adam had **decided** that he wanted to go his **own way**, apart from God.

- If Adam disobeyed God and wanted to go his own way, then the result would be death, meaning separation from God.
