# 🧭 [Lesson 8: Adam in the Garden](../README.md)

## 🧩 Seventh Day: God Rested From His Work

🧵Theme - God is all-powerful.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 2:2-3 ESV

> And on the seventh day God finished his work that he had done, and he rested on the seventh day from all his work that he had done.

How many days did it take for God to make **everything**? Only six days!

- 🔎 Compare:

  _Have you ever watched as a house was being built? It takes a long time, doesn't it?_

  _But look at all the things God created in just six days. There is no one as great as God. He is almighty. He can do anything He wants._

God finished in six days all He planned to do, so on the seventh day, He rested from His work of creating.

- Did God rest His work because He became tired?

- 🔎 Compare:

  _When you work hard, do you get tired? Do you sometimes like to take a break and rest?_

  _Just think of **all** that God had done in just one week! He made millions of stars, the sun and the moon. Look at the things he created on earth. Do you think He lay down and said, "I'm tired. I'm going to have a good rest today"?_

  _No. God doesn't get tired or sleepy like we do. He doesn't have a physical body that needs rest or sleep. God is Spirit._

## 📖 READ – Isaiah 40:28 ESV

> Have you not known? Have you not heard? The Lord is the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth. **He does not faint or grow weary**; his understanding is unsearchable.

No. God wasn't tired, He rested because He **finished** everything He planned to do!

- 🔎 Compare:

  _Who here has played with Lego? Let's say you were given a new Lego set for your birthday. You worked on it every day until every brick was placed and it was all done. What would you do at that moment when you finally placed the last brick? Don't you think you would take a break, stand back, and admire the work you had just done?_

  _That's how it was with God and all that He had created. He had done all that He planned to do and He was very pleased with everything He had made._

God created everything in six days, and so He rested on the seventh day.

He rested from creating because He saw that all His work which He had planned to do was finished.
