# 🧭 [Lesson 31: Israel Refused to Respond to God's Prophets](../README.md)

## 🧩 Introduction

Have you ever tried to tell someone something they didn't want to hear?

It's not easy, is it!

God sent many men to warn Israel, but Israel refused to listen.

## 🗣️ God Spoke to Mankind Since the Beginning

🧵Theme - God is loving, merciful, and gracious.

🧵Theme - God communicates with man.

God is the Creator of all the people in the world.

God loves all mankind and wants all people everywhere to be delivered from the power of Satan, sin, and death.

Therefore, from the very beginning of the world, God has spoken to people so they would know His will.

- 🔎 Recall:

  _God spoke to Adam and Eve, Cain, and Noah. God also spoke to Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph. God gave His message to Moses and the Israelites and spoke His commandments from the top of Mount Sinai._

God spoke through His messengers to Israel and Judah in order to teach them His ways and to warn them of His judgment of sin.

God also sent some of His messengers to other nations near the land of Israel.
