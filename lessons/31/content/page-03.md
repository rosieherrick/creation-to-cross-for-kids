# 🧭 [Lesson 31: Israel Refused to Respond to God's Prophets](../README.md)

## 🛟 God Didn't Forget His Promise

🧵Theme - God is faithful; He always does what He says; He never changes.

Even though thousands of years had passed since God gave the first promise of the Savior in the garden of Eden, the Lord had not forgotten His promise to send the Savior.

- 🔎 Recall:

  _God repeated the same promise to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. He promised them that the Savior would be one of their descendants. All through the years, God reminded Israel of the promised Savior. The Lord promised David that the coming Savior would be one of his family line._

🧵Theme - God is everywhere all the time; He knows everything.

🧵Theme - God communicates with man.

Hundreds of years before the great Savior came, God foretold many more things about Him.

| 📢 God Spoke Through the Prophets |
| :-------------------------------: |
|            🔄 Repent!             |
|      🗿 Destroy your idols!       |
|       🙏 Trust only in God!       |
|   🛟 God is sending the Savior!   |

- He told His chosen messengers, the **prophets**, what would happen to the Savior.

- They wrote these things down in their books which are now recorded in the Bible.

- God's messages about what will happen in the future are called **prophecies**.

- In the coming lessons we will look at some of the prophecies and see how God fulfilled them, that is, made them come true.

God knows **everything** before it ever happens.

🧵Theme - God is loving, merciful, and gracious.

God did not give up on His plan to send the Savior even though most of the people were not interested in His will for them nor in His promises.

God loves the world and does not want anyone to go to everlasting punishment.
