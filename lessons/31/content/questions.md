# 🧭 [Lesson 31: Israel Refused to Respond to God's Prophets](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. Why did God call men to be His messengers?

   _Because God loves all people, He wants them to know the truth and to be delivered from the power of Satan, sin, and death. (John 3:16)_

1. What did God's prophets say to Israel at this time?

   _They told them to 🔄 repent, 🗿 destroy their idols, and 🙏 trust only in the Lord. (Isaiah 45:22)_

1. What did the Lord say He would do if they refused to repent?

   _The Lord said that He would allow the Assyrians to capture Israel and the Babylonians to destroy Jerusalem. (Isaiah 10:5, 6; Jeremiah 20:5)_

1. What was the attitude of most of the Israelites toward God and His prophets?

   - They persecuted and killed God's prophets. (2 Chronicles 36:15–16)

   - They continued to worship idols. (2 Kings 17:7–8, 12)

   - They followed the wicked ways of the surrounding nations. (2 Kings 17:8,11)

1. Did the Israelites continue to go to the temple, offer sacrifices, and worship God?

   _Yes, they did. (Isaiah 64:6)_

1. Why didn't God accept the worship and sacrifices of many of the Israelites?

   _Because they were worshiping God with their lips, but in their hearts, they didn't trust in God. (Isaiah 29:13)_

1. Who sees into our thoughts and knows our hearts?

   _God. (1 Samuel 16:7)_

1. What does God say about the condition of all our hearts?

   _God says that we are all sinners and cannot please Him. (Jeremiah 17:9)_

1. What did the Lord allow to happen to the northern ten tribes of Israel?

   _The Lord allowed the Assyrians to take them as captives. (2 Kings 17:5–6)_

1. What did the Lord allow to happen to Jerusalem and the two southern tribes?

   _The Lord allowed the Babylonians to destroy Jerusalem and to take the two tribes captive. (2 Chronicles 36:19,20)_

1. What happened when the Israelites repented, or changed their minds?

   _They rebuilt the city and its stone walls, and they rebuilt the temple of the Lord in Jerusalem. (2 Chronicles 36:21–23)_

1. What new name were the Israelites given?

   _Jews. (2 Kings 25:25)_

1. What other people conquered the Jews?

   _The Greeks and the Romans._

1. What were synagogues?

   _They were meeting places of the Jews where they taught the Old Testament Scriptures. The Jews met in the synagogues on the last day of each week. (Acts 15:21)_

1. Were there any Jews who were true believers, like Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Moses, and David?

   _Yes, some realized that they were sinners and trusted in God. They came to God in a way He had told Moses, bringing animals and blood sacrifices. They were waiting for God to send the Savior. (Isaiah 1:9)_
