# 🧭 [Lesson 31: Israel Refused to Respond to God's Prophets](../README.md)

## 🧩 Israel's Attitude Toward God, Prophets, and Their Message

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

There were always some Israelites who believed God and believed the message of His prophets.

But most of the Israelites refused to obey the words of God.

They persecuted (mistreated and hurt) and sometimes even killed God's messengers.

- Israel and Judah continued to worship idols.

- They followed the wicked ways of the surrounding nations.

- Many continued to go to the temple to worship the Lord, to perform all the ceremonies, and to offer sacrifices, but they did not obey God in their everyday living.

- They said many good things to God, but He did not accept what they said because He could see that they did not mean them in their hearts.

- Isaiah 29:13 says, _"...this people draw near me with their mouth, and with their lips do honor me, but have removed their heart far from me...."_

🧵Theme - God is everywhere all the time; He knows everything.

We cannot fool God.

- Man can only see the outside of us and can only hear our words.

- God looks into our thoughts and our heart.

He knows what we are really like inside.

- He says that we are all sinners.

- None of us pleases God by what we do.

Most of the priests and leaders were evil men.

- They did not trust in the Lord.
- They did not obey His commands.

🧵Theme - Satan fights against God and His will. Satan is a liar and a deceiver. He hates man.

Besides the true prophets sent by God, there were also **false** prophets who spoke lies to Israel.

- They claimed to be the messengers of God, but they were the servants of Satan.

- They spoke lies to the people.

- They told the people everything would be alright and that God would not punish them.

## 📖 READ – Jeremiah 6:13,14 ESV

> For from the least to the greatest of them, everyone is greedy for unjust gain; and from prophet to priest, everyone deals falsely. They have healed the wound of my people lightly, saying, ‘Peace, peace,’ when there is no peace.

- 🔎 Compare:

  _Satan remains unchanged today. He speaks through people who may claim that God’s Word is not true, that God will not punish sin, or that He would never send anyone to Hell._

  _Some are suggesting that God exists so He can give people the things they want. They say that if you give a lot of money to the church, God has to give you lots of money in return._

  _God does not owe people **anything**. He is the Creator and greater than all. He will punish all sin. Do not listen to anyone who is teaching anything different from what God has said in the Bible._
