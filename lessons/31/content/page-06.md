# 🧭 [Lesson 31: Israel Refused to Respond to God's Prophets](../README.md)

## 🧩 The Return of Exiles From Babylon

🧵Theme - God is loving, merciful, and gracious.

🧵Theme - God is faithful; He always does what He says; He never changes.

After many years, the people of Judah who were in Babylon repented and asked the Lord to take them back to their own land.

The Lord heard their cry for His help, and He brought many of them back to Jerusalem.

- They rebuilt Jerusalem and the stone walls around the city.

- They also rebuilt the temple.

The Israelites who returned to their land were given another name.

They were called **Jews**, which is the name still used today for the descendants of these people.

Back in their land, they came and brought sacrifices to God. But most of them did not worship from their hearts.

They still did not believe they were sinners who needed God's mercy.

## 🧩 The Greeks and Romans

The Lord punished them once again by allowing other nations to conquer them.

The **Greeks** took control of the Jews' country and taught them to speak the Greek language.

🧵Theme - God is greater than all and more important than all; He is the highest authority.

🧵Theme - God communicates with man.

God was going to use the Greek language to spread His Word over much of the civilized world.

- 🔎 Expand:

  _At that time, only the first part of the Bible, the part we know as the Old Testament, had been written. Some of these Old Testament books were now being translated from the original Hebrew language into Greek. People in countries far from Israel who could read Greek read these Old Testament books. God used this to prepare them for the coming Savior._

  _Later, in the first century A.D., God gave the men the rest of His Word, the New Testament. He caused it to be written in the Greek language. By this time Greek was spoken by many people in distant countries._

After a time **Romans** overcame the Greeks, and they took control of Jerusalem and the land of Israel.

The Romans ruled over the Jews and made them pay taxes.

- If anyone disobeyed, he was strictly punished.
- The Romans killed many Jews with sword and spear; others they crucified.

The Roman Emperor, Caesar, appointed men to rule over the countries which he had conquered.

- The Romans worshiped many false gods; they also worshiped Caesar.
- But the Romans permitted the Jews to continue worshiping the Lord by going to the temple.

Again, in God's sovereignty, Roman rule had its benefits.

- God allowed the Romans to build many excellent roads, linking their huge empire.
- Later on God was going to use these roads for people to travel to distant places to tell others about God's Word.

🧵Theme - God is loving, merciful, and gracious.

God cares about the people of all nations and wants them to know Him.
