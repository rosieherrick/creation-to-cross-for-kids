# 🧭 [Lesson 10: Adam and Eve Disobeyed](../README.md)

## 🧩 Sin Separated Them from God

🧵Theme - God never changes. He always does what He says; He is faithful.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 3:7-8 ESV

> Then the eyes of both were opened, and they knew that they were naked. And they sewed fig leaves together and made themselves loincloths. And they heard the sound of the LORD God walking in the garden in the cool of the day, and the man and his wife hid themselves from the presence of the LORD God among the trees of the garden.

What did God say would happen to them if they ate of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil?

What did God mean by “die”?

- They didn't drop dead immediately when they ate the fruit.
- They were still walking around.
- They were making themselves coverings out of leaves.
- Does this mean that God's Word didn't come true and Satan was right?

No! They were separated from God immediately when they ate the fruit.

- God always does what He says.
- He never changes.
- He doesn't forget His threats to punish disobedience of His commands.

🧵Theme - God holy and righteous. He demands death as the payment for sin.

Why did their sin separate them from God?

Isaiah 59:2 says that, _“...your iniquities have **made a separation** between you and your God...”_

- Because God is holy and righteous. He will not continue in friendship with those who disobey His commands.

- He hates everything that is wrong and punishes all disobedience of His commands by death.
- Romans 6:23 says, _“...the wages [payment] for sin is death...”_
