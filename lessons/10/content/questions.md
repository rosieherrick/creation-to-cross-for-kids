# 🧭 [Lesson 10: Adam and Eve Disobeyed](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. When Satan came to deceive Eve, did he come to talk to her face-to-face so she could see him and know who he was?

   _No. (Genesis 3:1-2)_

1. What did Satan use to disguise himself when he spoke to Eve?

   _A snake. (Genesis 3:1)_

1. God told Adam that, if they ate the fruit from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, they would definitely die, but Satan told Eve they would not. What was Satan saying about God?

   _That He is a liar. (John 8:44)_

1. Does Satan want you to believe God?

   _No. (2 Corinthians 4:4)_

1. Adam and Eve didn't immediately drop dead when they ate the fruit. God had said they would die for their disobedience. What did He mean?

   _They would immediately be separated from God and their relationship with God would end. (Isaiah 59:2)_

   _Their bodies would eventually die because of their disobedience and then they would be separated forever in the Lake of Fire. (Romans 6:23)_

1. What did Adam and Eve do when they heard God coming to see them?

   _They hid because they were ashamed and afraid. (Genesis 3:8)_

1. Is it possible to hide from God?

   _No. God is everywhere. (Hebrews 4:13)_
