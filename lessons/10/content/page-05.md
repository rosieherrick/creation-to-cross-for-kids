# 🧭 [Lesson 10: Adam and Eve Disobeyed](../README.md)

## 🧩 Separated from God

Because Adam and Eve disobeyed God, they were cut off from their friendship with God.

- They had taken sides with God's great enemy, Satan, and they, too, were now enemies of God.

- 🔎 Illustrate:

  _Look at this branch. [Show the children a fresh cut branch]. You can see that it didn't turn brown the minute it was cut off the tree. It still has some green in it. But it is dying because it is cut off from its source of life._

  _The same thing happened to Adam and Eve. They didn't drop dead the very day that they disobeyed God. They were still breathing and walking around. But they had died to God. That part of them which was made in God's image so they could know, love and obey Him was immediately separated from God when they disobeyed His command._

## 🧩 Enemies with God

They were now separated from Him and had become enemies of God. Their bodies would also eventually die, and they would then go to a place of everlasting punishment which God had prepared for Satan.

## 🧩 Signs of Separation from God

These were the signs that they were now separated from God:

- First, their attitude toward their bodies changed immediately.

  Before they disobeyed God, they were naked, but they weren't embarrassed.

  Now they were separated from God, and their minds were evil, and they were embarrassed.

- Secondly, they tried to provide for their own needs.

  Before they were separated from God, they looked to God to provide everything they needed but now they tried to do things for themselves.
  - They clothed themselves with fig leaves.
  - Perhaps they thought that if they covered themselves, God wouldn't notice anything had happened.
  - But God doesn't look at our outward appearance… 1 Samuel 16:7 says _“...man looks on the outward appearance, but the LORD looks on the heart.”_

- Adam and Eve not only tried to cover their nakedness, but when God came to visit Adam and Eve, they also tried to hide from Him.

- 🔎 Compare:

  _Have you ever broke something that wasn't yours and then hid it when the rightful owner came near? Have you ever tried to cover up something wrong you did? Yes, all of us have done just that, just like Adam and Eve._
