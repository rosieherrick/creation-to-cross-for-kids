# 🧭 [Lesson 10: Adam and Eve Disobeyed](../README.md)

## 🧩 Conclusion

🧵Theme - God is everywhere all the time and He knows everything.

Were Adam and Eve able to hide from God?

No! God saw Adam and Eve when they were trying to hide.

Can anyone hide from God?

Is there anywhere we can go where God is not already there?

No! God is everywhere.

- No one can hide from God.
- God has heard and seen everything that we have said and done from the day we were born.
