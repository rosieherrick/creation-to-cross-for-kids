# 🧭 [Lesson 10: Adam and Eve Disobeyed](../README.md)

## 🧩 Introduction

Today we are going to learn about one of the most important events in history.

This event affected **every person who has ever lived!**

## 🧩 Life in the Garden of Eden – Friends with God

Life in the garden was pleasant for Adam and Eve. Everything was perfect.

- They had everything that they needed.
- God was their **friend**; He loved them, and they were very happy.

They had **perfect friendship** with God and each other.

But what about Satan's great enemy, Satan?

- Satan knew about God's warning to Adam concerning the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.
- Because Satan hates God, he wanted to destroy man and woman and the friendship they had with God.
- Satan came up with a plan to try to trick Eve.
