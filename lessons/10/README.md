# 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 [Creation to the Cross - for Kids](../../README.md)

## 🧭 Lesson 10: Adam and Eve Disobeyed

### 📖 Scripture – Genesis 3:1-8

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** Genesis 2:7; 1 Chronicles 29:11–12; Psalm 24:1; 139; Jeremiah 23:23–24; Matthew 12:30; Luke 11:23; Acts 5:3; 17:25; 2 Corinthians 4:4; 11:14; 1 Timothy 2:14; Hebrews 2:15

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show Satan's hatred for God and for man, and Satan's character as a liar, deceiver, accuser, and murderer.
- To show man's choice to willfully disobey God and the immediate results of that sin.

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To see that Satan is God's enemy and our enemy.
- To recognize some of Satan's schemes.
- To see the terrible consequences of sin.

### 🧭 Overview

This lesson presents Satan's deception of Eve and Adam's disobedience of God's command. It emphasizes how Satan tricked Eve, how Adam and Eve chose to disobey God, and how their sin separated them from God.

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – Romans 3:23 NIV

> For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.

---

### 💭 Perspective for the Teacher

Many people today think they already know what the Bible says about Adam and Eve's first sin in the Garden of Eden, and the part that Satan played. They treat it as a story or a myth without studying God's Word.

We have a tremendous obligation to present this passage clearly as God's truth. This is not simply a story about an apple, a snake, and a woman. It is the critical point of all human history before the coming of Christ.

The Bible shows that Satan is the enemy of God and of man. He is a liar, deceiver, accuser, and murderer. He wants to keep people from believing God.

### 🗣️ On Teaching This Lesson

You are carefully laying a scriptural foundation on which the Gospel will later be presented. Each lesson builds on previous lessons. So be sure to follow each point carefully. DON'T COMPLICATE THE MESSAGE! Carefully follow the lesson, emphasizing the doctrinal 🧵Theme given.
