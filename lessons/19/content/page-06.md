# 🧭 [Lesson 19: God Provided a Substitute for Isaac](../README.md)

## 🧩 Abraham Trusted God to Send a Savior

## 📖 READ – Genesis 22:14a ESV

> So Abraham called the name of that place, “The Lord will provide”; as it is said to this day, “On the mount of the Lord it shall be provided.”

Abraham called the place where God provided the ram, “The Lord will provide.”

- God provided a ram to die instead of Isaac.
- Abraham believed that God would also provide the Savior to rescue all people from the power of Satan and the punishment of sin.

God keeps every promise He makes.

## 🧩 Conclusion

We cannot begin to imagine such a difficult test as Abraham faced.

God provided a way for Isaac to be saved; He provided an acceptable sacrifice – a ram to die in his place.

The family line of the promised Savior was preserved.

God can be completely trusted to fulfill His promises.
