# 🧭 [Lesson 19: God Provided a Substitute for Isaac](../README.md)

## 📖 READ – Genesis 22:13 ESV

> And Abraham lifted up his eyes and looked, and behold, behind him was a ram, caught in a thicket by its horns. And Abraham went and took the ram and offered it up as a burnt offering instead of his son.

God caused the ram to be caught by its horns in the bush.

- If it had been caught by any other part of its body, it would have injured itself by struggling to get free.
- If it had been injured, it would not have been an acceptable offering to God.
- God provided an acceptable sacrifice to be offered in the place of Isaac.

🧵Theme - God never changes; He always does what He says; He is faithful.

God is faithful and keeps His promise.

- God kept His promise to Abraham.
- God promised that he would give Abraham a big family through his son, Isaac.

Abraham took Isaac off the altar and put the ram which God had provided on the altar so it could be sacrificed instead of Isaac.

- Abraham killed the ram and burned it as an offering to God.
- The ram died instead of Isaac.
- The ram was his substitute.
- God saved Isaac by providing the ram to die in his place.

## 🛟 Abraham Trusted God to Send a Savior

## 📖 READ – Genesis 22:14a ESV

> So Abraham called the name of that place, “The Lord will provide”; as it is said to this day, “On the mount of the Lord it shall be provided.”

Abraham called the place where God provided the ram, “The Lord will provide.”

- God provided a ram to die instead of Isaac.
- Abraham believed that God would also provide the Savior to rescue all people from the power of Satan and the punishment of sin.

God keeps every promise He makes.

## 🧩 Conclusion

We cannot begin to imagine such a difficult test as Abraham faced.

God provided a way for Isaac to be saved; He provided an acceptable sacrifice – a ram to die in his place.

The family line of the promised Savior was preserved.

God can be completely trusted to fulfill His promises.
