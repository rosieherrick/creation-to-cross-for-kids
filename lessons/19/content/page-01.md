# 🧭 [Lesson 19: God Provided a Substitute for Isaac](../README.md)

## 🧩 Introduction

In Genesis 15:6 we read about Abraham. It says, _"And he believed the Lord, and he counted it to him as righteousness."_

Abraham believed God, and God accepted him as though he were
righteous.

- Today we are going to study the greatest test of Abraham's faith.
- But more important than Abraham's faith is the faithful God he trusted in.

God is the main character of this story and the whole Bible!
Listen carefully to the amazing things God did for Abraham.

## 🧩 God Fulfilled His Promise of a Son

🧵Theme - God is all-powerful.

Even though Abraham and Sarah were too old to have children, God had promised to give them a son.

- By this time, Abraham was 100 years old and Sarah was 90.
- Sarah had laughed when God said she would have a child.

  Sarah had never had a child.

  She knew that now she was too old to naturally have a child.

But nothing is impossible with God; He can do anything He wants to.

🧵Theme - God never changes; He always does what He says; He is faithful.

Many years had passed since God first promised to give Abraham a son but God hadn't forgotten His promise.

- He had not changed His mind.
- Sarah had a son just as God had promised.

Abraham and Sarah named their son Isaac.
