# 🧭 [Lesson 19: God Provided a Substitute for Isaac](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. Why was God able to give Abraham and Sarah a son even though he was 100 years old and she was 90?

   - _Because God gives life to all people everywhere. (Job 12:10)_
   - _God is able to do anything He wants to do. (Jeremiah 32:17)_

1. What authority did God have to ask Abraham to offer Isaac as a sacrifice?

   - _God is the Creator of all things and He owns all things. (Psalm 89:11)_
   - _God gave life to Isaac. (Psalm 100:3)_

1. Did Abraham think that God had changed his mind about giving Abraham a big family and sending a Savior through Isaac?

   _🙏 No, Abraham believed and trusted in God because he was convinced that God always keeps His promises. (Genesis 22:8)_

1. What did Abraham think God might do?

   _Abraham thought that if he did kill Isaac as God had commanded him, then God would raise Isaac from the dead. (Hebrews 11:17–19)_

1. Once Abraham had tied up Isaac and put him on the altar was there any way Isaac could save himself from death?

   _No, Isaac could not save himself from death. (Genesis 22:9)_

1. Is there any one who can save themselves from the payment of death that their sins deserve?

   _No, no one can save himself or herself from the punishment of God. No one can escape from God. (Hebrews 4:13)_

1. Who provided a sacrifice to take Isaac's place?

   _God did. (Genesis 22:13)_

1. Why did Abraham call the place where God provided the ram, “The Lord will provide”?

   _🛟 Because Abraham believed that, just as the Lord provided the ram instead of Isaac, the Lord would one day provide the Savior who would overcome Satan and deliver all people from Satan's power and everlasting punishment. (Genesis 22:14)_
