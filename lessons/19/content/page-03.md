# 🧭 [Lesson 19: God Provided a Substitute for Isaac](../README.md)

## 🧩 God Commanded Abraham to Offer Isaac

🧵Theme - God communicates with man.

Many years passed by, and Isaac grew up and soon was a young man.

His father and mother loved him.

Abraham believed that all of God's promises concerning the coming Savior were to be fulfilled through Isaac and his family.

One day God told Abraham to do a most unexpected and difficult thing.

🧵Theme - God is greater than all and more important than all; He is the highest authority.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 22:1-2 ESV

> After these things God tested Abraham and said to him, “Abraham!” And he said, “Here I am.” He said, “**Take your son, your only son** Isaac, whom you love, and go to the land of Moriah, and **offer him there as a burnt offering** on one of the mountains of which I shall tell you.”

God had a very special test for Abraham.

- God was asking Abraham to take his son to a special spot and God will show him, and kill Isaac. God was asking Abraham to sacrifice his only son, and to burn him as an offering to God.
- God was testing Abraham to see, now that he had the son he always wanted, and was waiting **so long** for, if Abraham now loved Isaac more.
- Who did Abraham love more... God, or his one and only son, Isaac?

How could God ask Abraham to take the life if his son, did He have the authority to do that?

- God gave Isaac life. God had given him to Abraham and Sarah. Isaac belonged to God.
- God created everything. God has the authority over all people including Isaac.

## 🧩 Abraham Believed God

🧵Theme - Man must have faith in order to please God and be saved.

How did Abraham respond? Did he tell God “no”?

- Even though Abraham was told to offer Isaac as a sacrifice, he did not question God.
- He knew that God would not lie.
- He knew that somehow, God would keep His promises.
- Abraham believed God completely and actually decided that if God really was going to ask him to kill Isaac, then God must be also planning to bring him back to life.
- Abraham knew that this was impossible for man, but trusted God to do it.
