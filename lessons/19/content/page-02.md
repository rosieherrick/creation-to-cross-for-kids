# 🧭 [Lesson 19: God Provided a Substitute for Isaac](../README.md)

## 📖 READ – Genesis 21:1-3 ESV

> The Lord visited Sarah as he had said, and the Lord did to Sarah as he had promised. And **Sarah conceived and bore Abraham a son** in his old age at the time of which God had spoken to him. **Abraham called the name of his son who was born to him, whom Sarah bore him, Isaac.**

🧵Theme - God is greater than all and more important than all; He is the highest authority.

God could decide to give Abraham and Sarah a son because God is the Creator of all people and all things.

- God gives life to all people everywhere.
- He gave life to the people who lived on earth before us.
- He gave life to each of us.
- This world and everyone in it belongs to God.
- Abraham and Sarah belonged to God.
- Their son Isaac belonged to God.
