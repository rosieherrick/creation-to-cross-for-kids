# 🧭 [Lesson 1: Introducing the Bible](../README.md)

## 🧩 The Bible is God's Personal Message to Us; the Most Important Message we will Ever Be Given

## 📖 READ – Psalm 119:89 ESV

> Forever, O Lord, your word is firmly fixed in the heavens.

Why is it so important for you and I to study the Bible?

## 🧩 The Bible is Special – Different than Any Other Book

- The Bible tells us about God.
- The Bible tells us what God thinks of us.

**The Bible is important because it is God's personal message to each one of us!**

Boys and girls, dads and moms, grandmas and grandpas, **all people everywhere** – God wrote the Bible to each one of us personally.
