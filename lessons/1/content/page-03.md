# 🧭 [Lesson 1: Introducing the Bible](../README.md)

## 🧩 God wants us to Know Him

|       Learning About God        |
| :-----------------------------: |
| 📖 **God wants us to know Him** |

No other book in the world was written this way – with God telling men exactly what to write.

God is the author of the Bible.

God used more than 40 men to write down His Words.

God used all kinds of different men… Moses–a shepherd, David and Solomon–both were kings, Peter–a fisherman, Matthew–a tax collector, Paul–a tent maker.

The Bible was not written all at once – it took 2,000 years for all the Bible to be written down!

## 📖 The Bible – God's Message to the World

🧵Theme - God is greater than all and more important than all. He is the highest in authority.

God wants us to know Him.

- One of the ways God has chosen to communicate His message is through His chosen people, Israel.
- All but one of the men whom God used to write His Word were Jewish. (Luke was apparenty a Gentile or non-Jew.)
- God gave His message through the Jewish people, but His message wasn't just for them, but for the whole world.

|                  📢 Through ISRAEL                   |
| :--------------------------------------------------: |
| 📖 **Bible** – God gave His Word to the whole world. |
