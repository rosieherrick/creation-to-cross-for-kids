# 🧭 [Lesson 24: God Saved Israel at the Red Sea](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. How did the Lord show the Israelites where to go?

   - _☁️ God led them by a cloud during the day. (Exodus 13:21)_
   - _🔥 God led by a pillar of fire at night. (Exodus 13:21)_

1. To which land were the Israelites heading?

   _⛰️ To Canaan, the land God promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. (Genesis 13:14,15)_

1. Why did God protect Israel?

   - _🤍 Because He is loving and merciful. (Psalm 136:10-15)_
   - _🛟 Because one of their descendants was to be the Savior. (Genesis 12:3, Genesis 26:3-4, Genesis 28:14)_
   - _🤝 Because of His promise to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. (Deuteronomy 7:7-8)_
   - _📖 Because He was giving His Word through Israel to the whole world. (Isaiah 49:6)_

1. What did the Israelites do when they saw Pharaoh's armies coming behind them?

   _They blamed Moses and said they should have left them in Egypt. (Exodus 14:10-12)_

1. Was there any way the Israelites could save themselves?

   _No. (Exodus 14:11-12)_

1. What should they have done?

   _🙏 They should have called on the Lord and trusted Him to rescue them. (Psalm 107:6)_

1. Why wasn't it difficult for God to open up the sea?

   - _Because He created the sea. (Psalm 95:5)_
   - _Because God is almighty. Nothing is too hard for Him. (Jeremiah 32:17)_

1. What did God do to protect the Israelites from the Egyptians when they were walking through the sea?

   _God placed the cloud behind the Israelites so it was between them and the Egyptians. The cloud shone brightly to show the way for the Israelites, but it was a dark, black cloud in front of the Egyptians. (Exodus 14:19-22)_

1. Why couldn't the Egyptians escape from God?

   _Because the Lord is greater than all. No one can fight agaist Him and win. He is almighty. (Proverbs 21:30)_

1. Can anyone make a way to save themself from God's punishment for their sins and make themselves acceptable to God?

   _No. (Romans 3:23)_
