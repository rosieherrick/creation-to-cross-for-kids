## 🧭 Lesson 17: God Renewed Promises to Abraham

### 📖 Scripture – Genesis 13:14-17; 15:5-6; 15:13-16; 17:1-5; 17:15-17

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** Romans 4:3; Galatians 3:8; Hebrews 11:11-12

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show that God credited Abraham's faith to him as righteousness.
- To show God's concern for individuals.

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To see that Abraham's faith was counted to him as righteousness.
- To realize that God cares about them personally.
- To see that the Bible is true.

### 🧭 Overview

This lesson explores the foundational biblical truth that God declared Abraham righteous, which was not on the basis of his works, but solely because of his faith. It examines how God communicated directly with Abraham, revealing His covenant promises, which will be referenced and developed in future lessons. Abraham's response serves as a model for all believers, demonstrating that coming to God requires faith rather than human effort or merit.

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – Genesis 15:6 ESV

> And he believed the Lord, and he counted it to him as righteousness.

---

### 💭 Perspective for the Teacher

One of the great lessons of this story is that we need to be mindful of the choices we make. Abraham chose to believe God, and God is still pouring out blessing on the descendants of Abraham—those who, like Abraham, believe God. But Lot made a choice that unnecessarily placed him and his family in the middle of a perverted, godless environment. That choice will cost him the lives of his wife and sons-in-law as well as his testimony.

### 🗣️ On Teaching This Lesson

You are carefully laying a scriptural foundation on which the Gospel will later be presented. Each lesson builds on previous lessons. So be sure to cover each point carefully. DON'T COMPLICATE THE MESSAGE! Carefully follow the outline. Emphasize the doctrinal 🧵Theme given.
