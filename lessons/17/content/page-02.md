# 🧭 [Lesson 17: God Renewed Promises to Abraham](../README.md)

## 🙏 Abram Believed God

🧵Theme - God communicates with man.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 15:5-6 ESV

> And he brought him outside and said, “Look toward heaven, and number the stars, if you are able to number them.” Then he said to him, “So shall your offspring be.” And he believed the Lord, and he counted it to him as righteousness.

- 🔎 Consider:

  _How many stars can you see in the sky on a dark, cloudless night?_

  _God took Abram outside his tent and told him to look up and see if he could count the stars._

  _If you can, try that this week too; see how many stars you can count. And remember, we can only see with our eye just a few of the many, many stars that are actually there. Even astronomers and scientists cannot count them all!_

God promised Abram, even before he had one child, that his family would be as many as the stars!

Abram believed God.

- What God had promised would have seemed impossible.
- Abram and Sarai had been married for many years and were now old, but they had never been able to have a child.
- Abram trusted God to give him a child and to send the Savior as one of his family members.

Therefore, because Abram **believed** God, God accepted him as if he had no sin.

- Abram was a sinner; but because of his trust in God, he was accepted by God as if he were perfectly right.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 15:6 ESV

> And he **believed** the Lord, and he counted it to him as righteousness.

What is righteousness?

- The root word is right.
- God accepted Abram just as though he were completely righteous.
- But Abram was a sinner, in Adam's family line, like us.

What did God find in Abram that would cause God to credit Abram with righteousness?

Why should God accept Abram, a sinner?

The only reason why God credited righteousness to Abram was because **Abram believed God**.

🙏 Abram **BELIEVED GOD**

📜 God **counted** it to him for righteousness!

Why did God do this for him?

Because Abram **believed** God!

Abram knew that he could not save himself from sins, but he **believed** that God was going to send a Savior who would be able to save him from Satan and sin and death.
