# 🧭 [Lesson 17: God Renewed Promises to Abraham](../README.md)

## 🧩 Introduction

Have you ever received something really good, even though you didn't deserve it? Maybe someone gave you a gift even though you hadn't earned it.

God gives us many good things we don't deserve!

The special word we use for this amazing, undeserved love is GRACE.

Today we will see examples of God giving grace over and over again.

## 🧩 God Renews Promise to Abram

🧵Theme - God communicates with man.

After Lot left Abram, the Lord spoke to Abram and promised once again...

⛰️ **Land** - God told Abram that He would give him the land of Canaan.

👥 **Huge Family** - God promised that Abram's family would be great.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 13:14-16 ESV

> The Lord said to Abram, after Lot had separated from him, “Lift up your eyes and look from the place where you are, northward and southward and eastward and westward, for **all the land that you see I will give to you** and to your offspring forever. **I will make your offspring as the dust of the earth**, so that if one can count the dust of the earth, your offspring also can be counted.”

- 🔎 Consider:

  _How many pieces of sand is in just one handful of sand? (about ten thousand)_

  _Now imagine how many separate grains would be in one beach full of sand. (about seven quintillion, five hundred quadrillion grains)_

  _Now think of that multiplied by all the grains of sand or dirt in the earth! That's how many descendants God said He would give Abram._

How many children did Abram have at this time? That's right, none!

But God is again promising Abram a big family and also land for all those people to live in and call their own.

> “...for all the land that you see I will give to you and to your offspring forever. I will make your offspring as the dust of the earth...”

Not only has God promised Abram a big family, to bless all people on earth, and for the Savior to be in his family... now God promised to give him land. He promised to give Abram's family the land of Canaan.

- 🔎 Consider:

  _How many of us here has ever had to move to a new country, or a new city, or maybe you have changed schools? You know how it feels to be new, uncomfortable, right?_

  _Remember, God had asked Abram to leave his home and to come here. Not only was he not in his home area, he's living in the leftover land Lot didn't want. He's also living in a tent!_

God promised Abram that some day his family will no longer be foreigners or outsiders but would be able to claim this land as their own. What a great promise!
