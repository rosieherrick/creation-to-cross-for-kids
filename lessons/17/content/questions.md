# 🧭 [Lesson 17: God Renewed Promises to Abraham](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. What did God promise Abram after he left his home area, his father died, and now Lot left him?

   _⛰️ God told Abram that He would give him the land of Canaan. (Genesis 13:14,15)_

1. What did God promise Abram about his family?

   _👥 God promised that Abram's family would number more than the stars. (Genesis 15:5)_

1. What did God say would happen to Abram's family in the future?

   - _💍 God said that Abram's family would go live in another country and that they would be treated badly for 400 years, but after that they would leave with great possessions. (Genesis 15:13-14)_
   - _📍 God said that although they would leave, God would bring them back to Canaan, to that very spot and then the land would be theirs! (Genesis 15:15-16)_

1. What new names did God give to Abram and Sarai?

   _Abraham and Sarah. (Genesis 17:5,15)_

1. Why would it take a miracle from God for Abraham and Sarah to have a child?

   - _Sarah was unable to have a child. (Genesis 11:30)_
   - _They were too old. Abraham was now 100 years old and Sarah was 90. (Genesis 17:17)_

1. Who created the first man and woman and gives life to every baby?

   _God. (Nehemiah 9:6)_

1. Is there anything which God wants to do but cannot?

   _No, God can do everything He wants to do. (Psalm 135:6)_

1. Who knows the future of every person?

   _Only God. (Psalm 139:4,16)_
