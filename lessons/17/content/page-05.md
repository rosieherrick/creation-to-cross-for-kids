# 🧭 [Lesson 17: God Renewed Promises to Abraham](../README.md)

## 🧩 God's Promise That Abraham and Sarah Would Have a Son

🧵Theme - God is all-powerful.

🧵Theme - Man needs God.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 17:17 ESV

> Then Abraham fell on his face and laughed and said to himself, “Shall a child be born to a man who is a hundred years old? Shall Sarah, who is ninety years old, bear a child?”

It seemed impossible that Abraham and Sarah would have a son.

- Abraham would be 100 years old.
- Sarah would be 90 years old.
- But the promise did not depend on them and on their abilities.

It was **God** who made the promise, and He is almighty.

Nothing is too hard for God!

## 🧩 Conclusion

🧵Theme - God never changes; He always does what He says; God is faithful.

God had promised Abraham many things, and God was going to keep **every single** promise He made.

|                                                                                     🤝 God's Promises to Abraham                                                                                     |
| :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------: |
|                                                               ⛰️ **Land** - God told Abram that He would give him the land of Canaan.                                                                |
|                                                       👥 **Huge Family** - God promised that Abram's family would number more than the stars.                                                        |
|                                          ☂️ **Protection** - God told Abram that He would help those who helped his family, and hurt anyone who hurt them.                                           |
|                                                  🛟 **Future Savior** - God said one of Abram's future family members would be the promised Savior.                                                  |
| 💍 **Future Belongings** - God said that Abram's family would go live in another country and that they would be treated badly for 400 years, but after that they would leave with great possessions. |
|                  📍 **Returning Destination** - God said that although they would leave, God would bring them back to Canaan, to that very spot and then the land would be theirs!                   |

God is the same today as He was in the day of Abraham.

He is always the same.

He remembers and keeps His promises to bless those who trust in Him.
