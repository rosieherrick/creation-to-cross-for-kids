# 🧭 [Lesson 28: The Tabernacle](../README.md)

## 🧩 The Holy of Holies

🧵Theme - God is holy and righteous. He demands death as the payment for sin.

God told Moses that the tabernacle must have two rooms.

- The first room, which was entered from the outside, was called the **holy place**. God is holy, perfect, and righteous. God is set apart from sinners.

  It was a room set apart by God to be used only for Him.

  It was only to be used by God's chosen priests when they were serving God.

- The second room, the inner room, was even more important.

  This was called the **Most Holy Place**, or Holy of Holies, that is, the most separate room in God's house.

  This room was set apart just for God except for once a year, when the high priest, whom we will study shortly, was allowed to enter.

  God's presence would be in this inside room.

  This was the special room in the tabernacle where God promised to live in the center of the Israelite camp.
