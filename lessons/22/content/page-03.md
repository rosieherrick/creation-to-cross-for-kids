# 🧭 [Lesson 22: God Protected and Called Moses](../README.md)

## 🧩 Moses' Birth and His Mother's Plan

🧵Theme - Man must have faith in order to please God and be saved.

We are going to look now at the way God worked through the life of one Israelite named Moses.

## 📖 READ – Exodus 2:1-4 ESV

> Now a man from the house of Levi went and took as his wife a Levite woman. The woman conceived and bore a son, and when she saw that he was a fine child, she hid him three months. When she could hide him no longer, she took for him a basket made of bulrushes and daubed it with bitumen and pitch. She put the child in it and placed it among the reeds by the river bank. And his sister stood at a distance to know what would be done to him.

These parents trusted in God to take care of their baby son.

## 🧩 Moses Adopted by Pharaoh's Daughter

🧵Theme - God is faithful; He always does what He says; He never changes.

## 📖 READ – Exodus 2:5 ESV

> Now the daughter of Pharaoh came down to bathe at the river, while her young women walked beside the river. She saw the basket among the reeds and sent her servant woman, and she took it.

God used Moses' big sister and even Pharaoh's daughter to protect Moses!

Then God returned Moses to his own mother to nurse him until he was old enough to be sent back to Pharaoh's daughter.

God planned to use Moses to save the Israelites from slavery.

- God knew that Moses would be safer in Pharaoh's house than anywhere else.

- He also knew that Moses would learn many things there that would be important for him to know for his future work as the leader of his people.

🧵Theme - God is greater than all and more important than all; He is the highest authority.

God is greater than Satan; God is greater than everyone and everything.

- No one and nothing can stop Him from doing whatever He plans.
- We can always trust God, even when things look bad to us.
- He cared for His people Israel, even while they were slaves; and He also cares for you.
