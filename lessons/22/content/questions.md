# 🧭 [Lesson 22: God Protected and Called Moses](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. Why did the king of Egypt make the Israelites slaves?

   _Because they had become more in number than the Egyptians, the king was afraid that the Israelites might join with the Egyptian's enemies to fight against them and take control of Egypt. (Exodus 1:8-11)_

1. Who was guiding the king in his wicked plans?

   _Satan. (1 Peter 5:8)_

1. Why would Satan wish to destroy the Israelites?

   _🛟 Satan knew that God had promised Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob that one of their descendants would be the Savior. (Genesis 12:3)_

1. Why did the Lord allow Pharaoh's daughter to adopt Moses?

   _Because God planned to use Moses to deliver the Israelites from slavery. (Acts 7:20-22)_

1. Could the Israelites save themselves from slavery or could Moses save them?

   _No. (Exodus 2:11-15, 23-25)_

1. Who is the only One who can save a person from Satan's control?

   _God. (2 Timothy 2:25-26)_

1. Why did God decide to save the Israelites from slavery?

   - _👀 Because the Lord saw their suffering and heard their cries. (Exodus 2:24)_

   - _🤍 Because God loved them and planned to reveal His love and mercy to them. (Deuteronomy 4:37)_

   - _🛟 Because the Lord had promised Abraham that He would make Abraham's descendants into a great nation and that, through them, the Savior would come into the world. (Deuteronomy 7:7-8)_

1. Why was the bush whch Moses saw not being burned up, even though it was in flames?

   _🔥 The bush was not burned up because the Almighty God was in the bush. (Exodus 3:2-3)_

1. What does the name of God, "I AM," mean?

   _It means that God doesn't need anything or anyone. He was there before the beginning of all things. Because He is the Creator of all things, everything is under His power, and He is totally independent of all things. (Exodus 3:14, Revelation 1:8)_

1. Did God think that the king of Egypt would release the Israelites immediately when Moses told him what the Lord had said?

   _No, He knew the the king would not free the Israelites until he had to. God knows everything. (Exodus 3:19; 4:21)_
