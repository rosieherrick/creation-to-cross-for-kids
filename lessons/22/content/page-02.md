# 🧭 [Lesson 22: God Protected and Called Moses](../README.md)

## 🧩 The New King's Evil Plan

🧵Theme - Satan fights against God and His will. Satan is a liar and a deceiver. He hates man.

## 📖 READ – Exodus 1:8-11 ESV

> Now there arose a new king over Egypt, who did not know Joseph. And he said to his people, “Behold, the people of Israel are too many and too mighty for us. Come, let us deal shrewdly with them, lest they multiply, and, if war breaks out, they join our enemies and fight against us and escape from the land.” Therefore they set taskmasters over them to afflict them with heavy burdens. They built for Pharaoh store cities, Pithom and Raamses.

Who do you think was guiding Pharaoh in his evil plan?

- Satan was.

Why would Satan want to destroy the nation of Israel?

|                          📢 Through ISRAEL                           |
| :------------------------------------------------------------------: |
|           🛟 **Savior** – God was going to send a Savior.            |
|        📖 **Message** – God gave His Word to the whole world.        |
| ⭐ **Knowledge** – God was preserving the true knowledge of Himself. |

Satan hates God and man.

- Satan knew that God had promised to send the Savior who would destroy Satan and save man from his power.
  - He also knew that God had promised that this Savior would be born through the nation of Israel.

Satan didn't want anyone to be saved from his power.
