## 🧭 Lesson 22: God Protected and Called Moses

### 📖 Scripture – Exodus 1:6-11; 2:1-22; 3:1-20; 4:13-20, 27-31

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** Genesis 12:3, 28:14, Psalm 115:3, 135:5-6, 139, Isaiah 12:6, Acts 7:17-35, 7:22, Romans 8:28, Hebrews 4:13, 11:23-26

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show that God is sovereign, all-powerful, all-knowing, and holy.
- To show Satan's intense desire to ruin God's plan to save men from Satan's bondage.
- To show that no one can hold back the purposes of God; He is faithful to carry them out at His appointed time.
- To show that only God can save man from bondage to Satan. Man is helpless to save himself or to save others.

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To see that God is faithful and sovereign and will carry out His purposes.
- To have an increased awe of God.
- To better understand God's character.

### 🧭 Overview

God's sovereign, loving care is shown as He preserves Israel in slavery and raises up Moses to deliver them. The lesson highlights God's omniscience in knowing Pharaoh's response and Satan's counter-moves to thwart the coming Savior lineage. It also shows humanity's inability to save itself, as Moses attempts to save Israel by his own efforts and fails. God's holiness is seen in the burning bush encounter, where He commissions Moses by declaring, “I AM sent you.”

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – 1 Samuel 2:2 KJV

> There is none holy like the Lord: for there is none besides you; there is no rock like our God.

---

### 💭 Perspective for the Teacher

Our society delights in assessing the weaknesses of men without giving any thought to the power and purpose of God. But the Bible gives us a true picture of God and of men – men who are weak in themselves, but who, by faith, can be mightily used by our gracious, almighty, sovereign Creator, God.

God is really the main character in this wonderful true story. He has graciously presented to us in His Word many examples of the human frailty of His chosen servant, Moses. Yet through this frail vessel, God saved Israel. Ultimately, God provided salvation for all mankind through Israel's promised Savior, Jesus Christ.

The Bible is true. It is not only an exceedingly accurate history of humankind, it is also a living message from our God. We need to remind our students that this dramatic story is history.

As we teach this lesson, we are reminded by God's Word that "I AM" does not change. The God of the burning bush is the same God whom we worship and serve and whose Word we teach! Though He does not speak to us today from a burning bush, He speaks to us through His Word. And He is still using weak vessels to present that Word to others. It is still God Himself who uses His message to transform the hearts and lives of those who will hear and believe.

### 🗣️ On Teaching This Lesson

You are carefully laying a scriptural foundation on which the Gospel will later be presented. Each lesson builds on previous lessons. So be sure to cover each point carefully. DON'T COMPLICATE THE MESSAGE! Carefully follow the outline. Emphasize the doctrinal 🧵Theme given.
