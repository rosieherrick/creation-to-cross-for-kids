# 🧭 [Lesson 11: God's Promise and Curse](../README.md)

## 🛟 The Promised Savior

Once Adam and Eve disobeyed God and followed the advice of Satan, they were separated from God and controlled by Satan.

- They were no longer the children of God.
- They were Satan's children.

Satan had become the god of this world.

- At this point, Satan probably thought that he had beaten God and that he would now have complete control of the world and all people.
- This was what Satan wanted.
  But no one can win against God.

- God is the almighty Creator.

God promised that He would send a **Savior** who would overcome Satan and deliver mankind from his power.

What is a Savior?

🛟 Savior is someone who rescues or saves us.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 3:15 NIV

> And I will put enmity between you and the woman, and between your offspring and hers; **he will crush your head, and you will strike his heel.**

God planned that the promised Savior would be the child of a woman.

When a head is crushed, we know that that body cannot survive.

The one who's heel is crushed does survive, even though he is wounded.

- Explain:

  _The Savior would fight against Satan, and the Savior would win. Satan would fight against the Savior and would wound Him, but Satan would not be able to win. The promised Savior would destroy Satan and deliver mankind from Satan's power so man would be restored in friendship with God._

🧵Theme - God is loving, merciful, and gracious.

## 📖 READ – Psalm 145:8 ESV

> The LORD is gracious and merciful, slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love.

🎁 **Grace** is God's kindness to undeserving sinners.

🤍 **Mercy** is not getting the punishment that our sin deserves.

- Explain:

  _God always punishes sin but He also has great love for Adam and Eve. God promised to make a special way that by sending the Savior, Adam and Eve could be saved from the punishment their sin deserved._

  _He could have left Adam and Eve to die and go to everlasting punishment. That is what they deserved. But God was promising to send a Savior who would deliver them and all mankind from Satan's control and bring them back into a friendship with God._
