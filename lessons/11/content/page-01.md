# 🧭 [Lesson 11: God's Promise and Curse](../README.md)

## 🧩 Introduction

We previously let off at the point that Adam and Eve were hiding behind some bushes and had just admitted to disobeying God. Let's see how God responds to their sin.

Keep in mind:

- God had created them – in His own image and for His glory.
- They belong to God.
- He loved them.
- He had given them everything they could ever need.
- He had warned them of what would happen if they disobeyed Him,
- Even after all that, they had chosen to disobey Him.

## 🧩 God Called Adam

🧵Theme - God communicates with us.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 3:9 ESV

> "But the Lord God called to the man and said to him, 'Where are you?'"

Because Adam and Eve didn't come to talk with God, God called out “Adam, where are you?”

- Why did God call out?

  Didn't He know where they were hiding?

  Wasn't He there beside them among the trees?

  Couldn't He see them all the time?

- Yes, God could See Adam and Eve where they were hiding.
- Why then did He call Adam?

  God called Adam because, although God intended to punish Adam and Eve for their sin, He still **loved** them.

  God was giving them the chance **to agree with Him that they were wrong** in believing Satan instead of Him.

God has not changed; He still calls today even though we cannot hear His voice like Adam did.

- How does God call to us today?

  God calls us through all the things we can see around us.

  - 🔎 Illustrate:

    _Every day, God's voice is saying to us, “Listen to Me. I am the Creator of all things. I am the true God. I know everything. I am almighty. Look at all the things I have created for you. I love you. Look at all the food I have made for you. I love you. Look at the life I have given to you. I love you. Listen to Me seek Me.”_

- Every time we see the things God has created, He wants us to think of Him.

- But God is not only speaking to us through the things He has made; He is also calling us through His Word.

- The Bible is God's message to each one of us.

God really wants each one of us to know Him!
