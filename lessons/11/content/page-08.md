# 🧭 [Lesson 11: God's Promise and Curse](../README.md)

## 🧩 Adam and Eve – Parents of All People

## 📖 READ – Genesis 3:20 ESV

> The man called his wife's name Eve, because she was the mother of all living.

Even though people have different colors of skin, we all originally came from the same parents, Adam and Eve.

- Eve is the first woman and the mother of every human being.

- Adam is the father of us all.

- Adam and Eve's sin has affected all of us, ruining our relationship with God and the perfect earth that God had made for us.

**Because of Adam's sin, he would die, and all of his children would die also.**

Romans 5:12 says that, "...just as sin came into the world through **one man**, and death through sin, and so **death spread to all men...**"

All people in every country die because we are all descendants of Adam.

## 🧩 Conclusion

Adam and Eve knew what God had said and they chose to disobey God anyway.

There were terrible consequences for their sin.

- We live with those consequences every day.

But God promised to send a Savior – someone to rescue man from Satan, sin, and death.
