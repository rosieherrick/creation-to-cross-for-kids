# 🧭 [Lesson 11: God's Promise and Curse](../README.md)

## 🧩 Adam and Eve Tried to Hide from God

🧵Theme - God knows everything.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 3:10 ESV

> "And he said, 'I heard the sound of you in the garden, and I was afraid, because I was naked, and I hid myself.'"

Adam and Eve were afraid of God because they had disobeyed Him.

- 🔎 Compare:

_Have you ever hid when you did something wrong? Why did you hide? You hid because you were ashamed and because you were afraid that you would be punished! Adam and Eve were ashamed, and they were afraid too._

Adam and Eve had reason to be afraid.

- They knew what they had done.
- And they knew what God had told them would happen if they disobey Him.
