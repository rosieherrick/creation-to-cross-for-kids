## 🧭 Lesson 11: God's Promise and Curse

### 📖 Scripture – Genesis 3:9-20

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** Isaiah 7:14; 45:18, Psalm 19:1-3, John 12:31; 17:3, Luke 1:27, Romans 1:16-17; 5:12-21; 8:20-22, 1 Corinthians 15:21-22, 2 Corinthians 4:4, Ephesians 2:1-2, Philippians 3:10, 2 Peter 3:9

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show that God knows everything and will punish all sin.
- To show that God will not allow Satan to have permanent victory in leading man to sin, but that God would send a Savior who will overcome Satan and save mankind from Satan's power.
- To show the horrible, lasting results of sin.
- To show that man can do nothing to save himself from sin.

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To realize that no one can get away with sin.
- To realize that God has made a way to overcome Satan.
- To realize that all the problems in the world have come as a result of sin.

### 🧭 Overview

This lesson tells the Bible story of how Satan deceived Eve and how Adam and Eve chose to disobey God. It shows how their sin separated them from God, while also revealing God's omniscience, holiness, love, and grace as He saw them, called to them, judged the serpent and their sin, and promised to send a Savior.

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – Proverbs 3:6 NKJV

> In all your ways acknowledge Him, And He shall direct your paths.

---

### 💭 Perspective for the Teacher

You might say that this lesson explains the “mess we are in”. We often hear men and women blaming God for their problems and for the problems of the world around them. But Genesis 3 states the truth of the matter very clearly: man's own sin is the cause of man's problems. Children need to know this truth.

Remember that sinners do not have reason to fear God's wrath, so in your teaching do not diminish the awful consequences of sin. The introduction of God's grace and promise to send a Savior is only appreciated in light of 1. man's original position of friendship with his loving Creator and owner, God, 2. the awfulness of man's rebellion and disobedience to God, and 3. the horrible and sure consequences of that sin.

Many people in our society feel that they deserve forgiveness. It is common for children to learn this idea very early in life. But God is holy and righteous and has decreed that the punishment for sin is death. His provision of a Savior is the supreme act of His grace – totally undeserved favor.

### 🗣️ On Teaching This Lesson

You are carefully laying a scriptural foundation on which the Gospel will later be presented. Each lesson builds on previous lessons. So be sure to cover each point carefully. DON'T COMPLICATE THE MESSAGE! Carefully follow the outline, emphasizing the doctrinal 🧵Theme given.
