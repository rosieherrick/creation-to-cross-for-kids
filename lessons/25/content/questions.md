# 🧭 [Lesson 25: God Provided for Israel in the Desert](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. What new problem did the Israelites face after God saved them from the Egyptian army at the Red Sea and began their journey through the wilderness?

   _They didn't have any food. (Exodus 16:3)_

1. Did the Israelites remember all the great thing God had done, and did they trust in Him?

   _No, they complained and blamed Moses and Aaron. (Exodus 16:2)_

1. Did Israel deserve to be given food by God?

   _No. (Deuteronomy 9:6)_

1. Is there any person who deserves God's love or deserves to be saved from everlasting punishment?

   _No, we all deserve to be punished by God. (Romans 3:23)_

1. What did God tell Moses to do in order to provide water?

   _He had to hit the rock with his stick. (Exodus 17:5,6)_

1. When God saved the Israelites, did He allow them to do things the way they wanted to do it?

   _No, they had to do it God's way. (Acts 4:12)_

1. Why did God protect and provide for Israel?

   - _🤍 Because He is loving and merciful. (Psalm 136:10-15)_
   - _🛟 Because one of their descendants was to be the Savior. (Isaiah 9:6-7)_
   - _🤝 Because of His promise to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. (Genesis 12:3, Genesis 26:3-4, Genesis 28:14)_
   - _📖 Because He was giving His Word through Israel to the whole world. (Isaiah 49:6)_

1. Was there any way the Israelites could provide food or water for themselves?

   _No. (Deuteronomy 8:3)_

1. What should they have done?

   _🙏 They should have called on the Lord and trusted Him to provide for them. (Psalm 50:15)_
