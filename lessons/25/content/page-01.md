# 🧭 [Lesson 25: God Provided for Israel in the Desert](../README.md)

## 🧩 Introduction

In the Bible, a miracle is a special act of God where He does something that would not happen normally.

We've learned about a miracle God did for the Israelites at the Red Sea, saving them from the Egyptian army. Now we will learn about more miracles God did to keep them alive in the desert.

## 🧩 God Keeping His Promises

God made many promises to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

God always keeps every one of His promises.

God had already given Abraham a son, whose descendants grew into a great family—the nation of Israel. He had cared for Israel in Egypt, delivered them from slavery with great possessions, just as He said, and was now leading them toward the promised land of Canaan.

All the Israelites had seen God's awesome power in the plagues He sent against Egypt, and in how He protected Israel and destroyed the Egyptian army at the Red Sea.

The Israelites didn't even need to know which way to go. God had provided a cloud for them to follow, and He would show them the way. All they had to do was follow and trust God.
