# 🧭 [Lesson 25: God Provided for Israel in the Desert](../README.md)

## 🧩 God Provided Israelites with Water

🧵Theme - God is all-powerful.

## 📖 READ – Exodus 17:6b

> So Moses did this, while the leaders of the Israelites watched him.

In obedience to the Lord, Moses hit the rock.

Immediately, a great stream, or rather a flood, of water flowed out.

There was enough water for all of the Israelite people and their animals.

- 🔎 Consider:

  _We do not know the exact number of Israelites there were at this time because only the men over twenty years old were counted. But we can estimate that there were perhaps two-and-a-half million people. There was enough water for all of them and their animals! Psalm 105:41 NKJV says _"He opened the rock, and water gushed out; It ran in the dry places like a river."_ God is the great Creator. Nothing is too hard for God!

🧵Theme - God is loving, merciful, and gracious.

God gave the Israelites food and water even though they didn't deserve anything from God.

- They didn't work for this food and water.
- God didn't ask for anything in return.
- He gave them food and water because He is loving and merciful.
- We cannot gain acceptance with God by the things that we do.
- God's forgiveness to sinners is a gift (🎁 **grace**) that we don't deserve.

🧵Theme - God is faithful; He always does what He says; He never changes.

God faithfully protected the Israelites and provided all they needed.

- God even provided by ensuring their clothes never wore out. As Nehemiah 9:21 says, "Their clothes did not wear out, and their feet did not swell."
- God had promised that He would bless Abraham's descendants.
- God always does whatever He says.
