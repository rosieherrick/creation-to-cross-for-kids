# 🧭 [Lesson 25: God Provided for Israel in the Desert](../README.md)

## 🧩 Conclusion

The story of how God provided manna and water for the Israelites is retold many times in the Old Testament and the New Testament.

This is true history, it really happened.

These are historically verified miracles, **God's miracles**.

- They show us His faithfulness to keep His promises.
- They show us His great love for His people.
- They show us His mighty power over all creation.

God is beyond our understanding and so are His miracles!

We can know that these things really happened, because God said so in His Word!
