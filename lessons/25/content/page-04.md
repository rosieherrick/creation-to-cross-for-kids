# 🧭 [Lesson 25: God Provided for Israel in the Desert](../README.md)

## 🧩 God Gave Israel Food

🧵Theme - God is loving, merciful, and gracious.

## 📖 READ – Exodus 16:13-15 ESV

> In the evening quail came up and covered the camp, and in the morning dew lay around the camp. And when the dew had gone up, there was on the face of the wilderness a fine, flake-like thing, fine as frost on the ground. When the people of Israel saw it, they said to one another, “What is it?” For they did not know what it was. And Moses said to them, “It is the bread that the Lord has given you to eat.

God helps the helpless; He delivers those who have no way of escape.

🧵Theme - God is faithful; He always does what He says; He never changes.

## 📖 READ – Exodus 16:35 ESV

> The people of Israel ate the manna forty years, till they came to a habitable land. They ate the manna till they came to the border of the land of Canaan.

The Lord faithfully provided manna for the Israelites from this time forward and throughout the time they were in the wilderness.

- He never failed them.
- God is faithful.
- He can be trusted to do everything He has said in His Word.
