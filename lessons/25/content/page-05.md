# 🧭 [Lesson 25: God Provided for Israel in the Desert](../README.md)

## 🧩 Israel Grumbled and Complained

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

Although the Israelites were thankful for the Lord's provision of food, they soon forgot about His great power, and they began to complain again.

- Does that sound familiar to you?
- Listen to what happened this time.

## 📖 READ – Exodus 17:1-4 ESV

> All the congregation of the people of Israel moved on from the wilderness of Sin by stages, according to the commandment of the Lord, and camped at Rephidim, but there was no water for the people to drink. Therefore the people quarreled with Moses and said, “Give us water to drink.” And Moses said to them, “Why do you quarrel with me? Why do you test the Lord?” But the people thirsted there for water, and the people grumbled against Moses and said, “Why did you bring us up out of Egypt, to kill us and our children and our livestock with thirst?” So Moses cried to the Lord, “What shall I do with this people? They are almost ready to stone me.”

- 🔎 Consider:

  _How long can a man live without water? Only a few days. The Israelites were afraid they were all going to die there in the desert. Can you imagine the burden Moses felt. He had no way of supplying water for all these people. Only God could help them._

  _We know that God can do all things. But where could God get water in that desert? How much water would be needed for that many people and all their animals too?_

  _Why should God provide for these grumbling people? The people didn't deserve God's help._
