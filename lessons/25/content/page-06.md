# 🧭 [Lesson 25: God Provided for Israel in the Desert](../README.md)

## 🧩 God Told Moses What To Do

🧵Theme - God is loving, merciful, and gracious.

God didn't let any of the Israelites die of thirst.

He gave them water because He is loving, merciful, and gracious.

🎁 God was **gracious**, giving the Israelites something they didn't deserve.

🧵Theme - God communicates with man.

## 📖 READ – Exodus 17:5,6a EASY

> The Lord said to Moses, ‘Take some of the leaders of the Israelites with you and go in front of the people. Take your stick in your hand. Take the stick that you used to hit the Nile river. Now go! I will stand there, in front of you, by the rock at Sinai. Hit the rock with your stick, and water will come out of it. Then the people can drink.'

God told Moses to hit the rock with his stick.

God promised that He would give water out of the rock.

- 🔎 Consider:

  _How much more sense it would have made to us if God had told them to dig a hole in the sand or make a well. Then we could have "explained" how He provided the water. But this would be a historically verified miracle that God was going to do. He wanted the Israelites (and us) to know that only God was able to save them from dying in the desert._

🧵Theme - Man can come to God only according to God's will and plan.

Moses had to do it God's way.

Every time they had an impossible situation, God told them exactly what they must do.
