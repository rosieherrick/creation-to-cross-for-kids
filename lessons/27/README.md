## 🧭 Lesson 27: God Gave the Ten Commandments

### 📖 Scripture – Exodus 20:3-17

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** Matthew 22:36-38; John 8:44; Romans 1:18-23; Ephesians 6:1-4

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show God's holiness, sovereignty, and righteousness.
- To show man's absolute inability to keep the Law.
- To show that only God can make a way for man to be saved.

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To see the fallacy of the popular belief that the way to be saved is by keeping the Ten Commandments.
- To see that they have broken God's laws and are truly deserving of death.
- To see that they cannot save themselves.

### 🧭 Overview

God's sovereignty is emphasized throughout this lesson. Because each commandment was given by Him, it represents His righteous standard. The lesson contrasts God's holiness with humanity's sinfulness and inability to keep His perfect Law. Each commandment is read, explained, and applied to everyday life, showing that everyone has broken God's Law. The purpose of studying the Ten Commandments is to bring conviction of sin, help students recognize that they cannot save themselves through good works, and reveal their need for a Savior.

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – James 2:10 ESV

> For whoever keeps the whole law but fails in one point has become guilty of all of it.

---

### 💭 Perspective for the Teacher

Our society's view of the Ten Commandments reflects a progressive departure from biblical truth.

By rejecting God's Word and refusing to acknowledge Him as the sovereign Creator, people deny their accountability to Him and disregard what Scripture teaches about the consequences and punishment of sin. As a result, many rush headlong into deeper depravity. Our society has followed the downward progression described in Romans 1:21–32.

Yet many people still have some awareness of right and wrong and recognize their need for a moral or “religious” standard. However, they do not want to acknowledge God as their sovereign Creator, nor do they want to believe that He is holy and righteous, a God who judges sin. Instead, they seek just enough religion to ease their consciences and make themselves feel better.

Consequently, the Ten Commandments are often viewed merely as rules for good conduct or as a salve for the conscience. Sadly, many churches have contributed to this misunderstanding by failing to teach Scripture carefully and thoroughly.

The Bible, however, presents the Ten Commandments as an impossibly perfect standard given by the holy, righteous, and sovereign Creator. They reveal humanity's utter inability to obey God completely or save itself from the judgment and eternal consequences of sin.

### 🗣️ On Teaching This Lesson

You are carefully laying a scriptural foundation on which the Gospel will later be presented. Each lesson builds on previous lessons. So be sure to cover each point carefully. DON'T COMPLICATE THE MESSAGE! Follow the outline carefully and emphasize the doctrinal 🧵Theme given.
