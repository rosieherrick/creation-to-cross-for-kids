# 🧭 [Lesson 27: God Gave the Ten Commandments](../README.md)

## 🧩 The Eighth Commandment

🧵Theme - God is greater than all and more important than all; He is the highest authority.

## 📖 READ – Exodus 20:15 ESV

> You shall not steal.

In the eighth commandment, God said they must never take anything belonging to someone else.

- God is the One who gives each person the right to own and to keep his own property.

- Acts 17:25 says that God _"giveth to all life, and breath, and all things."_

- If a person takes something which belongs to someone else, then he has sinned against God.

- 🔎 Consider:

  _Even if a thief gives back the thing he stole or pays for the thing which he stole, that will still not satisfy God for the sin of stealing. The punishment for stealing is everlasting separation from God._

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

Even if a person wants to or plans to steal something but doesn't do it because he is afraid of being caught, he is still guilty of stealing before God.

God will never forgive sin until it is paid for in full.
