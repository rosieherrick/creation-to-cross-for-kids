# 🧭 [Lesson 27: God Gave the Ten Commandments](../README.md)

## 🧩 The Third Commandment

🧵Theme - God is greater than all and more important than all; He is the highest authority.

## 📖 READ – Exodus 20:7 EASY

> You must not speak the name of the Lord your God in a wrong way. The Lord will punish anyone who speaks his name in a wrong way.

In the third commandment, God told the Israelites that they must always respect God and His name.

They must believe that He is their Creator and rightful owner and ruler, and never speak or use His Name in a careless way.

- God is the almighty Creator and ruler of everyone and everything.

- Everyone should realize that God has the power to take our lives and to destroy us all in everlasting punishment.

- This is what we mean when we talk about "fearing God".

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

- 🔎 Consider:

  _It is wrong to say evil things about people. We need to speak with respect. You should show respect for your parents, teachers, and others._

  _Today, many people say disrespectful words against others and gain attention by trying to say even more hurtful and negative things against our leaders. People will say whatever they want against parents, teachers, and government officials. This is not what God wants._

  _To insult another human being is bad, but how much more evil it is when we have shown disrespect for God or used His Name in a careless way._

All who have not given God the respect He deserves are condemned.
