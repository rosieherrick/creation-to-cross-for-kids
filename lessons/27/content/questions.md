# 🧭 [Lesson 27: God Gave the Ten Commandments](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. Does God care if people worship or serve anything or anyone else besides Him?

   _Yes, that is sin against God. God will not share His position as God with anyone or anything else. (Exodus 20:3-5)_

1. Is it right to make an image of something or worship anything or anyone except God?

   _No, this, too, is sin against God. We do not know what God looks like, so we must not try to make anything that we think looks like Him or worship any creation of His or anything we have made. (Exodus 20:4,5)_

1. Does God care what we think about Him and what we say about Him?

   _Yes, God will punish all those who do not always honor Him in their thoughts and by their words. It is sin to use God's name in any way that is not honoring to Him. (Exodus 20:7)_

1. Why did God command the Israelites to rest on the seventh day of the week?

   - _Because God created all things in six days and rested on the seventh day. (Exodus 20:8-11)_

   - _Because God wanted them to always remember that He is the Creator and everything we have comes from Him. (Exodus 20:8-11)_

1. Does God care if children disobey or are disrespectful to their parents?

   _Yes, God will punish all those who do not obey and respect their parents. (Exodus 20:12)_

1. Why does God punish all murderers?

   _Because God gives life to all people. He is the only One who has the authority to take a person's life. (Genesis 9:5–6)_

1. What does God say about a person who hates someone else?

   _God says that person is a murderer. (Matthew 5:21–22)_

1. What is God's attitude toward adultery?

   _Adultery is sin. God condemns all sexual relationships except between a man and his wife. (Hebrews 13:4)_

1. Why will God punish all who steal?

   _Because God gives people the right to own things; therefore, stealing is a sin against God. (Exodus 20:15, Ephesians 4:28)_

1. Are people guilty of stealing if they plan to steal but then become afraid and do not do it?

   _Yes, they are guilty because God saw that they had planned in their hearts to steal. (Micah 2:1)_

1. What is God's command about telling lies?

   _God said that lying to anyone is sin against God because He always speaks the truth and hates lies. (Exodus 20:16, Proverbs 6:16-17)_

1. Does God care if we are envious of other people and want what they have?

   _Yes, that is also sin against God. That was Satan's sin. He was envious of God and wanted His position. (Exodus 20:17)_

1. Can anyone obey the Ten Commandments?

   _No. All people have been born outside the garden of Eden and are descendants of Adam. All of us continually sin against God. (Romans 3:20,23; James 2:10)_
