# 🧭 [Lesson 27: God Gave the Ten Commandments](../README.md)

## 🧩 Introduction

The Israelites, like us, were born sinners, unable to please God.

It is amazing that, as sinful as we are, we still think that we are able to obey God.

- Israel was sure they could keep their part of God's agreement with them.

- We are the same today.

Let's look in the Bible at God's Ten Commandments.

## 🧩 The First Commandment

🧵Theme - God is greater than all and more important than all; He is the highest authority.

🧵Theme - God is holy and righteous. He demands death as the payment for sin.

## 📖 READ – Exodus 20:3 ESV

> You shall have no other gods before me.

In the first commandment, God said that He and only He should be everyone's God.

This means that people should worship only Him.

- The word **"worship"** comes from the word **"worth"**.

- Only God is completely worthy.

He must be looked up to as the Highest, the greatest of all.

- God will not allow anyone or anything else to share His place as God.

- People must not depend on anyone or anything except Him for everything they need in this life or the life after death.

- They must not worship anyone or anything else.

- They must give God the highest place in their thoughts and give Him praise and thanks for everything.

Isaiah 45:5 says, _"I am the LORD, and there is none else, there is no God beside me..."_

Nothing and no one else should take God's place in our lives.

- If a person puts anyone else in God's place, that is rebellion and sin against God.

- Even if we say with our lips that God is our ruler, yet we do not give Him first place in our hearts **all of the time**, we have still broken this commandment.

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

- 🔎 Consider:

  _Adam deliberately turned away from giving God first place in his life. Adam went his own way instead of God's way. All the descendants of Adam have gone their own way. **No one has allowed God to rule every part of his life.**_

Our own ancestors disobeyed this first command of God. They put idols and spirits in God's place, and Satan took God's place in their lives.

- 🔎 Consider:

  _Today, many people place themselves in God's position. They believe they can control their own future and give humanity credit for everything that is accomplished, leaving no place for God in their thinking._

  _This is much like the sin of Satan and his followers when they rebelled against God. God removed them forever from their positions of service before God in Heaven. Satan and all his followers will spend eternity in the Lake of Fire._

  _What about you? Perhaps you simply want to make your own decisions. Whenever you do what you want without considering what God wants, you have broken the first commandment._

  _Have you ever given yourself or someone else credit for an accomplishment without acknowledging God's role in it? If so, you have broken this commandment._

  _Have sports, popularity, friendships, the latest fashions, or the news ever become more important to you than God? If so, you have broken the first commandment of God's Law._

None of us has continually put God first in everything.

The punishment for disobeying this first command is **separation** from God.

We are **all guilty of breaking it**.
