# 🧭 [Lesson 27: God Gave the Ten Commandments](../README.md)

## 🧩 The Seventh Commandment

🧵Theme - God is greater than all and more important than all; He is the highest authority.

## 📖 READ – Exodus 20:14 ESV

> You shall not commit adultery.

In the seventh commandment, God says that it is sin to have any sexual relationship with anyone except one's own wife or husband.

The punishment for all sexual sin is everlasting separation from God.

- 🔎 Consider:

  _After God created Adam, He gave Eve to him to be his wife. God said that because they were married, they were no longer two people before Him. They were now like one person. Husband and wife belong to one another, and they must live only with one another._

  _Many people today will tell you that this commandment is old-fashioned and out-of-date. But God does not change. Sin is still sin. God has not changed His mind about adultery._

Adultery and all types of sexual sins are also committed in people's minds.

## 📖 READ – Matthew 5:27,28 ESV

> You have heard that it was said, ‘You shall not commit adultery.' But I say to you that everyone who looks at a woman with lustful intent has already committed adultery with her in his heart.

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

- God knows every thought.

- Every sin will be judged, even if it was only committed in the mind.
