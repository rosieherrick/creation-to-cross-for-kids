# 🧭 [Lesson 27: God Gave the Ten Commandments](../README.md)

## 🧩 The Second Commandment

🧵Theme - God is greater than all and more important than all; He is the highest authority.

## 📖 READ – Exodus 20:4-6 EASY

> You must not make any idol for yourself. Do not make a false god in the shape of anything that is in the sky above. Do not make one in the shape of anything on the earth or in the sea. You must not bend down your head to any idol or worship it. I am the Lord your God and I want you to belong to me alone. I will punish children because of the bad things that their fathers have done. I will also punish their children and their grandchildren too. That is how I will punish everyone who turns against me. But I will truly love all those who love me. They are the people who obey my laws. I will continue to love them and their families for ever.

God gave this second commandment to the Israelites so they would always remember that He is greater than all the things He has created and He alone is the highest authority.

- 🔎 Consider:

  _God **IS** greater than all. No part of God's creation, no man-made object is worthy of worship. God alone is to be worshiped._

  _God is spirit. He does not have a material body. No one knows what God looks like, so God said that we should never try to make anything that we think resembles Him. The only way we can know what God is like is to let Him teach us through this book, the Bible. Anything different from what the Bible says is a lie of Satan._

  _We are learning the truth about God from the Bible._

  _All over the world, Satan has led people away from the true knowledge of God. They no longer know the true and living God. Satan has given other things in the place of God. People have followed their own ideas and other people's thoughts about what they think God must be like._

  _People worship man-made representations of things they think will bring them favor with God. In their minds, they think that these man-made things have powers that really belong only to God._

All who worship man-made things that they think represent God have broken this command of God and are condemned.

Again, in Isaiah 42:8, God tells us, _"I am the LORD: that is my name: and my glory will I not give to another, neither my praise to graven images."_
