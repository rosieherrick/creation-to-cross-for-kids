# 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 [Creation to the Cross - for Kids](../../README.md)

## 🧭 Lesson 15: God Remembered Noah in the Ark and Scattered at Tower of Babel

### 📖 Scripture – Genesis 8:1-4, 14-17; 9:1, 2, 12-15; 11:1-9

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** Psalms 19:1–3; 139; Jeremiah 50:35–40; Ezekiel 18:4; Romans 1:18–32

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show that God will not tolerate any attempt to take over His authority.
- To show where all the languages of the world came from.
- To show God's sovereignty in all the affairs of men.

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To understand that God is supreme and sovereign.
- To realize that every part of mankind's history is rooted in God and His acts.
- To see that the Bible presents a factual history of man.

### 🧭 Overview

This lesson wraps up the account of Noah and his family's salvation and their repopulation of the earth after the flood, then moves to the story of the tower of Babel. It highlights God's omniscience and sovereign control over history, especially as seen in how He scattered the rebellious builders and brought about the confusion of languages.

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – 1 Peter 1:25 ESV

> But the word of the Lord endures forever.

---

### 💭 Perspective for the Teacher

Babylon was rebuilt sometime after its first builders had been scattered by God. But it eventually became so thoroughly evil that God caused it to become a total ruin, with no inhabitants even to this day.

Babylon is a real place with a message for our times from our great sovereign God. God will not allow men to exalt themselves and live in continued rebellion against Him. He judges sin and moves with power to achieve all that He has purposed to do.

God is the sovereign controller of the affairs of men. Any attempts to explain the flow of history must include our Creator and sovereign God. In recent years His name has been “written out” of secular history books by whose who follow humanistic thinking. But His name can never be erased from the truth. The children need to know the God of all his story – the God of the Bible.

### 🗣️ On Teaching This Lesson

You are carefully laying a scriptural foundation on which the Gospel will later be presented. Each lesson builds on previous lessons. So be sure to cover each point carefully. DON'T COMPLICATE THE MESSAGE! Carefully follow the outline, emphasizing the doctrinal 🧵Theme given.
