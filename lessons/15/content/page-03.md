# 🧭 [Lesson 15: God Remembered Noah in the Ark and Scattered at Tower of Babel](../README.md)

## 🧩 The Rebellious, Proud Attitude of the People

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 11:1-4 ESV

> Now **the whole earth had one language** and the same words. And as people migrated from the east, **they found a plain** in the land of Shinar and **settled there**. And they said to one another, “Come, let us make bricks, and burn them thoroughly.” And they had brick for stone, and bitumen for mortar. Then they said, “Come, let us build ourselves a city and a tower with its top in the heavens, and let us make a name for ourselves, **lest we be dispersed over the face of the whole earth**.”

Many years had passed since God saved Noah and his family from the great flood.

Once again, many people lived on the earth.

Most of Noah's family soon forgot about God's terrible judgment on the earth.

They deliberately ignored God.

- They refused to consider His will for them.
- They did not want to know and believe on the Lord like Abel, Seth, and Noah did.
- They wanted to be great and important.

Remember, God had instructed Noah and his family to be fruitful and multiply to fill the earth.

They thought they knew better than God.

- Now they were specifically choosing to not spread out but to try to stay together.
- They thought they were smart.
- They knew so much and would show how much they knew in their building skills. Not only would they build a great city, but a tower right up to heaven!
- The Bible says that instead of worshiping God, and praising Him for His creation, they began to worship the sun, moon, and stars. They were worshiping things God created, rather than the Creator.
