# 🧭 [Lesson 15: God Remembered Noah in the Ark and Scattered at Tower of Babel](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. What sign did God give to show He would never again destroy the earth by a flood?

   _🌈 The rainbow. (Genesis 9:12, 13)_

1. Does God keep his promises?

   _Yes, God is true to His Word. He never lies. He never changes. (Numbers 23:19)_

1. Did the people who were later born from Noah's family worship God?

   _No, they followed their own ideas and worshiped the sun, moon, and stars instead of God, their Creator. (Romans 1:18-32)_

1. What was the reason the people had for wanting to build the tower of Babel?

   _So they would not be scattered all over the world and so they could be important. (Genesis 11:4)_

1. Did God know what they planned to do?

   _👀 Yes, God knows everything. (Genesis 11:5)_

1. What did God do to stop the building of the tower?

   _He gave different languages to different families so that they could no longer understand each other, forcing them to stop building. (Genesis 11:6-9)_

1. What happened after the people stopped building the tower of Babel?

   _The people moved away, spreading all over the earth. (Genesis 11:9)_
