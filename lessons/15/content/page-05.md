# 🧭 [Lesson 15: God Remembered Noah in the Ark and Scattered at Tower of Babel](../README.md)

## 🧩 Conclusion

## 📖 READ – Genesis 11:6a, 7-9 ESV

> And the Lord said, “Behold, they are one people, and they have all one language... Come, let us go down and there confuse their language, so that they may not understand one another's speech.” So the Lord dispersed them from there over the face of all the earth, and they left off building the city. Therefore its name was called Babel, because there the Lord confused the language of all the earth. And from there the Lord dispersed them over the face of all the earth.

- The Lord caused the people to speak different languages.
- Because the people could no longer understand one another, they separated into their different family groups and moved away to different parts of the world.

This was God's original plan – that people love all over the earth, not just in one region.

God is all powerful and able to make His plan to happen, no one can come against God and win.
