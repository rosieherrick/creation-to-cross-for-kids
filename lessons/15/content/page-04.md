# 🧭 [Lesson 15: God Remembered Noah in the Ark and Scattered at Tower of Babel](../README.md)

## 🧩 God Saw Them

🧵Theme - God is everywhere all the time; He knows everything.

Was God interested in these rebellious people?

- Did God see them?
- Did He know their thoughts and what they planned?
- Did He care what they did?

## 📖 READ – Genesis 11:5 ESV

> And the LORD came down to see the city and the tower, which the children of man had built.

Yes, the Lord was interested in them even though they had forgotten Him.

God sees all people everywhere.

Proverbs 15:3 says that _“The eyes of the Lord are in every place keeping watch on the evil and the good.”_

- He is interested in every person.
- He knows all about us, too.

  You and I cannot keep a secret from Him.

  He knows everything we have ever thought, said, or done.

## 🧩 God Scattered Them

🧵Theme - God is greater than all and more important than all; He is the highest authority.

Can anyone fight against God and win?

- When Satan was in Heaven, he tried to against God, but he lost.
- The people of Noah's day lived in rebellion against God and lost.
- What do you think will happen this time? Will the people win?

No one can fight against God and win. He is greater than all.
