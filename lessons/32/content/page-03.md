# 🧭 [Lesson 32: God Foretold the Birth of John and Jesus](../README.md)

## 🧩 God Promised Mary a Son

🧵Theme - God is faithful; He always does what He says; He never changes.

It was now God's time to fulfill all of His promises regarding the Savior.

Listen to what God says.

🧵Theme - God communicates with man.

## 📖 READ – Luke 1:26-31 ESV

> In the sixth month the angel Gabriel was sent from God to a city of Galilee named Nazareth, to a virgin betrothed to a man whose name was Joseph, of the house of David. And the virgin's name was Mary. And he came to her and said, “Greetings, O favored one, the Lord is with you!” But she was greatly troubled at the saying, and tried to discern what sort of greeting this might be. And the angel said to her, “Do not be afraid, Mary, for you have found favor with God. And behold, you will conceive in your womb and bear a son, and you shall call his name Jesus.

God sent an angel to tell a virgin named Mary that God had chosen her to be the mother of the Savior.

Mary was just an ordinary young woman who, although she was a sinner, trusted in God to send the Savior.

🧵Theme - God is greater than all and more important than all; He is the highest authority.

God chose Mary to be the mother of the Savior because God does whatever He wants to do.

God is sovereign, He is the highest authority.

🧵Theme - God is faithful; He always does what He says; He never changes.

🧵Theme - God is loving, merciful, and gracious.

Mary's son was to be the promised Savior.

🛟 **Savior** is someone who rescues or saves.

His name was to be Jesus, which means Savior.

God had never forgotten His promise to send the Savior.

God loved the whole world, and He wanted sinners to be saved from the punishment they deserved.
