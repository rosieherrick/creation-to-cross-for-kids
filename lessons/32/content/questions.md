# 🧭 [Lesson 32: God Foretold the Birth of John and Jesus](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. What name was Zacharias to give to his son?

   _John. (Luke 1:13)_

1. What was John's work to be?

   _John's work would be to prepare the Jews to receive and trust in the Savior. (Luke 1:17)_

1. Mary's son would be a descendant of what great king?

   _King David. (Luke 1:32)_

1. Why did the Savior have to be born of the line of David?

   _Because God had promised David that the Savior would be one of his descendants. (2 Samuel 7:12, 16)_

1. What was Mary's child to be called?

   _Jesus. (Luke 1:31)_

1. What does the name Jesus mean?

   _Jesus means Savior. (Matthew 1:21)_

1. Was Jesus to be more than just a man?

   _Yes, Jesus was to be God the Son whom God planned should come down from Heaven to be born as a human baby. (Luke 1:32,35)_

1. How could Jesus be given life without a human father?

   _God is all-powerful. He can do anything He wishes to do. (Luke 1:37)_

1. Was Jesus to be the promised Savior?

   _Yes, Jesus was to be 🛟 the Savior who God first promised in the Garden of Eden. (Luke 1:31-33)_

1. Who is the author of the New Testament?

   _God. (2 Timothy 3:16)_
