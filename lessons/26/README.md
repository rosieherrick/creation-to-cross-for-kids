## 🧭 Lesson 26: Preparation for the Ten Commandments

### 📖 Scripture – Exodus 19:1–13, 16–20; 20:1, 2

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** Exodus 3:1–2; Isaiah 59:1–2; Matthew 25:41; Romans 8:23; James 1:22–25; Hebrews 12:18–21, 25–29; 2 Peter 3:3–12; Revelation 20:15

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show God's holiness, sovereignty, righteousness, and wrath against sin.
- To show man's absolute inability to keep the Law.
- To show that man cannot be saved by anything he does.
- To show that only God can make a way for man to be saved.

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To see the fallacy of the popular belief that the way to be saved is by keeping the Ten Commandments.
- To see that all people are truly deserving of death and eternal punishment because we have broken God's Law.

### 🧭 Overview

This lesson sets the stage for the giving of the Ten Commandments. It emphasizes God's righteousness and holiness, as well as His fearful wrath and certain judgment against all sin. God's covenant with Israel depended on the people's obedience, yet they did not understand that they were incapable of obeying Him perfectly. The boundaries established around Mount Sinai for both the people and the animals emphasize God's holiness, humanity's sinfulness, and the certainty of judgment for anyone who approaches God in sin. The Ten Commandments demonstrate that God requires perfect obedience from every person. Although the Law does not reveal the way of grace, it serves an important purpose: it shows us that salvation cannot be achieved through our own good works. In this way, God prepares sinners to receive the gift of salvation made possible through Christ's sacrificial death on their behalf.

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – Exodus 20:2 NIV

> I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of Egypt, out of the land of slavery.

### 💭 Perspective for the Teacher

Jesus Christ told His disciples that the Holy Spirit would convict the world of _"sin, righteousness, and judgment"_ (John 16:8).

The truth of God's holiness, righteousness, and wrath is not a popular topic. Many people today who even profess to be Christians do not seem to realize that God is the same God we see in this passage in Exodus.

Much emphasis has been placed on God's love; little emphasis has been placed on the fact that we receive that love only as objects of His grace. God's amazing grace has at times been presented without stressing the fact that grace is God's undeserved kindness toward otherwise hopelessly lost sinners.

God still hates sin so much that He will punish forever in the Lake of Fire all who refuse His plan of salvation.

We must not assume that our students understand the words "holy" and "righteousness." They may have heard those words in religious ceremonies without ever realizing that God is not only pure and sinless, but also a God of wrath who hates and punishes all sin.

As we teach this lesson, we can know that God's Word is powerful to penetrate man's false ideas and Satan's deception. Our students, like the people of Israel, need to know that sinners cannot stand in the presence of a holy, righteous God.

### 🗣️ On Teaching This Lesson

You are carefully laying a scriptural foundation on which the Gospel will later be presented. Each lesson builds on previous lessons. So be sure to cover each point carefully. DON'T COMPLICATE THE MESSAGE! Carefully follow the outline. Emphasize the doctrinal 🧵Theme given.
