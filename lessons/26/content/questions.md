# 🧭 [Lesson 26: Preparation for the Ten Commandments](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. What did God promise Moses when Moses saw the burning bush at Mount Sinai?

   _God promised that He would bring Moses back safely to the same mountain. (Exodus 3:12)_

1. Did God do what He promised?

   _Yes, God always does what He says. (Exodus 19:1,2)_

1. Why did God plan to give His commandments to Israel?

   _God planned to give them His commandments to show them that they were sinners and could not please Him or be accepted by Him through their own efforts. (Romans 3:20)_

1. What was Israel's attitude?

   _They had forgotten the many times when they had doubted God and sinned against Him. They were proud and self-confident. (Exodus 19:8)_

1. Why did God tell Moses to put a boundary around the mountain?

   _To emphasize that sinners cannot approach a holy God and that God hates sin. (Exodus 19:21,22)_

1. Why were the people so frightened?

   _Because the mountain was shaking violently, and there was fire, smoke, and lightning. (Exodus 20:18)_

1. What did all of this show the Israelites?

   _It showed them that God is almighty, pure, and holy, and that He hates and punishes sin. (Exodus 20:20)_

1. Who was God's messenger to Israel?

   _Moses. (Exodus 19:9)_

1. How do we hear God's words today?

   _📖 We hear God's message from the Bible. (2 Timothy 3:16)_

1. Has God changed His attitude toward sin today?

   _No. (Romans 1:18)_

1. What is God's punishment for disobeying even one of His commandments?

   _Everlasting separation from God and punishment in the Lake of Fire, which God prepared for Satan and his angels. (Matthew 25:41)_

1. Did God think that the Israelites would be able to obey His Ten Commandments?

   _No. God knew that no one would be able to obey them perfectly. (Psalm 44:21)_

1. Why is it impossible for any person to perfectly obey God's Law?

   _We were all born sinners and separated from God, so we are not capable of pleasing God. (Romans 5:12)_

1. Will God accept us if we just do our best and obey as many of His commandments as we can?

   _No. (Isaiah 64:6)_
