# 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 [Creation to the Cross - for Kids](../../README.md)

## 🧭 Lesson 6: God Created the Heavens and the Earth - Days 5 and 6

### 📖 Scripture – Genesis 1:20-25

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** Job 38–41; Psalm 18:30; 19:1–4; 24:1–2; 33:6–9; 93:5; 95:3–5; 99:3, 5, 9; 104; 104:24; 119:89; Isaiah 6:3; 40:28; 44:24; 45:7–12; 48:12–13; Jeremiah 10:12–13; 32:17; Colossians 1:16; Hebrews 1:10–12; 11:3; James 1:17; Revelation 4:8

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show that God created everything from nothing.
- To show God's character and attributes as revealed through His creative acts.

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To give consideration to the Biblical account of creation: God is the Creator.
- To have an increased awareness of God's sovereignty, His holiness, His power, and His wisdom.

### 🧭 Overview

This lesson is more than a lesson on biblical facts of day 5 & 6 of creation, when God created the birds, fish, and land animals. God's nature and attributes are clearly displayed in His creative acts. Your primary goal in teaching on creation is to point out the attributes of God through His creative acts.

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – Genesis 1:31 NIV

> God saw all that he had made, and it was very good.

---

### 💭 Perspective for the Teacher

God's Word is eternal and unchanging. Yet over the span of a few decades, society has accepted evolutionary theories as fact. What is not widely published is the fact that much of the data on which those theories were based is now being questioned.

Dr. John Whitcomb has often said that evolutionism is a religion which puts faith in time and chance. All the questions about life and its infinite complexity and order are answered by the evolutionist in terms of millions of years and chance.

Satan has blinded the minds of men, but God's Word is powerful to penetrate that blindness. What good news we can share: God, the sovereign Creator made all things by His power. His Word, the Bible, is the greatest of all texts and the basis for all science. We can teach with confidence – not in time and chance, but in the Living God and His unchanging Word that is true!

### 🗣️ On Teaching This Lesson

You are carefully laying a scriptural foundation on which the Gospel will later be presented. Each lesson builds on previous lessons. So be sure to follow each point carefully. DON'T COMPLICATE THE MESSAGE! Carefully follow the lesson, emphasizing the doctrinal 🧵Theme given.
