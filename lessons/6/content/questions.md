# 🧭 [Lesson 6: God Created the Heavens and the Earth - Days 5 and 6](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. What did God say about all the things which He created?

   _God said that they were good. (Genesis 1:4,10,12,18,21,25)_

1. Why was God able to create all the things perfect?

   _Because He is perfect. (Psalm 18:30)_

1. How was God able to create so many kinds of birds and sea creatures?

   _God is all-powerful and all-knowing. (Psalm 104:24–25)_

1. When you see designs and patterns in living things, what does that remind you of?

   _The fact that God is the Master Designer, the Creator of everything. (Romans 1:20)_

1. Why did God make many different birds and fish?

   _To show His love, wisdom, and power in creation. (1 Timothy 6:17)_
