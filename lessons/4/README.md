# 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 [Creation to the Cross - for Kids](../../README.md)

## 🧭 Lesson 4: God Created the Heavens and the Earth - Days 1 and 2

### 📖 Scripture – Genesis 1:1-8

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** Job 38–41; Psalm 19:1–4; 24:1–2; 33:6–9; 95:3–5; 104; 147:5; Isaiah 40:28; 44:24; 45:7–12; 48:12–13; Jeremiah 10:12–13; 32:17; 51:15; Colossians 1:16; Hebrews 1:10–12; 11:3

**Further Scriptures on God's holiness:** Psalm 18:30; 93:5; 99:3, 5, 9, Isaiah 6:3, James 1:17, Revelation 4:8

**Further Scriptures on Trinity at Creation:** Father – James 1:17-18, Son – Colossians 1:16, Holy Spirit – Genesis 1:2

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show that God created everything from nothing.
- To show God's character and attributes as revealed through His creative acts.

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To give consideration to the Biblical account of creation: God is the Creator.
- To have an increased awareness of God's sovereignty, His holiness, and His power.

### 🧭 Overview

This lesson is more than a lesson on biblical facts about the first two days of creation, when God created light and the sky. God's nature and attributes are clearly displayed in His creative acts. Your primary goal in teaching on creation is to point out the attributes of God through His creative acts.

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – Psalm 147:5 ESV

> Great is our Lord, and mighty in power.

---

### 💭 Perspective for the Teacher

We live in a society that has forgotten its Maker. Theories of evolution are taught as fact and anyone who considers the Biblical account to be true is disregarded as naive and uneducated altogether.

This lesson will present the true, biblical facts of creation. This is an opportunity to share the truth written by the Creator Himself. As you teach, share the wonder, awe, and praise that you have for our Creator.

### 🗣️ On Teaching This Lesson

You are carefully laying a scriptural foundation on which the Gospel will later be presented. Each lesson builds on previous lessons. So be sure to follow each point carefully. DON'T COMPLICATE THE MESSAGE! Carefully follow the lesson, emphasizing the doctrinal 🧵Theme given.
