# 🧭 [Lesson 4: God Created the Heavens and the Earth - Days 1 and 2](../README.md)

## 📖 READ – Genesis 1:4 ESV

> And God saw that the light was good. And God separated the light from the darkness.

🧵Theme - God is holy.

The light God made was very good.

- You will notice that each time God created, He said, “It is good.”
- 🔎 Compare:

  We are not able to make **anything** perfect.
  - Even if we work really hard and make something amazing, they still...
    - break
    - wear out
    - eventually are replaced with something new and better

Everything God made was **perfect** because:

- **God is perfect.**

In other words, God is **holy**.

Psalm 18:20 says _“As for God, his way is perfect...”_

Isaiah 6:3 says, _“...Holy, holy, holy, is the LORD...”_

## 📖 READ – Genesis 1:5 ESV

> God called the light Day, and the darkness he called Night. And there was evening and there was morning, the first day.

God divided the light from the darkness.

- He called the light “day”.
- He called the darkness “night”.
- This was the first day in the beginning of the world.
