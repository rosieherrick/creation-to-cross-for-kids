# 🧭 [Lesson 4: God Created the Heavens and the Earth - Days 1 and 2](../README.md)

## 🧩 Conclusion

🧵Theme - God is all-powerful.
🧵Theme - God is all-knowing.
🧵Theme - God is holy.

God merely spoke, and the expanse was created.

- 💬 Discuss:

  _Think about the sky – it is so big! We can only see a tiny portion of what encircles the earth. Yet God spoke and created all of the earth's atmosphere, and it was perfect._

Man's ideas of how the world came to be are changing all the time.

But God's true written history of creation **has not changed**.

- He was there before the beginning.
- He is the Almighty Creator, and He alone knows how all things came to be.
- God has told us throughout His Word that He created everything.

As we study the Bible, we see that God is greater than all, more important than all, and more powerful than all.

Nothing is too hard for Him.

- He is the source of all energy and the Creator of all matter.
- He made everything from nothing.

We have studied the first two days of creation.

We will explore the rest of the creation week in the coming lessons.

- As you look at the light and see the sky, think about what we have studied.
- Only God can create, and **He created everything** in the beginning.
- He is greater than we could ever imagine, But He has given us the Bible, His Word, so **we can know Him!**
