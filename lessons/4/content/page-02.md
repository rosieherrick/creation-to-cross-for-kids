# 🧭 [Lesson 4: God Created the Heavens and the Earth - Days 1 and 2](../README.md)

## 🧩 In the Beginning

🧵Theme - God is greater than all and more important than all. He is the highest in authority.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 1:1 ESV

> In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.

The word “genesis” means “beginnings”.

- All things had a beginning.
- Nothing/no one (except God) existed before the beginning.
- **What did God use** to make the heavens and the earth?

"Created" means to "make out of **nothing**".

- 💬 Discuss:

  _If someone wants to build a house, what do they need?_

  _If someone wanted to bake a cake, what do they need?_

  _If you want to draw a picture, what do you need?_

  _Can you think of **anything** that you can make without first having the materials to use?_

## 📖 READ – Hebrews 11:3 ESV

> By faith we understand that the universe was created **by the word of God**, so that what is seen was not made out of things that are visible.

- God made the heavens and the earth out of nothing!
- Only God can make something out of nothing.

🧵Theme - God is all-powerful.

How was it possible for God to make the heavens and the earth out of nothing?

- The Bible tells us that nothing is too hard for God.
