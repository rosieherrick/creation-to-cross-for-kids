# 🧭 [Lesson 4: God Created the Heavens and the Earth - Days 1 and 2](../README.md)

## 🧩 The First Day: Light Created

🧵Theme - God is all powerful.
🧵Theme - God is all-knowing.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 1:3 ESV

> And God said, “Let there be light,” and there was light.

Only God could create light by simply speaking!

- 💬 Discuss:

  _Wouldn't it be something if we could simply speak and have light appear? But it doesn't work that way. No, we are totally dependent on light that we created by God from the beginning of time._

  _What are some of the “lights” we see?_

  [Help the children think of many different kinds of lights such as flashlights, candles, light bulbs, sunshine, moonlight, starlight, etc.]

  _Whenever we see the light of the sun, the moon, or the stars, flip on a switch, or light a candle, let's remember that it was God who in the beginning created light. Only God would do that because only God is all-powerful and all-knowing. He created light out of nothing._

  _Scientist can study and describe some of the characteristics of light. All of us are affected by light. But only God **understands** light, for He **created** it._
