# 🧭 [Lesson 4: God Created the Heavens and the Earth - Days 1 and 2](../README.md)

## 🧩 Introduction

Have you ever wondered how everything we see around us got here?

Where did the earth come from?

What was it made out of?

What about all the stars?

What about plants and animals?

What about you and me?

The Bible tells us that **God created everything!**

We are going to study God's record of creation as He has given it to us in the book of Genesis.

Genesis is the first book in the Bible.

The Bible is a true record of history – it is God's record of what happened.

Only God was there in the very beginning.

- We've already learned about how God created the angels, which we cannot see.
- Now we will learn about how God created the universe - all the things we **can** see.
