# 🧭 [Lesson 4: God Created the Heavens and the Earth - Days 1 and 2](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. Who, in the beginning, created the heavens and the earth?

   _God. (Genesis 1:1)_

1. **What** did God use to make the heavens and the earth?

   _God didn't use anything. He made everything out of nothing. (Psalm 33:6,9)_

1. Why was God able to make the heavens and the earth?

   _God is all-powerful. Nothing is too hard for Him. (Jeremiah 32:17)_

1. **How** did God know how to make the heavens and the earth?

   _God knows everything and He is all-powerful. (Proverbs 3:19-20)_

1. Who taught God how to make everything?

   _No one taught God. (Jeremiah 10:12)_

1. Is there any person or any angel who knows everything like God does?

   _No. Only God knows everything. (Romans 11:33-34)_

1. What was the earth like before God began to prepare it for people to live in?

   _It was without form and in total darkness; no land and no life. (Genesis 1:2)_

1. What did God **do** in order to create everything?

   _He spoke and commanded that things appear. (Hebrews 11:3)_
