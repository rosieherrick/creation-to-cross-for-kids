# 🧭 [Lesson 29: Israel's Unbelief](../README.md)

## 🙏 God Healed Those Who Believed

🧵Theme - Man must have faith in order to please God and be saved.

## 📖 READ – Numbers 21:9 EASY

> Moses made a bronze snake and he put it on a pole. When a snake bit anyone and they looked at the bronze snake, they did not die.

- 🔎 Emphasize:

  _What did the Lord say they had to do in order to be healed from the snake bites? They just had to **look at** the bronze snake. Did the snake on the pole have power in it to heal them? No! The power of God healed every Israelite who showed they trusted God by looking at the snake on the pole._

The Lord healed them because they believed in Him.

- 🔎 Consider:

  _Do you think people would have been healed if they had prayed but did not actually look? No! What if they did not look at the bronze snake but offered God a gift instead? Would they have been healed? No! They had to look at the bronze snake just as the Lord said._

## 🧩 Conclusion

The events we have just read about in the Bible happened some 3,400 years ago.

God put these true stories in the Bible for us.

God wants us, like the Israelites, to believe and trust Him.
