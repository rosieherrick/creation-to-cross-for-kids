# 🧭 [Lesson 29: Israel's Unbelief](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. Why didn't the ten spies think that Israel would be able to enter Canaan?

   - _Because they saw the giants and the walled cities. (Numbers 13:28)_

   - _Because they did not believe God's promise that He would give them the land. (Numbers 13:31)_

1. Why did Joshua and Caleb think that Israel could enter Canaan?

   _Because they believed God's promise that He would give them the land. (Numbers 13:30)_

1. What are we calling God if we do not believe His Word and trust in Him?

   _We are calling God a liar. (Numbers 23:19)_

1. What was God's punishment on the Israelites because they did not believe His promise to give them the land?

   _God said all who did not believe Him would die in the wilderness. They would not enter Canaan. (Numbers 14:28-29)_

1. Who did God say would enter into the promised land?

   - _Caleb and Joshua, because they had believed God. (Numbers 14:30)_

   - _The children of the Israelites. (Numbers 14:31)_

1. What should the Israelites have done when they needed water?

   _They should have trusted in the Lord. (Psalm 106:13-14)_

1. Why did the Lord say that Moses and Aaron would not enter the promised land?

   - _Because they did not trust and obey Him and thereby honor Him as holy and perfect before the people. Instead, they showed their own anger. (Numbers 20:12)_

   - _Because Moses struck the rock instead of speaking to it as God had instructed him. (Numbers 20:11)_

1. What did the Lord do to punish the Israelites for their unbelief?

   _The Lord sent poisonous snakes which bit and killed many people. (Numbers 21:6)_

1. What did God tell Moses to do to save the Israelites from death?

   _The Lord told Moses to make a snake out of brass and put it on a pole. (Numbers 21:8)_

1. Did the snake of brass have magical power to heal those who looked?

   _No. (Numbers 21:8)_

1. Who healed those who looked?

   _The Lord did. (Deuteronomy 32:39)_

1. Did they deserve to be healed?

   _No, they were sinners and deserved to die. (Numbers 21:7)_

1. Why did the Lord heal all who looked?

   - _Because He is loving, merciful, and gracious. (John 3:14-15)_

   - _Because they believed His promise and looked at the bronze snake just as He commanded. (Numbers 21:8,9)_

1. Did anyone who looked at the snake on the pole die?

   _No, God always does what He promises. (Psalm 89:34)_
