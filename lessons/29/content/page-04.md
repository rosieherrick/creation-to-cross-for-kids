# 🧭 [Lesson 29: Israel's Unbelief](../README.md)

## 🧩 Israel Blamed Moses and Aaron for Lack of Water

As the people wandered, they again complained.

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

## 📖 READ – Numbers 20:1,2 ESV

> And the people of Israel, the whole congregation, came into the wilderness of Zin in the first month, and the people stayed in Kadesh. And Miriam died there and was buried there. Now there was no water for the congregation. And they assembled themselves together against Moses and against Aaron.

The Israelites blamed Moses and Aaron, his brother, because they did not have any water.

Why didn't the Israelites ask the Lord for water?

- The Lord had given them water from the rock when they needed it before.

- The reason they did not ask the Lord is that they did not trust in Him, even though He had done so much for them.

## 🧩 God Commanded Moses to Speak to the Rock

🧵Theme - God communicates with man.

🧵Theme - Man can come to God only according to God's will and plan.

## 📖 READ – Numbers 20:7,8 ESV

> And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, “Take the staff, and assemble the congregation, you and Aaron your brother, and **tell the rock** before their eyes to yield its water. So you shall bring water out of the rock for them and give drink to the congregation and their cattle.”

The Lord commanded Moses to speak to the rock.

- Before, when they had needed water, the Lord had told Moses to hit the rock.

- This time, the Lord had told him to just speak to the rock.

- Each time, God told them exactly what they should do.
