## 🧭 Lesson 29: Israel's Unbelief

### 📖 Scripture – Numbers 13:1-3,25-33; 14:1-4,26-32; 20:1,2,7-12; 21:4-9

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** Exodus 17:5,6; Numbers 13:26; Deuteronomy 1:19-25; 2 Kings 18:4; John 3:14, 6:40, 8:28, 12:32; Hebrews 2:14,15, 11:6; Revelation 6:15-17

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show the results of unbelief.
- To show God's mercy and grace.
- To prepare a foundation for presenting the story of Christ's death on the Cross for sinners.

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To see that all are sinners.
- To realize how important it is to listen to God and to believe Him.

### 🧭 Overview

This lesson presents God's judgment, grace, mercy, and faithfulness toward unfaithful Israel. These truths are revealed through the report of the men who spied out the Promised Land and the people's continued unbelief. The account of the bronze serpent is also presented as a type of Christ and a means of illustrating the Gospel.

The lesson emphasizes that humanity is sinful, dependent on God, and helpless to save itself. Therefore, each person must come to God by faith. As in every lesson, the focus remains on God, His character, and His actions.

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – Hebrews 11:6a

> And without faith it is impossible to please God...

---

### 💭 Perspective for the Teacher

In our society, we are constantly surrounded by discouraging news, and the problems we face can often seem insurmountable. As teachers, we are called to be like Caleb and Joshua, consistently declaring that our God is able. Our students need to see in our lives a positive testimony of faith. They need to witness that we trust God to work through our problems and meet our needs. They need to understand that, yes, there are “giants” in the land where we live too, but we trust God to lead us and grant us victory for His glory.

We do not want to give the message that everything will be easy or that we “deserve” good things from God. Instead, we want to live lives that bring glory to God and make our students thirsty to know this wonderful God who gives us hope and makes our lives refreshing in a sad, confusing world.

This can happen only as we allow God to control us through the power of His Spirit. Our aim is not to impress our students with our faith, but for our students to be impressed with the **great God** in whom we have faith!

### 🗣️ On Teaching This Lesson

You are carefully laying a scriptural foundation on which the Gospel will later be presented. Each lesson builds on previous lessons. So be sure to cover each point carefully. DON'T COMPLICATE THE MESSAGE! Follow the outline carefully and emphasize the doctrinal 🧵Theme given.
