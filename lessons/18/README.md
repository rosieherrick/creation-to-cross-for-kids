## 🧭 Lesson 18: God Destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah

### 📖 Scripture – Genesis 18:20-21; 19:1-7; 19:10-17; 19:24-26

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** Genesis 19:18-20, 30-38; 2 Peter 2:6-9; 2 Peter 3:9

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show that God judges sin.
- To show the results of wrong choices.
- To show God's concern for individuals.

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To realize that no one “gets away with” sin.
- To see the seriousness of sin and rebellion against God.
- To realize that God cares about them personally.
- To see that the Bible is true.

### 🧭 Overview

This lesson shows God's rescue of Lot from Sodom and Gomorrah before He destroyed those wicked cities. It emphasizes that God sees and knows every sin, that He is patient and merciful, and that He cares about people individually. At the same time, it reminds us that God will certainly judge sin.

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – Proverbs 3:5-6 NKJV

> Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and do not lean on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will direct your paths.

---

### 💭 Perspective for the Teacher

This passage of Scripture is particularly timely for us because of the kind of sin prevalent in these cities. Homosexuality (and every other kind of sexual sin) is flourishing in our day, but many excuse or even glorify this sin as a “chosen lifestyle.” Godless lifestyles have become socially acceptable. But God has not changed. God never has and never will accept immoral behavior.

Many people in our society like to think that a “loving God” would never punish anyone. But God is righteous; and though He is indeed loving, He is also to be feared. God does punish all sin.

One of the great lessons of this story is that we need to be mindful of the choices we make. Abraham chose to believe God, and God is still pouring out blessing on the descendants of Abraham, specifically those who, like Abraham, believe God. But Lot made a choice that unnecessarily placed him and his family in the middle of a perverted, godless environment. That choice cost him the lives of his wife and sons-in-law as well as his testimony. The story of Lot and his daughters in Genesis 19:30-38 (we do not teach this passage) shows that even though they escaped the cities, they clearly had been influenced by the sin that flourished there. The sons born to Lot's daughters became the fathers of the Moabites and the Ammonites who were to be continual enemies of Abraham's descendants.

Some of the children you teach may have already been influenced by immoral, ungodly lifestyles. They need to know that this is not God's plan for their lives. In Him is truth, love, life, and freedom from the bondage of sin.

### 🗣️ On Teaching This Lesson

You are carefully laying a scriptural foundation on which the Gospel will later be presented. Each lesson builds on previous lessons. So be sure to cover each point carefully. DON'T COMPLICATE THE MESSAGE! Carefully follow the outline. Emphasize the doctrinal 🧵Theme given.
