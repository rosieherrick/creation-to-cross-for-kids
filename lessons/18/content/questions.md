# 🧭 [Lesson 18: God Destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. Who knows the future of every person?

   _Only God. (Psalm 139:16)_

1. Did God know about all the wickedness of Sodom and Gomorrah?

   _👀 Yes. (Genesis 18:20-21)_

1. If people ignore God, will He overlook their sin and not punish them?

   _No, God will judge every person. (Hebrews 4:13)_

1. Why didn't God immediately punish the evil people of Sodom and Gomorrah, and why doesn't God immediately punish sinners now?

   _🤍 Because God is loving, merciful, gracious, and patient. He gives people time to change their minds and trust in Him. (2 Peter 3:9)_

1. Does God just threaten but never actually do it?

   _No, even though God is patient, He will eventually punish sinners. (Ezekiel 24:14)_

1. Can anyone stop God from punishing people when He decides it is time?

   _No, God is supreme. No one is greater than He is. (Psalm 86:8)_

1. Why did the Lord send His angels to rescue Lot, his wife, and his family?

   _🙏 Because Lot agreed with God that he was a sinner and trusted in God's promises to send a Savior. (2 Peter 2:7–9)_

1. Why did God turn Lot's wife into a pillar of salt?

   _Because she disobeyed the Lord's command that they must not look back at the burning cities. (Genesis 19:17,26)_
