# 🧭 [Lesson 18: God Destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah](../README.md)

## 🧩 The Sinfulness of the Sodomites

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 19:4-7 ESV

> But before they lay down, the men of the city, the men of Sodom, both young and old, all the people to the last man, surrounded the house. And they called to Lot, “Where are the men who came to you tonight? Bring them out to us....” Lot went out to the men at the entrance, shut the door after him, and said, “I beg you, my brothers, do not act so wickedly.”

![alt text](./Front-Door-Of-Lot.svg)

- 🔎 Consider:

  _All the people of the city came to Lot's house because they knew that Lot had some foreigners staying with him. They surrounded the house and told Lot to send the men out and they planned to do very bad things to these men. Here we see Lot begging them to change their minds._

  _Lot probably thought he was rescuing these visitors from the evil intents of the people of his city, but actually these were angels sent to rescue him._

## 🧩 Lot, His Wife, and His Daughters Rescued Out of Sodom

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 19:10-11 ESV

> But the men reached out their hands and brought Lot into the house with them and shut the door. And they struck with blindness the men who were at the entrance of the house, both small and great, so that they wore themselves out groping for the door.

Lot thought that he was going to protect these two visitors to his home but actually, these two men protected Lot and his family.
