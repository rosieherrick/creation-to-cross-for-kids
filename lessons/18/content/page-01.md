# 🧭 [Lesson 18: God Destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah](../README.md)

## 🧩 Introduction

Have you ever given a gift to someone you really care about? Maybe you gave them a present for their birthday or for Christmas. You wanted to make them happy, and you wanted to show them that you love them. Do you remember how you felt when you gave it to them? You probably felt happy and excited to see them open it.

God enjoys giving good gifts too.

In James 1:17, it says, _“Every good gift and every perfect gift"_ actually comes from our loving God. God gives us many good things we don't deserve!

What is the special word we use to describe this love? It's GRACE.

🎁 **Grace** is getting something we don't deserve

We learned that God gave grace to Abraham, even though he didn't deserve it.Today we will see another example of God giving grace to someone who didn't deserve it.

## 🧩 God's Attitude Toward Sodom and Gomorrah

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

🧵Theme - God is everywhere all the time; He knows everything.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 18:20-21 ESV

> Then the Lord said, “Because the outcry against Sodom and Gomorrah is great and their sin is very grave, I will go down to see whether they have done altogether according to the outcry that has come to me. And if not, I will know.”

You will remember that Lot had moved down near these two wicked cities.

Even though there were many people in the world at that time, God saw everything that the people of Sodom and Gomorrah did, and He heard everything that they said.

🧵Theme - God is loving, merciful, and gracious.

🧵Theme - God is greater than all and more important than all; He is the highest authority.

🧵Theme - God is holy and righteous. He demands death as the payment for sin.

God had been very displeased with these wicked cities for a long time, even before Lot moved near them.

- For a long while, the Lord had been patient with the people who lived there, but now He decided that He could not tolerate their sinfulness any longer.
- They could not escape the judgment of God.
