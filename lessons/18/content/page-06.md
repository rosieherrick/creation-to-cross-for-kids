# 🧭 [Lesson 18: God Destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah](../README.md)

## 🧩 Lot's Wife Looked Back

## 📖 READ – Genesis 19:26 ESV

> But Lot's wife, behind him, looked back, and she became a pillar of salt.

![alt text](./Lots-Wife-Looks-Back.svg)

When the angels took Lot, his wife, and his two daughters out of Sodom, they told them specifically not to look back but to run to the mountains.

But Lot's wife disobeyed.

God knew what was in the heart of Lot's wife.

- Lot's wife was like Cain.
- She did not trust in God.

God knew why she looked back.

- She looked back because she liked the sinful ways of the people of Sodom.
- She didn't want to leave.

She was very foolish to ignore God's warning because, when she looked back, God turned her into a pillar of salt.

🧵Theme - God never changes; He always does what He says; God is faithful.

God doesn't threaten to punish and then forget.

## 🧩 Conclusion

God is the same today as He was in the day of Abraham and Lot and Sodom and Gomorrah.

He is always the same.

He remembers and keeps His promises to bless those who trust in Him and to punish those who disobey Him.
