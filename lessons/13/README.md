## 🧭 Lesson 13: God Rejected Cain's Offering and Accepted Abel's

### 📖 Scripture – Genesis 4:3-16; 25

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** Genesis 4:17–24; Leviticus 17:11; Psalms 66:18; Proverbs 1:23; 15:3; Isaiah 59:2; 64:6; Ezekiel 18:4; Acts 17:26; John 3:16; Romans 3:23; 5:12; 6:23; Hebrews 9:22; 10:4; 11:4, 6, 16; James 2:19; Revelation 20:10, 15

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show that the sin of Adam has been passed on to all people.
- To show that man can come to God only His way.
- To show that man must have faith to please God and be saved.
- To show that God is keeping His promise to send a Savior.

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To realize that they are sinners.
- To see that they are helpless to save themselves.
- To see that they need to come to God His way, by faith.
- To see that the penalty for sin is death.

### 🧭 Overview

This lesson presents God's acceptance of Abel, who approached Him by faith in the way God wanted, and His rejection of Cain, who tried to come to God on his own terms.

God's holiness is shown in His requirement of the death penalty for sin, while His grace and mercy are seen in His provision of a way for sinners to draw near to Him and in His patient reasoning with angry Cain. God judged Cain for murdering his brother, and Cain's descendants are portrayed as worldly-minded. Yet God continued the line of the promised Savior by raising up Seth to replace Abel.

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – 1 Samuel 16:7 ESV

> For the Lord sees not as man sees: man looks on the outward appearance, but the Lord looks on the heart.

### 💭 Perspective for the Teacher

God told Cain what was right and was gracious to tell him why He did not accept him when Cain disobeyed. But Cain turned against all that God had told him to do and went out and killed his brother.

God hates sin. Sin is not God's fault. Man is given the choice to believe and obey God. Man is responsible for his choices. Cain had consequences for the choices he made and we will be held responsible for the choices we make in life too.

Teach these truths clearly. If you do not teach them, some children will never hear them. God will honor His Word in their hearts.

### 🗣️ On Teaching This Lesson

You are carefully laying a scriptural foundation on which the Gospel will later be presented. Each lesson builds on previous lessons. So be sure to cover each point carefully. DON'T COMPLICATE THE MESSAGE! Carefully follow the outline, emphasizing the doctrinal 🧵Theme given.
