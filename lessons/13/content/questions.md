# 🧭 [Lesson 13: God Rejected Cain's Offering and Accepted Abel's](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. What did Cain and Abel bring to God when they came to worship Him?

   _Cain brought some of the fruit from the ground, and Abel brought a lamb. (Genesis 4:3-5)_

1. Which offering did God accept?

   _Abel's offering. (Genesis 4:4)_

1. Why did God accept Abel's offering?

   _🙏 Abel believed God, trusting that God would keep His promise to someday send a Savior, and came to God His way with a blood sacrifice. (Hebrews 11:4)_

1. Why did God reject Cain's offering?

   _Cain did not believe God, he did not trust that God would send a Savior, and came to God his own way, disregarding what God's instructions. (Hebrews 11:4,6)_

1. Why did God talk to Cain about his choice?

   _🤍 Because God loved Cain and wanted him to change his mind about doing it his own way and bring the correct sacrifice. (2 Peter 3:9)_

1. Did Cain believe and agree with God?

   _No. (Genesis 4:8)_

1. What did Cain do after God rejected his offering?

   _He became angry and killed Abel. (Genesis 4:8)_

1. How did God know what Cain had done?

   _👀 God sees and knows everything. (Hebrews 4:13)_

1. Why did God give another son, Seth, to Adam and Eve?

   _🛟 God gave Seth as a replacement for Abel, so that the coming Savior could be born in his family line. (Genesis 4:25)_
