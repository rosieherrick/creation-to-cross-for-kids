# 🧭 [Lesson 13: God Rejected Cain's Offering and Accepted Abel's](../README.md)

## 🧩 Introduction

How serious is sin?

What if someone refuses to believe God and chooses to disobey Him?

- Is that okay?

Today we will answer these questions as we learn more about Adam and Eve's sons,
Cain and Abel.

## 🧩 God Told Them What to do When They Worship Him

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

There was no way that Adam and Eve or Cain and Abel could come to God.

- They were all sinners, and God hates sin.
- They were separated from God.
- The punishment for sin is death.
- They could not give **anything** to God to pay for their sins.

🧵Theme - God is loving, merciful, and gracious.

🧵Theme - Man can come to God only according to God's plan.

But God planned a way so they could come to Him.

- It was not man's way.
- It was God who decided on this way.
- No one else could make a way for them.

God made a way for them because He **loved** them.

🧵Theme - God communicates with man.

🧵Theme - God is holy and righteous. He demands death as the payment for sin.

God had told Adam and Eve what to do and they would have taught their sons.

God had told them:

When they came to Him, they had to bring a sheep as an offering to Him.

- They had to take an animal, kill it, and make it's blood flow out.

  The blood of the animal could not pay for their sin, so why then would God tell them to kill a sheep?

- Because God wanted them to **remember** that the punishment for sin is death.

- He wanted them to **remember** that they too would **die** and go to everlasting punishment unless God saved them.

- If they agreed with God that they were sinners and that only He could save them from everlasting punishment, then they were to bring a sheep and kill it the way God had told them to do.

- God promised that if they came by faith, offering to Him the blood of animals, He
  would accept them and forgive their sins.
