# 🧭 [Lesson 13: God Rejected Cain's Offering and Accepted Abel's](../README.md)

## 🧩 God Reasons with Angry Cain

🧵Theme - God communicates with man.

🧵Theme - God is loving, merciful, and gracious.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 4:6, 7 ESV

> The Lord said to Cain, “Why are you angry, and why has your face fallen? If you do well, will you not be accepted? And if you do not do well, sin is crouching at the door. Its desire is contrary to you, but you must rule over it.

Even though Cain refused to believe God and deliberately went his own way, God still came to talk to Cain about what was in his heart.

God was loving and merciful to talk to Cain and try to help him see it God's way.

God wanted Cain to know that he too would be accepted if he came God's way, (as his brother had done), offering the blood and trusting in God and His promise for a Savior.

## 🧩 Cain Refused to Listen to God and Killed Abel

🧵Theme - Man is a sinner. He needs God and is helpless to save himself.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 4:8 ESV

> Cain spoke to Abel his brother. And when they were in the field, Cain rose up against Abel his brother and killed him.

Did Cain change his mind and decide to do it God's way?

Cain was very angry.

He would not listen to God. He was listening to Satan, just as his mother and father had done in the garden.

Cain became angry because God did not accept him.

## 🧩 God's Judgment on Cain

## 📖 READ – Genesis 4:9 ESV

> Then the LORD said to Cain, “Where is Abel your brother?” He said, “I do not know; am I my brother's keeper?

Did God ask Cain about Abel because He didn't know? No.

God already knew what Cain did, He was giving Cain the chance to fess up on his own. (Just like God had done in the garden with Adam and Eve when they had sinned and were hiding.)

## 📖 READ – Genesis 4:10-12 ESV

> And the LORD said, “What have you done? The voice of your brother's blood is crying to me from the ground. And now you are cursed from the ground...when you work the ground, it shall no longer yield to you its strength. You shall be a fugitive and a wanderer on the earth.

God is Creator of all people, and He will punish those who sin.

The payment for sin is death – no other payment for sin is acceptable.

All sin will be punished by God, for He is the rightful owner of every person.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 4:25 ESV

> And Adam knew his wife again, and she bore a son and called his name Seth, for she said, “God has appointed for me another offspring instead of Abel, for Cain killed him.

God gave Adam and Eve another child in place of Abel who was killed by Cain.

In the garden God had promised He would send a Savior who would overcome Satan.

- This would have come through the family line of Abel, who trusted God.

- Satan tried to stop this from happening by guiding Cain to kill his brother.
