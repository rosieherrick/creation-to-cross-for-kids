# 🧭 [Lesson 13: God Rejected Cain's Offering and Accepted Abel's](../README.md)

## 🧩 Cain and Abel Offer Sacrifices to God

🧵Theme - Man can come to God only God's way.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 4:3-5 ESV

> "In the course of time **Cain brought to the Lord an offering of the fruit of the ground**, and **Abel also brought of the firstborn of his flock** and of their fat portions. And the Lord had regard for Abel and his offering, but for Cain and his offering he had no regard."

Both Cain and Abel knew that God existed, and both came to offer sacrifices to Him.

On the outside it looked like Cain and Abel both looked good but God knew the truth of what was in their hearts, what they were thinking.

## 🧩 God Accepts Abel's Offering

🧵Theme - Man must have faith in order to please God.

🧵Theme - Man can come to God only His way.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 4:4 ESV

> And Abel also brought of the firstborn of his flock and of their fat portions. And the LORD had regard for Abel and his offering.

Abel brought one of his sheep as an offering to God.

- It was the firstborn lamb of it's mother.
- Abel killed the sheep so it's blood ran out, and he offered the sheep as a sacrifice to God.

  When Abel did this, he was showing that he needed God and was trusting Him to save Abel from everlasting punishment.

- Abel was trusting in the promise God had made to his mom and dad back when they were still in the garden of Eden, the promise that God would some day send a Savior.

  🛟 God promised to send a Savior to save us from the power of sin, Satan, and death.

Was Abel's offering accepted by God? _Yes!_

## 🧩 God Rejects Cain's Offering

🧵Theme - Man must have faith in order to please God.

🧵Theme - Man can come to God only His way.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 4:5

> But for Cain and his offering he had no regard. So Cain was very angry, and his face fell.

As an offering to God, Cain brought the things which he had grown.

But God would not accept Cain or his offering, why?

- Cain didn't come to God the way He had told them, he was coming to God his own way.

- He had his own ideas on how he thought it should be done, so he disregarded what God had said and did it his way. That is why God rejected him.
