# 🧭 [Lesson 7: Man - God's Special Creation](../README.md)

## ❤️ Man's Emotions - The Ability to Feel

**God created man with emotions, so we can love Him.**

What are some feelings or emotions we might have?

- Joy
- Sadness
- Love
- Anger...

The Bible shows us that God has emotions too: He is very kind and loving, but He is also angry at what is evil.

God planned to love man, but God also wanted man to be able to love Him.

God gave man **emotions** so we could **love God**.
