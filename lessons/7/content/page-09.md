# 🧭 [Lesson 7: Man - God's Special Creation](../README.md)

## 🧩 Everything God Made Was Good

🧵Theme - God is holy.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 1:31 ESV

> And God saw everything that he had made, and behold, it was very good. And there was evening and there was morning, the sixth day.

Because God is perfect and good, everything He created was absolutely right and beautiful.

In the beginning, it was different than we see the world today…

- Nothing in the animal world would hurt or harm a man.
- Both man and animals didn't have to kill in order to eat.
- Thorns, thistles, and weeds didn't grow.

## 🧩 The Conclusion

God is greater than all; He is the highest in authority.

He is the great and only **Creator** of all things, which makes Him the **owner** of all things.

He made the first man from the dust of the earth.

God created man in His own image, to be like Him having:

- A mind so he can know God
- Emotions so he can love God
- A will so he can choose to obey God

Adam, the first man, is the father of all the people everywhere.

As Creator and owner of man, God gave man the special job of being manager over the earth.
