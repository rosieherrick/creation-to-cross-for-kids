# 🧭 [Lesson 7: Man - God's Special Creation](../README.md)

## 💪 Man's Will - The Ability to Choose

**God created man with a will, so we can choose to obey Him.**

Besides having a mind and feelings, God also has a will; He is able to decide that He will do something. He decided that man would be able to make decisions, too.

- 🔎 Consider:

  _When you get up in the morning, you get to decide what you are going to wear? Later, your mom probably decides what to feed you for breakfast._

  _Do your clothes get to say if they feel like being worn that day? Does your breakfast have any opportunity to say when it will be eaten?_

  _God could have made the first man so he would not have a choice, like your clothes or your food. That's how God made the sun, moon, and stars. God made them so they have to do the same thing every day, every month, and every year._

  _But God **didn't** plan to make man that way. God decides what He will do, and God wanted man to **be able** to make choices **just** as God makes choices._

God planned to make man so he could choose to love and obey God.

## 🧩 Summary: In The Image of God

| 🪞 God created man in the IMAGE OF GOD with a... |
| :----------------------------------------------: |
|       🧠 **Mind** so man can **Know** God.       |
|     ❤️ **Emotions** so man can **Love** God.     |
|  💪 **Will** so man can **Choose** to obey God.  |

- God planned for man to be made in God's image so he could do God's work on earth.
- God was going to give man a special job in His creation.
