# 🧭 [Lesson 7: Man - God's Special Creation](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. Who did God prepare the earth for?

   _For man. (Psalm 115:16)_

1. What great difference was there between the creation of man and the creation of the animals?

   _🪞 God created man in His image. (Genesis 1:26)_

1. What does it mean to be made in His image?

   _To be made in the image of God means to be made to be like Him._

1. What three things did God give man because man was made in His image?

   - _🧠 Mind so he can KNOW God._
   - _❤️ Emotions so he can LOVE God._
   - _💪 Will so he can CHOOSE to OBEY God. (Mark 12:30)_

1. Who is the father of all people?

   _Adam. (Acts 17:26)_

1. What special job did God give to Adam?

   _To be manager over all the earth. (Genesis 1:28-30)_

1. What did God say everything was like at the beginning?

   _Everything was very good. It was perfect! (Genesis 1:31)_
