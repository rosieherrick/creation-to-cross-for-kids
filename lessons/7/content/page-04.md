# 🧭 [Lesson 7: Man - God's Special Creation](../README.md)

## 🧠 Man's Mind - The Ability to Think

🧵Theme - God communicates with us

**God created man with a mind, giving us the ability to think.**

Because God has a mind, He planned to make people with a mind which had the ability to know God.

- God wanted to talk to us. He wanted us to talk to Him.
- He wanted to be able to communicate with man, not only by spoken word, but also by God's written word, the Bible. (Just as we are doing now.)
- God wanted to enable man to do God's work here on earth.

- 🔎 Note: _We realize that God has given animals minds too. Some animals are very smart, but no animal has the ability to think like we do. Animals don't have the ability to talk with us; they cannot share our thoughts and make the kind of decisions we make; they cannot communicate with us by speech and by writing._

God decided that He would give man a mind so he could think and reason like God does.

God gave man a **mind** so we should **know** God.
