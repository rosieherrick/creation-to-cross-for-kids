# 🧭 [Lesson 7: Man - God's Special Creation](../README.md)

## 🪞 God's Plan to Create Man in His Image

## 📖 READ – Genesis 1:26 ESV

> Then God said, "Let us make man in our image, after our likeness. And let them have dominion over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens and over the livestock and over all the earth and over every creeping thing that creeps on the earth.

To whom do you think God was speaking when He said _"...Let **us** make man in **our** image..."_?

- It was God the Father, God the Son, and the Holy Spirit who were talking together.
- They were discussing their plan to make man in the image of God.

## 🪞 Meaning of Being Made in the Image of God?

_“God created man to be ...like Him."_ (...in the image of God.)

What does it mean when God tells us that the first man was made in God's image?

- Have you ever heard an adult say that someone looks just like their parents? Similarly, God is saying that He's going to make man to be like Him.
- We know that God's not talking about our physical bodies.

  God is Spirit.

  God doesn't have a body of flesh and bones like us.

- Rather, God was referring to the part of man that can't be seen, the real us inside.

God planned the unseen part of us would have a **mind, emotions, and a will, created in the image of God**.
