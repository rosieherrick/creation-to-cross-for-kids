# 🧭 [Lesson 7: Man - God's Special Creation](../README.md)

## 🧩 The Creation of Adam

🧵Theme - God is all-powerful.

🧵Theme - God is loving greater than all and more important than all; He is the highest in authority.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 2:7 ESV

> Then the Lord God **formed** the man of **dust from the ground** and **breathed** into his nostrils the breath of life, and the man became a living creature.

When God made the first man and the first woman, He did it differently than anything He had already made because this was going to be God's special creation, made in His image.

God formed the first man out of the dirt. Then to give the body life, He breathed into his body and he became a living person.

God called the first man "Adam," which means "man."

Adam was the first and only man God created from the ground.
