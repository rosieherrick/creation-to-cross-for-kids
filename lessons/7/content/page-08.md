# 🧭 [Lesson 7: Man - God's Special Creation](../README.md)

## 🧩 Man Placed as Manager Over the Earth

## 📖 READ – Genesis 1:28-30 ESV

> "...Be fruitful and multiply and fill the earth and subdue it, and have dominion over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens and over every living thing that moves on the earth." And God said, "Behold, I have given you every plant yielding seed that is on the face of all the earth, and every tree with seed in its fruit. You shall have them for food. And to every beast of the earth and to every bird of the heavens and to everything that creeps on the earth, everything that has the breath of life, I have given every green plant for food." And it was so.

**God chose Adam to be a manager over the earth.**

God put man in charge of the earth and everything in it.

Man was to look after God's things here on earth and to be the leader over the animals, the birds, and the fish.

What a special job God gave to man!

God had a right to decide who he would give this job to.

- He didn't give it to the angels.
- He didn't give it to Satan.

Why was God the only one who had the authority to give man control over the earth?

God created it.
