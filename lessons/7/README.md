# 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 [Creation to the Cross - for Kids](../../README.md)

## 🧭 Lesson 7: Man - God's Special Creation

### 📖 Scripture – Genesis 1:26-31; 2:7

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** Deuteronomy 30:20; Job 12:10; Job 38–41; Psalm 86:11; 95:6; 97:11–12; 100:1–3; 119:73; 139; 144:3; Proverbs 2:1–6; Isaiah 45:5–12, 21–22; 46:9; Jeremiah 33:3; John 4:24; 17:3; Acts 17:24–28; Philippians 3:10; 1 John 3:1–2; Revelation 4:11

**Further Scriptures on Emotions:**

- Love: Jeremiah 31:3, John 3:16, 1 John 4:7-10
- Hate: Proverbs 6:16-19, Malachi 2:16
- Sadness: Matthew 23:37, Luke 19:41
- Joy/Delight: Jeremiah 9:24, Zephaniah 3:17, Psalm 97:11-12

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show God's sovereignty
- To show the uniqueness of man in all of God's creation
- To show God's original plan for man to be manager over all the earth

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To see that we are God's most special creation, made in His image
- To see God's ownership of man

### 🧭 Overview

This lesson presents God as the sovereign Creator and owner of man and presents man as God's special creation, made in the image of God. The lesson emphasizes that God created the earth and everything in it specifically to prepare for His most amazing creation, namely man. Man alone was made in God's image with a mind to know God, emotions to love God, and a will to choose to obey God. Adam, the first and only man created from the ground, the ancestor of all people.

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – Deuteronomy 6:5 NIV

> Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your strength.

---

### 💭 Perspective for the Teacher

God's Word is very clear in showing forth the uniqueness of man in God's creation. The Bible does not leave an option for man to be any less than the **only being created in the image of God**. This unique creation includes man's unique relationship to God. Man is not only God's creation, made in God's image; man is also God's **cherished** possession, **accountable** to his Creator.

Believing and understanding this relationship between God, the Creator, and man, the one created in God's image, is essential to understanding all other biblical truth. If this part of the foundation is lacking, nothing else will be stable. The person who is blind to the fact that he was created is also blind to the fact that he is accountable to his Creator. If a person grows up thinking that he evolved, why should he worry about what God has to say?

The subtlety of the lie of evolution is that it deceives man about the existence and character of God and makes man think that he has no need of God. Interestingly, this kind of thinking produces tremendous insecurities because man was never designed to be his own god. We were created to be in submission to One who is almighty, sovereign, all-knowing, all-powerful, eternal; One who can supply **all** our needs. Little wonder that without faith in our almighty Creator, men live lives of utter frustration, for man **cannot** find in himself the supply for all that he needs. The only One who can truly meet our needs is God.
