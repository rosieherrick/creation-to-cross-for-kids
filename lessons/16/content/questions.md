# 🧭 [Lesson 16: God Called Abram and Lot Chose Sodom and Gomorrah](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. Did God abandon His plan to send a Savior for mankind because they rebelled against God and built the tower of Babel?

   _No. God always does what He says; He never changes. (Numbers 23:19)_

1. What did God tell Abram to do?

   _God told him to leave his own country and go to the place God promised to lead him to. (Genesis 12:1)_

1. How many children did Abram and Sarai have when God told Abram to go into a different country?

   _They didn't have any children. (Genesis 11:30)_

1. What things did God promise Abram?

   - _⛰️ Land - God told Abram that He would give him the land of Canaan. (Genesis 12:1)_

   - _👥 Huge Family - God said that Abram's family would become so great they would be a nation. (Genesis 12:2)_

   - _☂️ Protection - God said he would protect and prosper Abram so that he would be an important man and through him others would benefit. (Genesis 12:3)_

   - _🛟 Savior - God also said that all the world would be helped by the Savior God would send in Abram's family line. (Genesis 12:3)_

1. God spoke directly to Abram. How does God talk to us today?

   _📖 Through His Word, the Bible. (2 Timothy 3:16–17)_

1. Did God choose Abram because he wasn't a sinner?

   _No, all people are sinners. (Romans 3:23)_

1. What did Abram do when God made him these promises?

   _He believed God. He left his home country and went where God showed him to go. (Genesis 12:4)_

1. Why did Lot move away from Abram and live near Sodom and Gomorrah?

   - _Abram and Lot had so many animals that there wasn't enough grass and space for them both to live together. (Genesis 13:5-7)_

   - _Lot moved to the plain because it was well-watered and had much more grass for his animals. (Genesis 13:10-11)_
