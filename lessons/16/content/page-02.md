# 🧭 [Lesson 16: God Called Abram and Lot Chose Sodom and Gomorrah](../README.md)

## 🧩 God's Great Promises to Abram

### ⛰️ God Promises Abram Land

🧵Theme - God always does what He says; He never changes; He is faithful.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 12:1 ESV

> Now the Lord said to Abram, “Go from your country and your kindred and your father's house **to the land that I will show you**.”

- God called Abram to leave his home but didn't tell him where to go, just to get moving.
- God spoke directly to Abram; the Bible was not yet written in Abram's day.

  God speaks to us now through His written Word, the Bible.

  The only way we can know about God and what He wants to tell us is through the words of this book, the Bible.

### 👥 God Promises Abram a Huge Family

## 📖 READ – Genesis 12:2 ESV

> And **I will make of you a great nation**, and I will bless you and make your name great, so that you will be a blessing.

- Even though Abram and Sarai did not have any children, God promised Abram that he would become the father of a great nation.
- God also promised that He would protect him and would help him do well so that he would become an important man, and through him, others would be helped.

When God makes a promise, you can be sure He will keep His Word.
He promised Abram that he would have a big family, such a great big family that they would be a whole nation of their own.

He also promised Abram that He would bless him and his family, so much so that they would be able to then be a blessing to the world.

### ☂️ God Promises Abram Protection

## 📖 READ – Genesis 12:3 ESV

> I will bless those who bless you, and him who dishonors you I will curse, and in you all the families of the earth shall be blessed.

- God promised that He would help those who helped Abram, but He would bring evil on anyone who treated Abram wrongly.

### 🛟 God's Promised Savior is a Future Family Member

Let's take a closer look at the end of verse 3: _“...in you all the families of the earth shall be blessed.”_

- This is the greatest of all the promised given to Abram, because it's about the Savior.

- Do you remember back at the garden of Eden, God promised He would send a Savior into the world to crush the power of Satan?

- God now promised that one of Abram's family would be that Savior!

- All families of the earth would be blessed through Abram's future family member, the Savior.

That promise includes you and me and all of the people in the world!
