# 🧭 [Lesson 16: God Called Abram and Lot Chose Sodom and Gomorrah](../README.md)

## 🧩 Abram Believed and Obeyed God

🧵Theme - Man must have faith in order to please God and be saved.

Where Abram lived, everyone worshiped idols, and didn't worship their Creator God. God's promises seemed impossible since Abram and Sarai still didn't have any kids, or even one child of their own.

But in spite of these things, Abram believed God's promises.

- Abram believed that God would send a Savior and that the Savior would be in his family line.
- Abram, like Noah, believed God's Word, and for that reason, he obeyed God.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 12:4-5 ESV

> So **Abram went, as the Lord had told him**, and Lot went with him. Abram was seventy-five years old when he departed from Haran. And Abram took Sarai his wife, and Lot his brother's son, and all their possessions that they had gathered, and the people that they had acquired in Haran, and they set out to go to the land of Canaan...

- 🔎 Consider:

  _Abram was very rich and had many servants, cattle, sheep, and goats. He took all that he owned with him._

  _Can you imagine taking off across the land with all our family and possessions and heading for a strange country? Abram's neighbors may have even laughed at him. Remember, Abram was a man of wealth and importance. People were watching him._

  _Now Abram was leaving the city and going out to travel and live in tents._

  _But Abram believed God and obeyed Him._

Lot, Abram's nephew, also went with Abram and Sarai.

Lot also believed God and trusted His promises.

God faithfully guided Abram to Canaan, the land that He had promised to give him.
