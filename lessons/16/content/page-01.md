## 🧩 Introduction

How many of you know your grandfather? How many know your great-grandfather? What about your great-great-grandfather?

Not many of us know the names of our family members who lived before us and already died, but today we're going to talk about a man who lived about 4,000 years ago and is still remembered today by his family.

Why is he remembered?

- He is remembered because of the special promises God made to him.
- He is remembered because he believed God.

![City of Ur](City-Of-Ur.svg)

## 🧩 God Called and Commanded Abram

The Bible tells us that in a big city of “Ur” there lived a man named Abram.

Abram lived there near his father, Terah, and brother.

Abram's brother had gotten married and his brother and wife had several children.

Abram managed to marry a beautiful wife named Sarai, and they too wanted to have kids of their own.

The only problem was that even though they wanted to have children, many years went by and they still had no kids. They didn't even have **one** baby of their own.

- 🔎 Note:

  _In those days, couples who had no children were looked down upon by society. People mostly wanted a baby boy so that their family money and things would then be passed down to the son when you die. If you didn't have a son, then your things could be given away when you die. It was very important to have a son._
