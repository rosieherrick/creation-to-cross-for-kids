# 🧭 [Lesson 5: God Created the Heavens and the Earth - Days 3 and 4](../README.md)

## 🧩 God's Love Displayed

🧵Theme - God is love.

- Why did God make flowers, trees, and plants?
  - Did God need them? No! He doesn't need anything.
  - God made the plants and trees for man, whom He was going to create next.

- God made the plants to meet our needs:
  - To provide **food** for us to eat
  - To give **oxygen** for the air we breathe
  - To provide **wood** for building

|        Learning About God         |
| :-------------------------------: |
|    📖 God wants us to know Him    |
| 🪐 God is everywhere all the time |
|      💡 God knows everything      |
|    👑 God is greater than all     |
|          💯 God is holy           |
|      ⚡ God is all-powerful.      |
|       ❤️ **God is loving**        |

- God made the plants to show us His love for us.

  He didn't have to make such a variety of colors, sizes, shapes, flavors, and fragrances. A few kinds would have met our needs.

- 🔎 Consider:

  _God could have made everything black and white. But instead, He created colors, beautiful colors!_

  _Everything could have been tasteless, or worse yet, it could have all tasted like sour milk! Yuck!_

  _But God didn't do that! He created wonderful flavors. (What do you like: chocolate, vanilla, strawberry…?)_

  _He did the same thing with fragrances. He could have made everything smell the same. He could have made everything smell like a stinky trash can. God didn't do that. He made wonderful smells and fragrances we enjoy… lilac bushes blooming in the spring, a fresh mowed grass or the smell outside after it stops raining, or the amazing smell of freshly baked cookies!_

- He made the earth not just livable, but truly **beautiful**.
- He wanted to remind us every day of His **great love for us!**

  _Each and every seed has the ability inside it to make a plant just like the one it came from. The seed might look tiny and dry, but it has all the information packed inside to produce beautiful flowers and more seeds that will produce more plants._

  _If you were to look at a flower under a magnifying glass, you would see other patterns special to that kind of flower._

  _All these things show that there is a **Master Designer.**_

- Everything that God created on this day was good.

  All the plants were good, lovely, and created perfect.
  - Thorns and weeds did not exist.
  - No fruit was poisonous.
  - Vegetables and fruit did not get diseases or spoil.

Everything was perfect in the beginning, because God is perfect, and everything He does is perfect.
