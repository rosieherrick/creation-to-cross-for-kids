# 🧭 [Lesson 5: God Created the Heavens and the Earth - Days 3 and 4](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. Why was God able to command the ocean to go back and stay where He wanted it?

   _Because He is all-powerful and He created the ocean. (Psalm 95:5)_

1. Why did God create everything beautiful and why did He make all different kinds of fruit and vegetables to eat?

   _He made them because He is loving and kind. He prepared everything for us. (Psalm 104:14–15)_

1. What did God say about all the things which He created?

   _God said that they were good. (Genesis 1:4,10,12,18)_

1. How was God able to create such a big universe, with so many stars, so far apart?

   _God is all-powerful and He is everywhere, all the time. (Jeremiah 10:12)_

1. When you see designs and patterns and repeated shapes in flowers and trees and all living things, what does that remind you of?

   _The fact that God is the Master Designer, the Creator of everything. (Romans 1:20)_
