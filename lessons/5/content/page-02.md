# 🧭 [Lesson 5: God Created the Heavens and the Earth - Days 3 and 4](../README.md)

## 🧩 Third Day: Dry Land and Plants Created

## 📖 READ – Genesis 1:9-13 ESV

> And God said, ‘Let the waters under the heavens be gathered together into one place, and let the dry land appear.' And it was so. God called the dry land Earth, and the waters that were gathered together he called Seas. And God saw that it was good.

God gathered the waters into seas and made the dry ground appear.

- Only God has the power to do this.
- No person can move the oceans or make dry land appear.

- 🔎 Consider:

  _Have you ever tried to carry a big bucket of water? Water is heavy, isn't it! Have you ever seen the damage done by powerful waves in a storm or by a flood? No man can control the rivers and oceans._

Nothing is too hard for God. Just imagine: God gathered all the waters that covered the whole earth. And He formed the seas and the dry land just as He decided they should be.

## 📖 READ – Psalm 95:5 ESV

> The sea is his, for he made it, and his hands formed the dry land.

- Only God, who made the waters of the earth, can control them.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 1:11-13 ESV

> And God said, ‘Let the earth sprout vegetation, plants yielding seed, and fruit trees bearing fruit in which is their seed, each according to its kind, on the earth.' And it was so. The earth brought forth vegetation, plants yielding seed according to their own kinds, and trees bearing fruit in which is their seed, each according to its kind. And God saw that it was good. And there was evening and there was morning, the third day.

🧵Theme - God is all-powerful.
🧵Theme - God is all-knowing.

God made all of the plants and trees.

- Only God has the knowledge and power to create plant life.
- God made the plants to make seeds which would grow into more plants just like the originals.
- 🔎 Consider:

  _An apple tree doesn't make sunflower seeds! Each plant makes seeds of its own kind._

- 💬 Discuss:

  _What if someone made a nice shirt for you. In time it would wear out, wouldn't it? If you wanted another one just like it, it would have to be made from another piece of cloth and sewn together. But when God created plants, He put within each plant the ability to make seeds which could sprout into new plants like the first. So even though a plant dies, it produces seeds that grow into plants like the original; then, those plants produce seeds that grow into more plants like the first. God gives life, and He's the One who keeps it going!_

- The plants we have today came from those God created in the beginning.
