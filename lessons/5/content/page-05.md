# 🧭 [Lesson 5: God Created the Heavens and the Earth - Days 3 and 4](../README.md)

## 🧩 Conclusion

Our God is an awesome God!

- He gathered the water to form the oceans and the sky.
- He designed with great detail each of the different kinds of plants and programmed into them the ability to reproduce more plants just like them.
- He then spoke into existence all the millions and billions of stars we both can and can't see.
- He even specifically designed one star, just the right size, for our solar system… the sun.

🔎 Consider:

_Remember how we earlier imagined that your mom made you the best cookies you've ever tasted in your life? Well, if you enjoyed that cookie, what do you think you should do afterwards? Don't you think it would be good to thank your mom for the cookie?_

_As we look around and see the beautiful world God has made, let's thank God for what He made. He made this world for us to enjoy! We can see how much He loves us every day!_
