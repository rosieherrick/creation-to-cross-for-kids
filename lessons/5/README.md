## 🧭 Lesson 5: God Created the Heavens and the Earth - Days 3 and 4

### 📖 Scripture – Genesis 1:9-19

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** Genesis 1:31; Job 38–41; Psalm 18:30; 19:1–4; 24:1–2; 33:6–9; 93:5; 95:3–5; 99:3, 5, 9; 104; 119:89; Proverbs 3:19; Isaiah 6:3; 40:26, 28; 44:24; 45:7–12, 18; 48:12–13; Jeremiah 10:12–13; 32:17; Romans 1:20; Colossians 1:16; 1 Timothy 6:17b; Hebrews 1:10–12; 11:3; James 1:17; Revelation 4:8

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show that God created everything from nothing.
- To show God's character and attributes as revealed through His creative acts.

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To give consideration to the Biblical account of creation: God is the Creator.
- To have an increased awareness of God's sovereignty, His holiness, and His power.

### 🧭 Overview

This lesson is more than a lesson on biblical facts of day 3 & 4 of creation, when God created the dry land, plants, sun, moon, and stars. God's nature and attributes are clearly displayed in His creative acts. Your primary goal in teaching on creation is to point out the attributes of God through His creative acts.

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – Genesis 1:1 ESV

> In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.
