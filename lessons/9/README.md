## 🧭 Lesson 9: God Made Eve

### 📖 Scripture – Genesis 2:18-25

**Additional Scriptures for Study:** Jeremiah 32:27; Matthew 6:8b; Acts 17:24–25; Philippians 4:19

### 🎯 Lesson Goals

- To show that God in His sovereignty, love, holiness, wisdom, and power provided a wife for Adam.
- To show that marriage is part of God's original design for mankind.

### 💡 This Lesson Should Help the Students

- To see God's wisdom and love in providing exactly what we need.
- To see that God values both man and woman very highly.
- To see that marriage was ordained by God.

### 🧭 Overview

This lesson shows God's sovereign creation of woman as a gift to man. It emphasizes that God created Eve in the special way He chose, that Eve was made to be Adam's helper and companion, and that marriage is part of God's design for His people.

### ✨🔑 Key Verse – Psalm 118:1 ESV

> Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His steadfast love endures forever!

---

### 💭 Perspective for the Teacher

In our culture, the sanctity of marriage is often obscured by the desire to control one's own life and to submit to no one. This lesson presents God's plan for marriage and shows that marriage was ordained by God.

When we recognize that marriage comes from God, we won't view it as outdated, because God does not change. Nor should we treat it as something to “try out” to see if it works. God created man and woman in His image and entrusted them with sacred responsibilities toward Him and toward each other. When children understand this original design, they see themselves as accountable to their Creator and cherished by Him.

Many children today bear the hurt of broken families. They need to know that this was never God's intention for them. We cannot alter their family circumstances, but we can teach them the truth from God's Word and trust Him to move in their hearts.

### 🗣️ On Teaching This Lesson

You are carefully laying a scriptural foundation on which the Gospel will later be presented. Each lesson builds on previous lessons. So be sure to follow each point carefully. DON'T COMPLICATE THE MESSAGE! Carefully follow the lesson, emphasizing the doctrinal 🧵Theme given.
