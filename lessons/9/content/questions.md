# 🧭 [Lesson 9: God Made Eve](../README.md)

## ❓ Questions

1. Who decided that Adam needed a wife?

   _God decided. (Genesis 2:18)_

1. Why did God decide to make a wife for Adam?

   _Because God loved Adam and did not want him to be alone. (Genesis 2:18)_

1. Was it right for God to decide to do this without asking Adam?

   _Yes, God created Adam and he belongs to God. (Genesis 2:7)_

1. How did God make the first woman?

   _God put Adam to sleep. He took out one of Adam's ribs and then made the first woman from it. (Genesis 2:21-22)_

1. **How** was it possible for God to make Eve from Adam's rib?

   _God is all-powerful and can do anything He wants to. (Jeremiah 32:17)_

1. Who told Adam and Eve to marry and to have children?

   _God, their Creator. (Genesis 1:28)_
