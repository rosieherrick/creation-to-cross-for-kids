# 🧭 [Lesson 9: God Made Eve](../README.md)

## 🧩 God Created Eve from Adam's Rib

🧵Theme - God is all-powerful.

## 📖 READ – Genesis 2:21-22 ESV

> So the LORD God caused a deep sleep to fall upon the man, and while he slept **took one of his ribs** and closed up its place with flesh. And **the rib that the LORD God had taken from the man he made into a woman** and brought her to the man.

🧵Theme - God is all-powerful.

When God decided to make a wife for Adam, He did it in a special way that only God can do.

God made the first woman as a gift for man.

- 🔎 Compare:

  _When your mom or dad gives you a very special gift, they expect that you will take very special care of it too. In the same way, God had given Adam a wife, and He expected Adam to take good care of her and to love her._

  _Notice that Eve was made from Adam's rib. God actually made her from a part of his own body. How precious and close she must have been to Adam! God had also given her a mind, emotions, and a will, so she also was able to talk with God and with Adam._

## 🧩 Marriage Ordained by God

## 📖 READ – Genesis 2:23-24 ESV

> Then the man said, “This at last is bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh; she shall be called Woman, because she was taken out of Man.” Therefore a man shall leave his father and his mother and hold fast to his wife, and they shall become one flesh.

🧵Theme - God is holy and righteous.

God made a woman for man so they could be married, live together, and have children.
