# 🧭 [Lesson 9: God Made Eve](../README.md)

## 🧩 Conclusion

Life was perfect for Adam and Eve.

- God had given them everything they needed.
- Everything around them was beautiful.
- They were able to talk to God and each other.
- He had made them rulers over His creation.
- He was right there with them to guide them in every decision.
- Their work wasn't hard.
- They were perfectly healthy.

Adam and Eve had so much to be thankful for.

- God was loving and good to Adam and Eve, giving them everything they needed.
- 🔎 Consider: _What has God given us that we can be thankful for?_
